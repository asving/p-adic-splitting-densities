## A. The mathematical intuitions (why the proof works)

   at each residue root, rescale. Every counting law in the corpus is this

   with re-based parameters. W-11's per-center law, HEX3's α-scaling,
   HMENU3's block dictionary, GENH4's stage fibration are all instances.

   history decomposes into α-events (self-similar: same degree, deeper
   window; closed-form bracket (Q−1)Q^{(m(m−1)/2+1)μ−1}) and β-events
   (fracture into a PARTITION-indexed product of strictly-lower-degree
   systems, consuming the lower degrees' already-proved menus). This is
   why the degree-n theory assembles from degrees < n and why P(5), P(6),
   P(7) fired with zero new degree-specific work. Five event types total:
   DRAIN / order-1-decided / α / β / composite-opening (GENIND-1).

   stage's COUNT law is W-12.A's tame cell law with re-based inputs

   dimension E appears as LADDER DENSITY / alphabet squaring, never as a

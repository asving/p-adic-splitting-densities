## 1. Route adjudication

**Do not start a degree-by-degree campaign.  Select the pure-engine architecture, but mark the
uniform theorem BLOCKED at `A0G-GC`, the finite rational genre-cover theorem.**  The completed
dv-graded Hensel engine makes the local factorization step degree-generic.  It does not prove
that all inputs fall into finitely many *symbolic, countable* coefficient genres, nor that the
infinite refinement parameters sum to rational functions of `q`.

The currently available cite-backed route does not repair that gap:

* FGMN, *Residual ideals of MacLane valuations*, Theorem 6.6, is an excellent source for the
  slope/residual-factor dissection already isolated by `C33Cite.exists_dvDissection`.  It is a
  local factorization theorem at a supplied inductive valuation and proper key polynomial; it
  is not a finite coefficient-cylinder classification or a rational-measure theorem.
* Alberich-Carramiñana–Guàrdia–Nart–Poteaux–Roé–Weimann, *Polynomial factorization over
  henselian fields*, Theorem 6.12, proves correctness/termination of its rank-one
  factorization algorithm under the stated factorization-section hypotheses.  That section
  assumes a monic square-free input and `char(k)=0` or `char(k)>deg(g)`.  The present theorem
  ranges over complete DVRs with finite residue field, including wild primes dividing `n`, so
  that result cannot be imported as an all-characteristic backstop.

Even an all-characteristic, pointwise OM-termination theorem would still be weaker than the
needed statement.  `DecidedSliceAt` asks for one rational function valid for every complete DVR
with residue cardinality `q`.  The proof needs a uniform finite schema of cells, exact cell
counts, and a proof that all unbounded depth parameters are geometric/Presburger summations.
Pointwise termination alone supplies none of those three assertions.

Accordingly:

| route | decision | what it supplies | faithfulness cost / missing clause |
|---|---|---|---|
| pure engine | **selected design; presently blocked** | factor splitting, exact degrees, residual transport, type algebra | must prove `A0G-GC` and the rational cell-count grammar internally |
| cite-backed OM completeness | **not sufficient as sole route** | may justify local dissection or future termination | current broad termination source excludes wild residue characteristic; any pointwise theorem still lacks uniform rational counting |
| direct density axiom/cite | **rejected** | would close the field syntactically | no source-faithful theorem with the exact quantifiers; it would hide the main mathematical content |

The permitted future hybrid is narrow: cite an exact all-characteristic factorization or
termination clause, bind every hypothesis to the repository's complete-DVR setting, and prove
the finite genre enumeration, measures, disjointness, and rational summation in Lean.  No cite
may directly assert the desired density formula.

### 3.2 Degree-generic proof patterns not yet exported generically

The cubic fixed-point argument has a uniform mathematical form:

1. partition the first residue layer;
2. decide simple residual factors immediately;
3. recurse on each repeated residual cluster;
4. split coprime residual labels by the Hensel engine;
5. isolate the all-roots-together rescaling loop;
6. solve a scalar fixed point and assemble the type convolution.

For a size-`m` rational cluster, scaling
`x = πy` divides the coefficients by `π^m, π^(m-1), ..., π`; its conditional Jacobian is

```text
q^(-κ_m),              κ_m = m(m-1)/2.
```

In the full monic degree-`m` box, choosing the common residue centre contributes `q^(1-m)`,
so the corresponding full-box loop has weight

```text
q^(1-T_m),             T_m = m(m+1)/2.
```

At `m=3` this is `q^-5`, exactly IFC7's step from `M` to `M+3`.  The future solver must
parameterize `m`, `κ_m`, the shift, and the base sequence; IFC7's
`density_ge_of_step` (`IFC7.lean:2031`) hard-codes `3` and `5` and is only the model.
Since `q≥2` and `κ_m>0` for `m≥2`, `1-q^-κ_m` is positive and every fixed-point denominator is
nonzero.  `m=0,1` are base cases, not uses of that division.

The mass/drainage move is also a reusable *pattern*, not a landed all-degree theorem.  Cubic
`totalMass_three` and `drainageAt_three` are at `H97b.lean:226,236`; the conditional general
reduction `H98.drainage_of_package` (`H98.lean:148`) still assumes
`∀ n, InductionPackage n`.  Therefore the uniform A0 proof should establish exact disjoint
recurrences for `decidedDensity` directly.  Reusing IFC7's five lower bounds plus a total-mass
squeeze at every degree would create an unnecessary dependency on the still-open uniform
drainage campaign.

## 4. The uniform genre classification to prove

### 4.1 First layer: residue-divisor patterns

For fixed `n`, define a residue pattern to be a finite multiset of pairs `(δ,m)` satisfying

```text
δ ≥ 1,  m ≥ 1,  and  Σ δ*m = n.
```

It describes closed residue points of degree `δ` occurring with multiplicity `m`.  There are
finitely many such patterns.  The number of choices is a polynomial in `q`, obtained from the
standard irreducible-polynomial census

```text
I_δ(q) = (1/δ) * Σ_{d|δ} μ(d) q^(δ/d),
```

with falling factorials and symmetry factors for repeated multiplicity labels.  A block with
`m=1` is already the unramified factor `(e,f)=(1,δ)`.  A block with `m>1` invokes the size-`m`
cluster law over residue cardinality `q^δ`; its output residue degrees are multiplied by `δ`.
Distinct closed points combine by multiset convolution, justified algebraically by
`typeOf_of_residual_coprime`.

All patterns except `(δ,m)=(1,n)` call strictly smaller cluster sizes.  That remaining pattern
is the single rational-cluster fixed point.

### 4.2 Inside one cluster: finitely many parametric NP/OM schemas

A genre is not one literal Newton polygon.  Slopes can have arbitrarily large numerators as
roots become arbitrarily close.  The needed finiteness statement is instead:

```lean
structure ClusterGenre (m : ℕ) where
  -- finite combinatorial data: face lengths, reduced slope denominators,
  -- residual degree/multiplicity patterns and recursive child positions
  schema : ...
  params : Fin r → ℕ
  constraints : PresburgerRegion r
  weight : RatFunc                         -- after summing params
  children : List (Σ k : Fin m, ClusterCall k)
  children_lt : ∀ c ∈ children, c.1 < m
  sound : ...

def FiniteGenreCoverAt (m : ℕ) : Prop :=
  ∃ G : Finset (ClusterGenre m),
    PairwiseDisjoint G ∧ ExhaustsClusterBox G ∧
    (∀ g ∈ G, ExactRationalWeight g) ∧
    (∀ g ∈ G, SoundTypeConvolution g)
```

For each lower fractional Newton face, write its reduced slope as `a/b`, `b≥2`.  Its horizontal
length is `b*d`; residual factors of degree `D` and multiplicity `μ` generate leaves `(b,D)`
when `μ=1`, or recursive clusters of size `μ` over `F_(q^D)` when `μ>1`.  Since
`μ≤d≤m/b<m`, recursion decreases.  Integral translations/recentrings either expose such a
face or enter the one π-rescale loop.  The unbounded numerator/gap variables must be summed as
geometric series.  This is the general classification argument that the degree-four engine is
experimentally implementing.

The stop-line theorem is:

```lean
theorem finiteGenreCoverAt_all : ∀ m : ℕ, FiniteGenreCoverAt m
```

Its proof must include all of the following, not merely OM factorization correctness:

1. every coefficient vector enters exactly one schema;
2. schemas are disjoint at every truncation level;
3. all non-loop recursive multiplicities are strictly smaller;
4. every unbounded depth variable has a positive decay exponent;
5. the symbolic sums are rational functions of `q` with denominators nonzero for `q≥2`;
6. every engine-produced factor and residual label transports to the claimed `typeOf`.

Without this theorem, a verified genre table at `n=4` remains a degree-four result.

## 5. Uniform recursion and reduction to `DecidedSliceAt`

Strong induction on cluster size constructs a finite law

```lean
def ClusterLaw (m δ : ℕ) :
    Finset (FactorizationType × RatFunc)
```

with `δ` denoting the residue closed-point degree.  The induction uses three classes of terms:

* shallow NP/OM leaves, decided by Theorem A and the type algebra;
* products of `ClusterLaw μ D` with `μ<m`;
* the diagonal rescale term `q^-κ_m * ClusterLaw m 1`.

For every output type `σ`, the last term is diagonal: it returns the same `σ`.  Hence

```text
L_m(σ) = shallow_m(σ) + q^-κ_m * FullMonic_m(σ; L_m),
```

and the only occurrence of the unknown at size `m` is its own all-at-one-rational-point cell.
Solving this scalar equation gives a rational function.  The residue-pattern convolution then
produces the full monic law at degree `n`.

The Lean-facing endpoint should target `decidedDensity`, not detour through
`genuineDensity`:

```lean
def DecidedValueLaw (n : ℕ) (σ : FactorizationType) : Prop :=
  ∃ num den : Polynomial ℚ, den ≠ 0 ∧
    ∀ (O : Type) [CommRing O] [IsDomain O] [IsDiscreteValuationRing O]
      [IsAdicComplete (IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal O) O]
      [Finite (IsLocalRing.ResidueField O)],
      den.eval ((residueCard O : ℕ) : ℚ) ≠ 0 ∧
      decidedDensity O n σ =
        ((num.eval ((residueCard O : ℕ) : ℚ) /
          den.eval ((residueCard O : ℕ) : ℚ) : ℚ) : ℝ)

theorem decidedSliceAt_of_valueLaws
    (n : ℕ) (h : ∀ σ, σ.degree = n → DecidedValueLaw n σ) :
    DecidedSliceAt n := h
```

The substantive equality comes from the disjoint finite-level genre recurrence, passage to
the limit by `decidedSeq_tendsto`, and uniqueness of the scalar fixed point.  An off-menu type
gets the rational function `0/1`, using the output menu and `typeOf_degree`.

## 7. Gates and falsifiers

1. **Wild-prime gate:** every theorem is instantiated at `(n,q)=(4,2)` and `(5,5)` or another
   case with residue characteristic dividing the degree.  A tame-only imported premise fails.
2. **No pointwise-termination substitution:** the genre-cover theorem must expose one finite
   schema list before an input coefficient vector is supplied.
3. **No bounded-depth substitution:** arbitrarily large slope numerators are represented by
   parameters and summed; no fixed precision cap occurs in the theorem.
4. **Disjointness tooth:** every finite truncation class has a unique genre and unique parameter
   tuple, up to explicitly divided finite symmetries.
5. **Decrease tooth:** remove the rescale genre and print a proof that every recursive child has
   cluster mass `<m`.
6. **Loop tooth:** the only same-mass edge is the all-at-one rational cluster, with coefficient
   exactly `q^-κ_m` conditionally and `q^(1-T_m)` in the full box.
7. **Faithfulness footprint:** Theorem A/frontier consumers print only Lean core plus the existing
   C.33 dissection cite.  Any new literature axiom gets its own hypothesis-by-hypothesis ledger.
8. **Density target tooth:** final value laws mention `decidedDensity`; no hidden general
   `DrainageAt` premise enters through a genuine-density conversion.
9. **Denominator gate:** prove both the polynomial denominator is nonzero and its evaluation at
   every residue cardinality is nonzero, exactly as `DecidedSliceAt` demands.
10. **Examples-first gate:** n=2 and n=3 reproduce landed values; n=4 reproduces all eleven rows
    in §8.  These are regression tests only.

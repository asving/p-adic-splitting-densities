(`leanspec/Leanspec/ChapH.lean:2963`) and decomposes the outline into five proof rows:
raw-to-image ghosts (GR-1/2), cofactor elimination and admissibility (GR-3/3b), the

**(b) Mechanism and the proof case-split.**  Normalize two planted factors at the smaller slope.
If `k<k'`, the first normalized reduction lives in the nonzero residue class `z`, while the
second reduces to a power of `X`; if `k=k'`, frame separation gives `z≠z'`, so the two reductions
are coprime powers of distinct linear factors.  In both cases every root-pair difference has
valuation `min(k,k')`, hence

```text
v(Res(P_p,P_p')) = μ_p μ_p' min(k_p,k_p').
```

This is the exact point where `min(k,k')` enters, and it explains both the equal-slope and
mixed-slope exhaustive rows (`verification/openmath/OM2_h116b_gauge_resultant.py:32`;
`H116B4_OUTLINE_2026-08-18.md:302`).  It also dictates the paper-proof split:

1. group children into equal-slope blocks; distinct residues give unit normalized pairings inside
   a block;
2. order the slope blocks; unequal-slope interactions are filtered triangular, with the lower
   slope contributing the `min` shift;
3. conjugate by the triangular planted-shear bases and by monic division; the root-free cofactor
   acts invertibly on each normalized obstruction quotient;
4. compute every determinantal ideal of the resulting block matrix, then cap each exponent at
   `N`.

The different lists `[2,3,4,5]` and `[2,3,5,7]` show why the equal-slope and unequal-slope blocks
cannot be merged.  They also warn that the displayed SNF list includes the planted source grades
and target/cofactor grades; the pairwise resultant controls the *interaction* after those fixed
shifts, not the raw list by itself (`runs/wave-b/h116b4_battery.py:159`).  In particular,
`sum e_i = v(det)` does not determine the list, the precise gap recorded by GR-7
(`H116B4_OUTLINE_2026-08-18.md:422`).

### Row 4 — one-level nonlinear lifting: GR-9

**(a) What the battery shows.**  Final nonlinear fibres and linear kernels have equal cardinality
at every checked base: CELL-1 `128/128`, CELL-2 `59049/59049`, CELL-4 `32768/32768`, and CELL-3

elements; the nonlinear fibre-delta set is not a group and separates from the kernel only at the
last digit (`out_DEC5R_battery.log:24`).  Thus a proof by identifying the nonlinear fibre with the
linear kernel is false even though their sizes agree.

The DEC5R extension supplies the missing explanation.  Check `(GR-9ii mechanism)` verifies at
every visited node that the one-grade digit map is affine, has one fixed linear part for the whole
level, and is live exactly when the target obstruction lies in its image
(`runs/wave-b/h116b4_battery.py:535`).  Numerically:

```text
CELL-1: three raw digits over F_2, |image|=2, hence K=2^3/2=4 at every level;
CELL-2: four raw digits over F_3, |image|=1, hence K=3^4=81 at every level;
CELL-4: four raw digits over F_2, |image|=1, hence K=2^4=16 at every level.
```

(`out_DEC5R_battery.log:48`, `:65`, `:74`).

**(b) Mechanism read from the numbers.**  Lift a level-`n` presentation by increments
`δ_p ∈ π^n Λ_p`.  Modulo `π^(n+1)`, every product term containing two increments vanishes, so the
next digit is an affine translate of the restricted Sylvester derivative.  Replacing the base by
another lift with the same level-`n` data changes coefficients only above that graded piece; the
derivative on the grade is therefore level-fixed.  Consequently every attainable next digit is
a coset of one kernel and has size `K_n`; an unattainable digit has size `0`.  This is the honest
two-case split:

```text
obstruction ∈ image(T_n)      -> exactly #ker(T_n) lifts;
obstruction ∉ image(T_n)      -> dead node, zero lifts.
```

CELL-1 happens to have nonlinear fibre = kernel as sets, but CELL-2 refutes using that shortcut;
the general proof must construct the affine torsor at *one grade at a time*
(`H116B4_OUTLINE_2026-08-18.md:461`; battery check `fibre-vs-kernel AS SETS [CELL-2]`).

**(c) Lean-facing statement sketch and landed inputs.**  Define `FitAtLevel`, truncation,


The expansion uses `alphaParent_coeff` (`ChapH/H115b.lean:111`); the source/target bases and kernel
cardinality come from Row 3; cofactor invertibility uses Row 2 plus

by the outline (`H116B4_OUTLINE_2026-08-18.md:493`).

**(d) REUSE vs NEW.**  Product expansion and finite affine-coset counting are **REUSE**.  The

**(b) Mechanism read from the numbers.**  Row 4 explains each node: it contributes `K_n` lifts to

```text

```

particular current node's obstruction misses the common image.  The counts satisfy


not absence of dead branches, is therefore the correct GR-10 induction hypothesis.  Iterating it
from the forced residue tuple yields the final leaf laws of Row 1; no comparison map between
child-read fibres is involved (`H116B4_OUTLINE_2026-08-18.md:504`, `:527`).

## Ordering recommendation

1. **Land Row 1 first** (b4.01--b4.04): it fixes the raw/image boundary and prevents later gauge
   counts from silently including ghosts (`H116B4_OUTLINE_2026-08-18.md:617`).
2. **Land Row 2 next** (b4.05--b4.06b): `Fit` and reverse admissibility define the correct target
   lattice and license the normalized cofactor action (`H116B4_OUTLINE_2026-08-18.md:369`).
3. **Research Row 3 in the explicit case order** equal-slope blocks -> ordered unequal-slope
   blocks -> cofactor conjugation -> truncated full list.  Land the resultant and generic Smith
   wrappers while the determinantal-ideal calculation is being completed; never replace the full
   list by the determinant (`H116B4_OUTLINE_2026-08-18.md:682`, `:686`).
4. **Prove Row 4 one grade at a time**, with the literal obstruction-zero/obstruction-nonzero
   split.  Do not assert nonlinear fibre = kernel as sets; CELL-2 refutes it
   (battery check `fibre-vs-kernel AS SETS [CELL-2]`).
5. **Prove Row 5 using `UniformOnSupport` as the induction invariant**, then land GR-11 and the
   empty/nonempty final assembly.  Do not infer iteration by multiplying the nonzero branch sizes;
   the dead-node rows show why that loses information (battery checks `(GR-10 mechanism)` and
   `(GR-9ii) [CELL-1/2/4]`).

This is a paper-proof-first schedule.  Rows 1 and 2 can be implemented immediately; generic pieces
of Row 3 can proceed in parallel once `Fit` is fixed, but the signed theorem must remain
**research-open, numerically certified, statement unchanged** until both the restricted truncated
Smith calculation and the uniform obstruction-pushforward induction are proved
(`H116B4_OUTLINE_2026-08-18.md:691`).

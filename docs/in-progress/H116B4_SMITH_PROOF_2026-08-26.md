and then discard `b_d`.  The surviving vector of length `mu_p` is
`rho^(p,j):=trem_p(h^(p,j))`.  Finally define

`V^rem[i,(p,j)] := 1 + ( (star_{p' != p} p^(p')) star rho^(p,j) )_i`,

with value `INF` outside its support.  Every object in this definition is a
function only of `(L,D,r)`: `beta_a=D_a-sum_b s_b min(k_b,k_a)`.

LEMMA SMGR-R1 (monic tropical division bound).  Let `H,P in O[X]`, with `P`
monic of degree `mu`.  Suppose `v(H_i)>=h_i` and `v(P_i)>=p_i`, with `p_mu=0`.
Then

`v(coeff_i(rem(H,P))) >= trem_p(h)_i` for every `i<mu`.

Proof.  Use the ordinary top-down monic division algorithm.  At degree `d`, its
quotient coefficient is the current leading coefficient `c_d`.  If the current
lower bounds are `b`, then `v(c_d)>=b_d`; subtracting
`c_d X^(d-mu)P` changes coefficient `d-mu+i` to a difference of two elements
with valuations at least `b_(d-mu+i)` and `b_d+p_i`.  The ultrametric inequality
gives the displayed minimum.  The leading term cancels exactly because `P` is
monic.  Descending induction on `d` is exactly the recurrence defining `trem`.
No division by a nonunit occurs. ∎

`v(coeff_i(S_(p,j))) >= V^rem[i,(p,j)]`.

`v(coeff_i(u^(a)_ell)) >= min_{p in L_a, j<mu_p} V^rem[i,(p,j)]`.

  Live only at μ ≥ 4 ∧ D′ ≥ 2, i.e. n ≥ 8 (OM-7's Lean `branch_ii_needs_mu_four` +
  `mu4_lives_at_eight`).

  the same shadow ledger at keys with f₁ ≥ 2 (ANNEX 6.3′'s corrected lift) — prose-only,
  machine-uncontacted at HEAD ("a NEW honest fence since BOX-4's leg is μ₂ = 2").

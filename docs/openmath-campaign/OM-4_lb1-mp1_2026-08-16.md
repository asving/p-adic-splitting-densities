### 1.3 The classical shadows (why CITE-CANDIDATE is on the table at all)

The repo's own citation-scope resolution (`docs/CITE_SCOPE_RESOLUTION_2026-08-13.md`) already
pins, at COVERS-ALL-O scope (arbitrary discrete valued field; our complete `O` a special case):

* **NS-2/NS-3 — [GN15] Theorem 2.3** (Guàrdia–Nart, *Genetics of polynomials over local
  fields*, Contemp. Math. 637 (2015) 207–241): for ANY inductive valuation `µ` with a MacLane
  chain and any key `φ ≁_µ φ_r`, every monic `g` factors as
  `g = g₀·φ^{ord_φ(g)}·∏_{(λ,ψ)} g_{λ,ψ}` with
  `deg g_{λ,ψ} = e_λ·ord_ψ(R_λ(g))·deg ψ·deg φ`, and "if `ord_ψ(R_λ(g)) = 1`, then `g_{λ,ψ}`
  is irreducible in `O_v[x]`". This is the block suite in one display, at every order.
* **GMN Thm 3.1/3.7/2.26** (TAMS 364 (2012); `docs/GMN_citations.md`): theorem of the polygon
  (side blocks, one-sided hulls), theorem of the residual polynomial (label blocks, pure
  residuals `R(G_i) ∼ ψ_i^{a_i}`), theorem of the product (multiplicativity feeding
  disjoint/exhaustive bookkeeping) — the ℚ_p-specialized secondary display.
* **GMN Thm 2.11** (`docs/references/HigherNewton.tex` lines 1258–1282, label `phir`): "We can
  effectively construct a monic polynomial `φ_r(x)` of type `t` such that
  `R_{r−1}(φ_r)(y) ∼ ψ_{r−1}(y)`. **This polynomial is irreducible over `ℤ_p[x]`** and it
  satisfies `deg φ_r = m_r, ω_r(φ_r) = 1, v_r(φ_r) = e_{r−1}f_{r−1}v_r(φ_{r−1})`." The proof's
  irreducibility step (line 1281): "The polynomial `φ_r(x)` is irreducible over `ℤ_p[x]` **by
  the Theorem of the product in order r−1**" — consuming only representative-defining
  properties. Companion sentence (line 887, §2.1 standing setting): "By the Theorem of the
  product in orders 1,…,r−1, the polynomials `φ_i(x)` are all irreducible over `ℤ_p[x]`."
* **[GN15] eq (2.1)**: `e(F) = e₀⋯e_r`, `f(F) = f₀⋯f_r` — the `(e,f)` emission.
* **[AGNPRW] Thm 4.4 + Prop 4.5** (NS-1/NS-3): the same at arbitrary valued fields, with the
  rank-one remark "If `v` has rank one, then `φ = Q`" (keys are irreducible over the
  henselization in the discrete rank-one case).

The identification that makes GMN Thm 2.11 bite for `(MP1)`: at the refined type
`t′ = (t; λ, y − s)` (order r+1 data with `e_r = 1` since `λ ∈ ℤ` and `f_r = deg(y−s) = 1`,

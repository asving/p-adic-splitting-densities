## §1. HIGH-LEVEL OUTLINE (from the sources, in the mandated order)

### 1.1 What the box says, and what it does not

`inertiaDegOf g'` (`leanfinal/Uniformity/Density/TypeOf.lean:193`) is the gcd (as
`sSup (normDivisors g')`) of the set of positive valuations `addVal O (Algebra.norm O x)`,
`x ≠ 0` ranging over the ORDER `AdjoinRoot g' = O[x]/(g')` — not over the maximal order
(CHAP-B H-7: "`inertiaDegOf` is a gcd over the **order** … this chapter never assumes the two
agree"). The box asks `m·d` to divide that gcd for every monic irreducible factor `g'` of the
leaf `g`. Since the additive set of norm-valuations generates a subgroup `f·ℤ` of `ℤ`, the box
is equivalently: **`m·d` divides `addVal O (norm x)` for every nonzero `x` in the order.**

The chapter proves, unconditionally (CHAP-B §7, all landed-vocabulary contracts):

* **B.52** — `m ∣ inertiaDegOf g` at every `d` (the `m` half: `AdjoinRoot g` is local with
  residue field `resField φ` of degree `m`, and the length mechanism B.51 gives
  `m ∣ v(N x)` for all `x`);
* **B.54** — `inertiaDegOf g ∣ m·d` (the upper bound, from the two witnesses
  `v(N(φ(α))) = m·u·d` and `v(N(π)) = ℓ·m·d` plus `Nat.Coprime u ℓ`);
* **B.55** — the bracket, collapsing at `d = 1` (B.58's unconditional leaf theorem);
* **B.60** — the `ℓ = 1, m = 1` slice, via `scaleRoots` extraction + landed `InertLeaf`.

The box is the residual gap `m·d ∣ …` at `d ≥ 2`, where the bracket `m ∣ f' ∣ m·d` does not
pin `f'`. H-7's own diagnosis of why B.52's mechanism stops at `m`: "the lower bound needs the
residual root to be realised in the residue field **of the leaf** [= of the factor field's
valuation ring] … and that is a statement about the valuation ring of `K(α)`, not about
`AdjoinRoot g`."

## §3. ANALYSIS AND VERDICT

### 3.1 New structural finding: the box's region lies entirely OFF the monogenic-maximal locus

**Claim.** Let `g'` be monic, `(u,ℓ)`-pure at an order-1 key `φ` of degree `m`, with
`ḡ' = φ̄^{ℓ'd'}` (`ℓ'd' ≥ 1`, `u ≥ 1`), and let `A := AdjoinRoot g' = O[x]/(g')`. Then
`A` is local with maximal ideal `(π, φ(α))` and residue field `A/(π, φ(α)) ≅ resField φ`,
of `ResidueField O`-degree `m`.

*Proof.* `A/πA ≅ (ResidueField O)[x]/(φ̄^{ℓ'd'})`, a local ring with nilpotent maximal ideal
`(φ̄)`; so `A` is local with maximal ideal `𝔪 = (π, φ(α))`, and
`A/𝔪 ≅ (ResidueField O)[x]/(φ̄) = resField φ`, a field of degree `m`. ∎ (This is B.49's
landed mechanism; nothing new.)

**Consequence.** Suppose `d ≥ 2` (any factor `g'` of a `d ≥ 2` leaf that still has
`d' ≥ 2` — under B.61's hypotheses, `g' = g` itself). If `A` were a DVR (= the
monogenic-maximal locus of `TypeOfFaithful.lean`), then the classical `(e,f)` of `𝔪` would
be read off `A` itself: `f = [A/𝔪 : ResidueField O] = m` and, by the fundamental identity
(Quarry), `e = ℓ'd'`. But the classical pair of the extension `L/K` is `(ℓ', m·d')` by (A).
A DVR order IS the maximal order, so the two readings would coincide, forcing `m = m·d'`,
i.e. `d' = 1` — contradiction. **Hence at `d ≥ 2` the order `AdjoinRoot g'` is never a DVR,
and the landed HYP.01 machinery (`typeOf_eq_classical_of_isDiscreteValuationRing`,
`typeOf_inert_faithful` — both keyed to `[IsDiscreteValuationRing (AdjoinRoot g)]` or to
`Irreducible ḡ`, and `ḡ = φ̄^{ℓd}` is never irreducible at `ℓd ≥ 2`) can NEVER fire on the
box's region.** No assembly of landed pieces proves any instance of `B-BOX-1` at `d ≥ 2`:
the box genuinely requires the field-side carrier (B.62's object) or an axiom. This
*confirms* H-7's sizing and CHAP-I I.21's stub note ("typing needs the factor-field
valuation-ring carrier — the same object B-BOX-1's repair route would build") by a proof
rather than an impression, and it upgrades the "order/field gap" from a caution to a
structural theorem of the region: **on the box's entire region, order ≠ maximal order.**
(Numeric exhibit: CERT-C(ii).)

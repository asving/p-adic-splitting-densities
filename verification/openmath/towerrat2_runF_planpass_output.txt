    Merely listing components does not enforce that they arise from the same stage construction or commute with projection, block splitting, and composition. A carrier could supply unrelated residual maps that happen to return the desired labels. The theorem would then be true for an over-permissive synthetic interface but would not express the original ladder mechanism.

11. **T2 can weaken quantifier order and parameter uniformity.**

    > “HE3.A's recursion step … HE6's ℓ ≥ 2 branch … GENTOW4's per-block literal HE7.A instances”

    **Classification: DESIGN-RISK**

    The five sources appear to quantify over different indices—stage degree, height, side, block, and tower parameters. An abstract carrier can silently replace “for every parameter, one construction works uniformly” by “for every parameter, there exists a carrier,” or even by “given a carrier containing chosen witnesses.” Those statements admit the same individual instance examples but differ in uniformity and downstream composability.

12. **T2’s five consumption checks cannot detect circular proofs without provenance checks.**

    > “full 2-clean arc + five consumption checks”

    **Classification: BLIND-SPOT**

    A consumption check establishes an implication in the assembled environment, not that the implication avoids the absorbed theorem. If old HE6R1, HETOW, or HE7 results remain available, an instance proof or bridge can cite the very statement it is said to replace. Two hostile passes and an MD5 comparison do not detect dependency cycles unless proof-term/import provenance is audited.

13. **T2’s compression estimate omits the cost of changing downstream signatures.**

    > “the σ-chain's Lean surface shrinks from 4 bridge layers to 1 theorem + instance records”

    **Classification: UNDERESTIMATE**

    Generalizing HE7.A changes the types consumed by HE6R1, HETOW, GENTOW4, and potentially HE3/HE6. Projection lemmas, coercions, block-index conversions, termination measures, non-vacuity witnesses, and literature-faithfulness entries may all move. “One theorem + instance records” prices only the visible theorem count, not the interface migration demanded by the dependency DAG.

14. **T3 identifies a verbal shape, not a demonstrated common statement signature.**

    > “the common shape of GENTOW2-B″ … HETOW-4 … and GENTOW-6.4”

    **Classification: DESIGN-RISK**

    The examples involve respectively a height-indexed unit formula, a polynomial substitution/normalizer identity, and a window cap. They may require different monoids, actions, index orientations, boundary conventions, or side conditions. A sufficiently abstract “telescope” can cover all three only by accepting each desired endpoint equation as an input, yielding a vacuous unification.

15. **T3’s regression plan is biased toward only one of its three consumers.**

    > “battery re-uses the committed HETOW-4 frames (203 checks / 37 dual-route gp jobs) as regression”

    **Classification: BLIND-SPOT**

    HETOW-4-heavy tests cannot establish preservation of GENTOW2-B″’s `ϑ(t)·w^{f₃−t}` indexing or GENTOW-6.4’s 𝒯-free cap and window hypotheses. Three textual consumption checks may catch statement mismatch, but the advertised battery does not test the distinct boundary and degeneracy cases of all three instantiations.

16. **T4 conflates witness attainment with the stronger threshold and count-box conclusions.**

    > “one attainment lemma covering GENTOW3's faithfulness threshold … and GENTOW6's CERT-TOP”

    > “also gives the four count-grade sub-boxes … ONE home — they are refinements of this lemma's count side”

    **Classification: DESIGN-RISK**

    A touched-coordinate witness may establish existence or attainment without proving the quantitative threshold formula, uniqueness, faithfulness, or count refinements. Calling the boxes “refinements” does not establish that they share hypotheses or derive from one count-side interface. The unified lemma could preserve CERT-TOP while weakening the threshold’s uniformity, or vice versa.

17. **T4 understates its dependency and future revision surface.**

    > “RE-VERIFY. 2-clean arc; two consumption checks”

    > “their eventual closure revises one text”

    **Classification: UNDERESTIMATE**

    T4 is intended to become the home of four open sub-boxes and is consumed from both GENTOW3 and GENTOW6, which themselves feed later tower conclusions. Closing BOX-4 or changing the certificate definition can require updates to the unified note, both instance maps, box hypotheses, regressions, and Lean consumers. “Two checks” and “one text” do not reflect that fan-out.

18. **T5 is not merely definitional if it changes the canonical frame and records nontrivial evidence.**

    > “RE-VERIFY. Light: mostly definitional + record consolidation; 2-clean arc at reduced size; no absorbed statement changes.”

    > “defining the cross-frame unit w and the w-twisted realized residual ψ^{(w)} in the w-inclusive canonical frame”

    **Classification: UNDERESTIMATE**

    Moving a definition into a new canonical frame changes coercions, normalization equalities, and the interpretation of existing occurrences. The attached facts `w ≠ 1` and `ψ₃^{(w)}(Y)=0` are propositions with instance scope, not mere record formatting. Their transfer requires frame-equivalence and provenance checks, especially because T5 depends on T1’s frame.

19. **T5’s “open question” formulation risks changing a proposition into an assumption.**

    > “The w = 1 open question becomes a named hypothesis, the honest-`sorry` analogue at statement level.”

    **Classification: DESIGN-RISK**

    A named hypothesis can make downstream statements conditional on `w=1`, `w≠1`, or decidability of the case, depending on its precise formulation. That can silently weaken unconditional statements currently supported by measured `w≠1` instances. Describing it as a `sorry` analogue is particularly hazardous: unresolved mathematics can migrate into the interface and make the new note appear complete.

20. **SG-1 mistakes archival replacement for logical simplification.**

    > “the notes HE6, HE7, HE6R1, HETOW, and GENTOW4's read layer become history/instances”

    **Classification: DESIGN-RISK**

    Unless carrier instances are proved without importing those notes’ substantive results, the notes remain proof suppliers. Labeling them “history” can cause stage two to omit necessary formal proofs. The failure mode is especially direct for HE7: the generalized theorem may simply be HE7 repackaged, while HE6R1 and HETOW remain necessary to construct its instances.

21. **SG-1’s success criterion is only syntactic size.**

    > “Failure mode: the carrier signature bloats to re-encode each note's frame, and nothing is shorter.”

    **Classification: BLIND-SPOT**

    A compact signature can still be logically weak, circular, non-uniform, or semantically incoherent. Conversely, a large but conservative interface may be sound. The probe checks HE6 first and watches bloat, but it does not require a field-by-field implication ledger, independent instance construction, or preservation of quantifier order for all five consumers.

22. **SG-2’s proposed retirement of supplier notes is incompatible with proving WELDMASTER unless its proof is axiomatized.**

    > “GRTJA/GRTJB/GRTJC … retire to suppliers-of-WELDMASTER”

    > “Lean would formalize the consumed faces only”

    **Classification: DESIGN-RISK**

    If WELDMASTER is to be kernel-proved, its suppliers—or replacement proofs of their content—remain required. If WELDMASTER is declared as an axiom so only its faces are consumed, that expands the literature/axiom policy beyond the enumerated allowed axioms. The plan does not distinguish “not downstream-consumed” from “not needed to prove the upstream theorem.”

23. **SG-2 tests dependence of one theorem but draws a whole-path retirement conclusion.**

    > “Probe: one session tracing GENHN-4's proof inputs against WELDMASTER's M2/M3 faces.”

    > “If yes, the load-bearing weld surface = WELDMASTER + JD0”

    **Classification: UNDERESTIMATE**

    Tracing GENHN-4 alone does not establish that no other theorem, definition, non-vacuity witness, or semantic-faithfulness obligation consumes GRTJA/B/C. Nor does it prove that M2/M3 expose enough data without the junction definitions. A 6,900-line scope removal needs a corpus-wide dependency and proof-provenance audit, not one proof-input session.

24. **SG-3 contradicts the plan’s declared separation between letters and counts.**

    > “the T1 lemma applied at every event type”

    > “the LETTER layer … never enters counts (JD0)”

    **Classification: DESIGN-RISK**

    GENIND’s α/β formulas are count-side, while T1 is explicitly a letter-layer lemma invisible to counts. To make T1 generate those formulas, SG-3 must either enrich T1 with count transport—expanding T1’s scope—or weaken JD0’s blindness claim. The plan does not price this architectural change or say which boundary is expected to move.

25. **SG-3’s example-first probe cannot validate dissolution of the general case split.**

    > “re-derive HMENU3's 33-family menu from the candidate unified form on the committed exact tables”

    **Classification: BLIND-SPOT**

    Agreement on the cubic table can result from finite interpolation or a formulation specialized to the base case. It cannot show that the five regimes collapse uniformly for arbitrary degree, especially where GENIND’s composite-opening criterion begins only at `n ≥ 4`. The probe lacks a generality or non-overfitting check.

26. **SG-4 has no independent test oracle.**

    > “Probe rides SG-3's battery.”

    **Classification: BLIND-SPOT**

    SG-3 tests reconstruction of count menus, whereas SG-4 concerns whether letters-versus-depth splits disappear under a frame change in GENTOW2/GENTOW5. A shared battery can pass the menu predictions while never exercising the old split’s exceptional cases. The two speculative claims require separately preregistered failure criteria.

27. **S-0 indexes only named statements, while the campaign absorbs arguments and annexes.**

    > “extract every named theorem/lemma statement of the 26 texts”

    > “For each absorbed argument, a CONSUMPTION CHECK”

    **Classification: BLIND-SPOT**

    Local claims, displayed equations, scope riders, definition clauses, annex conclusions, and prose-bound restrictions need not be named theorems or lemmas. Such material can be absorbed or weakened without any ledger entry. This is acute for T1’s gauge annexes and T5’s pass-record facts.

28. **S-0 hashes bytes but not the statement’s binding context.**

    > “md5 + commit pin per statement”

    > “the absorbed statement is byte-identical to the accepted text per the S-0 ledger”

    **Classification: BLIND-SPOT**

    Identical statement text can mean something different after namespace changes, altered notation, changed implicit variables, different local instances, or relocated definitions. For example, `∀ x, Read x = …` may hash identically while `Read`, the carrier type, or a section variable is resolved differently. A byte hash of the extracted span does not preserve elaborated semantics.

29. **S-0 permits re-quantification outside the hashed span.**

    > “display ‘new lemma ⟹ absorbed statement,’ where the absorbed statement is byte-identical”

    **Classification: BLIND-SPOT**

    The display can place extra binders or assumptions before the quoted statement: “for a fixed admissible carrier `C` satisfying completeness and termination, [byte-identical statement].” The quoted body matches MD5, but a formerly universal theorem has become conditional or restricted. The same failure occurs when an existential witness is fixed in surrounding prose.

30. **S-0 permits hypotheses to migrate into definitions or typeclasses.**

    > “the absorbed statement is byte-identical to the accepted text per the S-0 ledger”

    **Classification: BLIND-SPOT**

    A new definition of “carrier,” “side,” “test family,” “canonical frame,” or “touched coordinate” can include exactly the property that was formerly proved. The consumed statement remains byte-identical but ranges over a narrower subtype. This is the central danger for T2 and T4, and hashing cannot detect it.

31. **S-0 does not hash prose that fixes scope and interpretation.**

    > “extract every named theorem/lemma statement”

    **Classification: BLIND-SPOT**

    Surrounding prose may specify that a theorem is base-change-free, uniform in all primes, includes wild cases, uses the original frame, or excludes a citation. If those constraints are not within the extracted statement span, a new consumption check can match the statement bytes while dropping them. The plan itself relies repeatedly on such prose-level properties.

32. **S-0’s commit pin becomes ambiguous once allowed appends are made.**

    > “md5 + commit pin per statement”

    > “the accepted notes get dated appends only”

    **Classification: MINOR**

    An append changes the file and repository commit without changing the statement. The plan does not say whether the ledger remains pinned to the pre-append commit, receives a new file-level pin, or records both. Without a policy, later checks can incorrectly report staleness or silently re-extract from a newer context.

33. **MD5 is an unnecessarily weak identity mechanism for an adversarial ledger.**

    > “md5 match required”

    **Classification: BLIND-SPOT**

    MD5 is collision-broken. Accidental collision is negligible, but this is explicitly an adversarial verification instrument and statements are text under repository control. More importantly, the plan treats an MD5 match as semantic protection when it supplies only weak byte identity. A collision or extraction-boundary manipulation could pass the mandatory check.

34. **S-1 is scheduled before the plan has resolved the abstraction hazard shared with T2 and T5.**

    > “S-1 | T1 GAUGE-COCYCLE: compose …”

    > “proved once in the w-inclusive canonical frame, as a cocycle statement over the composed carrier”

    **Classification: UNDERESTIMATE**

    T1 already needs a composed-carrier and canonical-frame design, and its output defines the frame consumed by T5 and SG-4. Yet only T2 receives a dedicated design probe and compose-time Lean signature. A faulty T1 carrier boundary propagates immediately into S-4 and S-8, so treating S-1 as one composer session understates its statement-design work.

35. **S-1’s seven checks do not gate downstream work on semantic, rather than textual, preservation.**

    > “Then S-1 … its frame feeds S-4/S-8.”

    > “A consumption check failing = the unit is wrong”

    **Classification: DESIGN-RISK**

    S-4 and S-8 are gated only on S-1 landing, while S-1’s checks can pass through narrowed carrier definitions or imported old lemmas. Thus downstream units can build on a formally accepted but weakened frame abstraction. The ordering amplifies S-0’s blind spots instead of containing them.

36. **S-7’s declared independence omits dependence on the ledger and on formalization policy.**

    > “S-7 | SG-2 probe … | 1 session | —”

    > “S-7 anytime (independent, one session).”

    **Classification: UNDERESTIMATE**

    The probe compares exact proof inputs and supports a future claim that notes leave the load-bearing set. That requires stable statement identities, definition provenance, and a decision about whether WELDMASTER is proved or axiomatized. Those are respectively tied to S-0 and stage-two axiom policy. It is operationally runnable anytime, but its advertised conclusion is not independent.

37. **The verification protocol does not require freshness from the absorbed proofs.**

    > “model-diverse verifiers, fresh route per pass”

    > “display ‘new lemma ⟹ absorbed statement’”

    **Classification: BLIND-SPOT**

    “Fresh route” describes reviewer behavior, not the dependency graph of the proof artifact. A new note may import or quote an absorbed result inside its carrier construction or consumption proof. Reviewers can independently verify a circular implication unless they are required to inspect imports and proof provenance.

38. **The protocol allows absorption appends to overstate what was preserved.**

    > “the accepted notes get dated appends only (‘absorbed by S-k as instance …’)”

    **Classification: DESIGN-RISK**

    The append is made after unit acceptance, but acceptance relies on the deficient ledger and consumption protocol. An umbrella statement may be preserved while an unnamed rider, uniformity clause, or construction method is not. The append can therefore record full “instance” status more strongly than the actual check establishes.

39. **The stage-two replacement rule magnifies any single false-positive consumption check.**

    > “accepted S-units replace per-note formalization targets wherever a consumption check landed”

    **Classification: DESIGN-RISK**

    A consumption check can land using extra hypotheses, restricted carrier definitions, or circular citations. Stage two then drops the original target, removing the very source that would expose the missing obligation. This turns a ledger blind spot into an irreversible scope omission rather than a local documentation defect.

40. **The plan’s line-count savings are not aligned with its own dependency DAG.**

    > “If only S-1/S-2/S-3 land, stage two already drops ~12 per-note argument sites”

    > “if S-7's probe lands, the junction notes leave the load-bearing set”

    **Classification: UNDERESTIMATE**

    Replacing argument sites does not remove the need to formalize definitions, instance construction, compatibility lemmas, upstream proofs, or non-vacuity evidence. Likewise, a downstream theorem ceasing to cite a junction note does not remove that note if it proves WELDMASTER. The plan repeatedly equates fewer consumption sites with less total formalization without pricing upstream proof dependencies.

### CHECKED CLEAN

No charged item is checked clean: each of T1, T2, T3, T4, T5, SG-1, SG-2, SG-3, SG-4, S-0, S-1, and S-7 has at least one design-level failure mode identified above.

Overall, the blueprint has a clear corpus map, explicit sequencing, honest speculation grades, and a serious regression discipline, but its central preservation mechanism proves byte identity of selected text rather than semantic conservativity. That defect is especially dangerous for T2, where abstract structures can internalize conclusions, narrow quantifiers, hide base changes, or create circular instances while all five checks appear to pass. Because stage two is instructed to discard per-note targets based on those checks, the blind spot can produce a genuinely weakened artifact and an incomplete formalization scope. The underestimated coupling around T1/T5/SG-3/SG-4 and the unsupported retirement inference in SG-2/S-7 compound the problem.

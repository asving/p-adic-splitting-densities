## 2. Proof attempt

### The cocycle carry identity

Assume first that X(i) supplies the scored stage-level twisted algebra. Because it is the associated graded algebra of commutative division arithmetic, its multiplication is associative and commutative. Consequently \(c\) satisfies the normalized cocycle identity

\[
c(a,b)c(a+b,d)=c(b,d)c(a,b+d).
\]

This is the standard equivalence between associativity of a twisted group algebra and the 2-cocycle identity.

Fix \(j\le i\), and put \(r=i-j\). The exact-height part of \((-w)^rA_i\) is

\[
s^r n(\kappa)^r\cdot a_i n(d_i)
 =s^ra_iq_r c(r\kappa,d_i)n(d_j),
\]

because \(d_i+r\kappa=d_j\).

When this contribution is assembled as the degree-\(j\) residual coefficient, it acquires the factor

\[
\lambda_{\mu,j}=q_jc(d_j,j\kappa).
\]

The crucial carry identity is therefore

\[
\lambda_{\mu,j}q_r c(r\kappa,d_i)
   =\lambda_{\mu,i}. \tag{1}
\]

Indeed, the left-hand side is the scalar obtained by writing

\[
\bigl(n(\kappa)^r n(d_i)\bigr)n(\kappa)^j
\]

in the basis \(n(\mu\kappa)\), whereas the right-hand side is the scalar obtained from

\[
n(d_i)n(\kappa)^i.
\]

These are the same product because \(i=r+j\), and multiplication is associative and commutative. Expanding the two products gives (1) explicitly.

This proof uses the full cocycle, including the outer \(\bar z^\delta\). It does not attempt to gauge that factor away. WM-COB is therefore unnecessary for (1); it only explains why the inner factors \(\prod z_j^{D_j}\) form a coboundary.

### Coefficient transformation

In a commutative ring the binomial theorem gives

\[
(\Phi')^i
 =(\Phi'_{\rm new}-w)^i
 =\sum_{j=0}^{i}\binom{i}{j}
   (\Phi'_{\rm new})^j(-w)^{i-j}.
\]

Hence the exact-height part of the new coefficient at position \(j\) is the sum over \(i\ge j\) of the exact-height parts of

\[
\binom{i}{j}(-w)^{i-j}A_i.
\]

Canonical reductions needed to restore coefficient degree may create carries. Under (M0), the associated-graded calculation uses the same division chain; GENHN-3(d) says those carries move upward only. Terms containing either the higher-\(dv\) part of \(w\) or a higher-\(dv\) part of \(A_i\) have valuation at least

\[
(i-j)\kappa+d_i+1=d_j+1,
\]

so they do not affect the residue at \(d_j\). This is the standard initial-form rule for a multiplicative filtration.

Using (1), the normalized residue contributed by index \(i\) is

\[
\binom{i}{j}s^{i-j}\lambda_{\mu,i}a_i
 =\binom{i}{j}s^{i-j}r_i.
\]

Therefore

\[
\operatorname{res}_{d_j}(A_j^{\rm new})
 =\sum_{i=j}^{\mu}\binom{i}{j}r_i s^{i-j}
 =[T^j]R(T+s).
\]

This proves X(ii), conditional on the stage-level instantiation in X(i).

The proof also covers:

- **Collisions:** terms landing in the same line are added in that line. If they cancel, their sum has zero initial form and the valuation rises, exactly as the coefficient formula predicts.
- **Mod-wrap:** every wrap is included in \(c\) and \(q_t\); identity (1) follows from associativity after reduction modulo \(E\).
- **Positive characteristic:** no division by factorials occurs. The displayed binomial coefficients are their images in \(K\), and

## 2. Proof attempt

### The cocycle carry identity

Assume first that X(i) supplies the scored stage-level twisted algebra. Because it is the associated graded algebra of commutative division arithmetic, its multiplication is associative and commutative. Consequently \(c\) satisfies the normalized cocycle identity

\[
c(a,b)c(a+b,d)=c(b,d)c(a,b+d).
\]

This is the standard equivalence between associativity of a twisted group algebra and the 2-cocycle identity.

Fix \(j\le i\), and put \(r=i-j\). The exact-height part of \((-w)^rA_i\) is

\[
s^r n(\kappa)^r\cdot a_i n(d_i)
 =s^ra_iq_r c(r\kappa,d_i)n(d_j),
\]

because \(d_i+r\kappa=d_j\).

When this contribution is assembled as the degree-\(j\) residual coefficient, it acquires the factor

\[
\lambda_{\mu,j}=q_jc(d_j,j\kappa).
\]

The crucial carry identity is therefore

\[
\lambda_{\mu,j}q_r c(r\kappa,d_i)
   =\lambda_{\mu,i}. \tag{1}
\]

Indeed, the left-hand side is the scalar obtained by writing

\[
\bigl(n(\kappa)^r n(d_i)\bigr)n(\kappa)^j
\]

in the basis \(n(\mu\kappa)\), whereas the right-hand side is the scalar obtained from

\[
n(d_i)n(\kappa)^i.
\]

These are the same product because \(i=r+j\), and multiplication is associative and commutative. Expanding the two products gives (1) explicitly.

This proof uses the full cocycle, including the outer \(\bar z^\delta\). It does not attempt to gauge that factor away. WM-COB is therefore unnecessary for (1); it only explains why the inner factors \(\prod z_j^{D_j}\) form a coboundary.

### Coefficient transformation

In a commutative ring the binomial theorem gives

\[
(\Phi')^i
 =(\Phi'_{\rm new}-w)^i
 =\sum_{j=0}^{i}\binom{i}{j}
   (\Phi'_{\rm new})^j(-w)^{i-j}.
\]

Hence the exact-height part of the new coefficient at position \(j\) is the sum over \(i\ge j\) of the exact-height parts of

\[
\binom{i}{j}(-w)^{i-j}A_i.
\]

Canonical reductions needed to restore coefficient degree may create carries. Under (M0), the associated-graded calculation uses the same division chain; GENHN-3(d) says those carries move upward only. Terms containing either the higher-\(dv\) part of \(w\) or a higher-\(dv\) part of \(A_i\) have valuation at least

\[
(i-j)\kappa+d_i+1=d_j+1,
\]

so they do not affect the residue at \(d_j\). This is the standard initial-form rule for a multiplicative filtration.

Using (1), the normalized residue contributed by index \(i\) is

\[
\binom{i}{j}s^{i-j}\lambda_{\mu,i}a_i
 =\binom{i}{j}s^{i-j}r_i.
\]

Therefore

\[
\operatorname{res}_{d_j}(A_j^{\rm new})
 =\sum_{i=j}^{\mu}\binom{i}{j}r_i s^{i-j}
 =[T^j]R(T+s).
\]

This proves X(ii), conditional on the stage-level instantiation in X(i).

The proof also covers:

- **Collisions:** terms landing in the same line are added in that line. If they cancel, their sum has zero initial form and the valuation rises, exactly as the coefficient formula predicts.
- **Mod-wrap:** every wrap is included in \(c\) and \(q_t\); identity (1) follows from associativity after reduction modulo \(E\).
- **Positive characteristic:** no division by factorials occurs. The displayed binomial coefficients are their images in \(K\), and

  \[
  ((T+s)-s)^\mu=T^\mu
  \]

  is a polynomial-substitution identity in every characteristic.

Thus the additive carry calculation itself is complete once the event is licensed to use the relevant twisted graded algebra.

1. **Verbatim quote:** “Deep perturbation \(f = \Phi2^{\mu2} + g\) (\(g\) in-budget with slots at heights \(> \Theta_j\), disc \(\ne 0\)): both reads shift by the SAME \(g\)-digits (linearity of both divisions in \(f\)), so the divergence at \(\Theta_j\) persists…”

   **Classification:** JUSTIFICATION GAP.

   **Failure:** Linearity gives
   \[
   \operatorname{Sh}(f_0+g)-C(f_0+g)
   =(\operatorname{Sh}(f_0)-C(f_0))+(\operatorname{Sh}(g)-C(g)),
   \]
   not \(\operatorname{Sh}(g)=C(g)\). Thus the height-\(\Theta_j\) digit can cancel unless the note proves
   \[
   (\operatorname{Sh}(g)-C(g))_{\Theta_j}=0
   \quad\text{or at least}\quad
   \ne-(\operatorname{Sh}(f_0)-C(f_0))_{\Theta_j}.
   \]
   Neither “in-budget,” honest slots above \(\Theta_j\), nor \(\operatorname{disc}(f)\ne0\) supplies that conclusion. The note’s own FR-M3 construction exposes the missing implication: for
   \(g_W=2x\Phi2^2+8\Phi2\), the honest \(j=0\) coefficient is zero, hence has pin \(+\infty>\Theta_0\), while the shadow has \(ShC_0=32\) and first discrepancy exactly \(\Theta_0=10\). Therefore honest depth at coordinate \(j\) does not imply that the perturbation shifts the two reads identically there. If “all coordinates are deep” is intended to prevent this, a corresponding filtered-linearity lemma is absent.

2. **Verbatim quote:** “The shadow read at heights \(\ge \Theta_j\) is NOT a function of the honest data.”

   **Classification:** JUSTIFICATION GAP.

   **Failure:** Under the note’s own Setting, the full honest data are the composed coefficients
   \[
   f=\Phi2^{\mu2}+\sum_{j<\mu2}C_j\Phi2^j.
   \]
   With the key fixed, this tuple uniquely determines \(f\), and the stage-shadow algorithm then deterministically determines every \(ShC_j\). Thus the shadow is necessarily a function of the full honest composed data. The proof supplies only the reverse-direction example—“identical shadow data and different honest reads”—and examples with fixed honest \(\sigma\), which show at most that the shadow is not determined by a coarse honest label or truncated pricing datum. They do not prove the displayed claim about “the honest data.” The claim needs an explicit truncation/coarsening scope; literally read, it is false.

## 6. BOUNDARY SELF-AUDIT (prose-genre pilot)

### 6.0 The rule, and how this shard differs from shard 1

Same rule — **a unit is the smallest span carrying one complete claim together with its proof obligations** — and the same four-part test (own proof obligation / cited by designation / own supersession history / own conditionality or consumer set; see `spec/EFF-HE7-s1of3.md` §6.0).

**What is different here.** §S9–§S11 is a **ledger region**: boxes, preregistered predicates, verdict tables and dated disclosures. Its "claims" are *records*, and the proof obligation of a record is its **audit** (a re-count, a git comparison, a guard analysis), not a derivation. ANNEX F-1 is a **post-freeze annex**: its units are simultaneously new statements (homed here) and supersession instructions (targeting shard 1). Both cases needed rule extensions, recorded in §6.3.

### 6.1 Boundaries drawn, with the arguable ones recorded both ways

### 6.3 Which v2 rules fought the prose genre (this shard's five, additional to shard 1's eight)

1. **`regression-record` conflates three different things.** A *preregistration* (`.15`, `.38`), a *run record* (`.20`, `.21`, `.39`), and an *instrument description* (`.13`) have different truth conditions and different failure modes; the enum gives them one name. **Fix: `preregistration | run-record | instrument-record`, with `regression-record` retired.**
2. **Disclosures have no disposition vocabulary.** The seven instrument disclosures needed four distinct labels that the template does not supply: *signed vacuity disclosure* (tested on 1 of 42), *disclosed non-repair* (guard never fired), *accepted-with-decorrelation-supplied* (answered by an independent instrument), and *stale self-description* (the frozen `WIP = False` comment). **Fix: extend the TEETH disposition enum to cover disclosures, not only tests.**
3. **The header assumes ONE freeze predicate; this note has two.** Text-freeze (acceptance, line 1607) and instrument-freeze (seal 1956151, line 1384) are independent, and the second is what turns every later defect into a disclosure. **Fix: `FREEZE PREDICATES:` as a header list, each with its quote and its pin.**
4. **Tables need an arithmetic-audit slot, and it pays.** Recomputing Q1's thirteen rows found a real, previously unrecorded defect (source defect 2: the `[r1]` degenerate distribution's third row is labelled `s = 2` where the table forces `s = 1`) and an unstated convention (source defect 3: the σ columns count READ-OK members, not members). Neither is visible without doing the sums. **Fix: for any `[TABLE]` of counts, require a compiler arithmetic audit line — row sums, column totals, and cross-checks against every other number in the note that should match.**
5. **Commit hashes and artifact filenames are DAG edges and the XREF rule ignores them.** This shard cites nine commit hashes and eleven artifact files; all were resolvable (`git cat-file -t`, `ls`), and **one machine family was reported with no filename at all** (the 780/780 f₁ = 2 family), which no amount of grepping can verify. **Fix: extend rule 2 to `PIN VERIFICATION` — commit hashes via `git cat-file -t`, artifact paths via existence, and an explicit `UNPINNED` disposition for a reported run with no artifact.**

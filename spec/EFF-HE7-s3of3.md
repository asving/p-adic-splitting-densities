### 6.3 Which v2 rules fought the prose genre (this shard's five, additional to shard 1's eight and shard 2's five)

1. **There is no SUPERSESSION KIND field, and this note needs seven values.** Across the three shards the compiler had to distinguish: **replacement** (`.03`, `.28`, `.31`), **license** (`.16` — supplies a missing justification for text that stays as written), **wording-rider** (`.18`, `.20`), **provenance-rider** (`.25`, `.26`), **as-of scoping** (`.30`), **counter re-reading** (`.24`), **inventory completion** (`.19`), plus shard 2's **scope-pin** (ANNEX F-1's “Read as”). 2. **A unit that is both a statement and an instruction needs a second structured field.** Twelve units here are homed in this shard and *targeted* at another. RESOLUTION TRACE handles “where did this text come from”; nothing handles “where does this text land”. The compiler improvised a `Targets:` sentence inside CONDITIONALITY. **Fix: a `TARGETS:` field listing `<shard>.<id>` per supersession, so the merge run can build the edge list mechanically instead of by prose-reading.**
3. **Supersession CHAINS have no representation.** §S5.5(b)'s parenthesis carries **three** layers (frozen text → `.32`'s scalar → `.33`'s pin height); LEMMA HE7-L1's display carries **two** (`.28` → `.31`); ANNEX F-1's HE7-13′ supersedes a lemma that ANNEX R then leaves alone. A flat “this supersedes that” field cannot say which reading is terminal. **Fix: `CHAIN:` with an explicit TERMINAL marker** — otherwise a chapter cut can pick a superseded intermediate.
4. **“Frozen self-description that became false” recurs and has no disposition.** Four instances: ANNEX F-1's “has NOT been through a hostile pass” (`EFF.HE7.s2of3.30`), ANNEX R's identical clause (`.01`), `.11`'s “none of it has been through a hostile pass”, and `.21`'s “the annex itself is unpassed”. All four were falsified by `.23` and none is edited, correctly, under the freeze. **Fix: a `STALE-SELF-DESCRIPTION` conditionality tag** — the reader needs to know a frozen sentence about the note's own status is no longer true, and “superseded” is the wrong word for it.
5. **The teeth vocabulary cannot express “found by a decorrelated model; no tooth could have”.** F1 (a definition's incoherence) and F2 (an underived equivalence) are unfalsifiable by any σ-agreement battery, and the note's own machine record is silent about them. The inverse table needs to say so rather than leaving the rows blank. **Fix: an `AUDIT` disposition class alongside the executable ones — `decorrelated-model audit | in-house hostile pass | transfer audit | arithmetic recount` — with the note's own “no tooth applies” sentence quoted.** This is the single most important addition for the remaining prose notes, because prose proofs fail in ways batteries cannot see.

## 6. BOUNDARY SELF-AUDIT

This range is records, not proof — six boxes, a supplier stack, a battery, a scorecard, eleven appends, and (at the tail) one genuinely new LaTeX theorem. The boundary problem is therefore different from shard 1's: not “where does a statement end inside a blockquote?” but **“when is a record a unit?”**

### 6.0 The rule applied

> **A unit is the smallest span carrying one complete claim together with its proof obligations.**

Applied to records, the four tests read: (1) does the span make a falsifiable claim (a count, a status, a scope) rather than merely point? (2) is it cited BY DESIGNATION from elsewhere? (3) does it have its OWN supersession history? (4) does it have its own conditionality or consumer set?

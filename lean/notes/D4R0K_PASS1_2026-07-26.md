## Numbered findings

1. **CRITICAL ERROR — Lemma W’s definition of “grammatical” is insufficient, so “grammatical ⇒ realized” is false.**

   Quoted text:

   > “Call an abstract datum 𝒟 GRAMMATICAL at the state if: every side slope exceeds \(w(\widehat\Phi)\) … both endpoints of every side carry nonzero digits … the rightmost side ends at \((\mu,\mathrm{old}(\mu))\) with lead \(=\mathrm{vtx}(\nu_k)\) …”

   These conditions do not require that the listed sides form one contiguous lower-convex polygon. Missing conditions include compatible shared vertices, absence of gaps or overlaps between successive spans, the required ordering of slopes, and the non-rightmost extension condition corresponding to (NA).

   Consequently, an abstract list of individually legal sides can satisfy every listed “grammatical” clause while being impossible as a lower hull. The compactness/freshness proof cannot turn such incompatible side data into an \(f\). This invalidates the asserted per-leaf realized witness.

2. **CRITICAL ERROR — Lemma E is not exhaustive for the same reason.**

   Quoted text:

   > “Each clause names its witness fact; no further case exists.”

   The listed empty cases cover bad slope, unattainable digit, zero endpoint, bad rightmost endpoint/(HV), and bad factorization. They omit malformed multi-side topology and failure of (NA)/convex compatibility. Thus “non-grammatical ⇒ empty” may be true semantically, but the displayed case list does not prove it, and the assertion “no further case exists” is false.

   Findings 1–2 mean the required “each leaf realized or empty” classification is not discharged.

3. **CRITICAL ERROR — O1 is blocking for the claimed full outcome case tree.**

   Quoted text:

   > “The accepted sources pin no verdict list…”

   and:

   > “The verdict list must be pinned by the assembly … an interface obligation, not a gap in the partition itself.”

   The supplied kernel statement requires value cells containing:

   > “all polygon/digit values AND all terminal verdicts”

   and requires every input to produce either a read datum or one terminal verdict. The derivation defines the structural no-side status \((ns,\mu)\), root Hensel leaves, and branch factor data, but it does not define the general terminal-verdict function or prove that every non-continuing configuration receives exactly one verdict.

   Therefore O1 is genuinely non-blocking only for the narrower structural map “polygon + digits + factor multisets + no-side status.” It is blocking for Theorem B as advertised as the full next-outcome/terminal partition and for adoption as a discharge of L5.

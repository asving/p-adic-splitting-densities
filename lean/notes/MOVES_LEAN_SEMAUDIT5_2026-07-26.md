## 1. `locT` is a unit—but not yet the accepted localization unit

`locT` is unquestionably an actual unit of the concrete formal ring

```lean
LTwo K = LaurentPolynomial (LaurentPolynomial K).
```

And

```lean
grRes f * T (-w f) = C (R f)
```

is genuinely definitional.

The problem is that

```lean
grRes f := C (σ.R f) * T (σ.w f)
```

defines the purported initial form from the residual output. It does not construct:

- `gr_w(A)`;
- the genuine initial form `[f]`;
- the homogeneous localization of that graded ring;
- the degree-one unit in that localization;
- an isomorphism carrying `[f]` to `grRes f`.

D.1(e) only supplies the presentation `L ≅ L₀[T±¹]` after the genuine `L` and its unit `T` exist. It does not authorize constructing `[f]` backwards from an arbitrary pair `(w,R)`.

Consequently `grRes_mul_T_neg` proves normalization for the object engineered to satisfy normalization. It does not prove that the accepted residual is normalized by a genuine localized initial form.

`TvecUnitLaw` is therefore a substantially better formal-output equation than round 4’s two-evaluation shadow, but it still states the product law inside the presented residual codomain, not inside the accepted graded localization.

## 2. `coeffLocSR`: correct formal image, missing source provenance

Within `LTwo`, the first two clauses of `CoeffLocLaw` correctly say

```text
coeffLocSR ∩ outer-degree-zero = K
```

in both directions. This is a meaningful improvement:

- the subring includes sums and products;
- representative inverses are explicitly generated;
- the test is not merely equality between two residual outputs.

The `z` clause also pins a genuine formal monomial vector to the inner Laurent variable.

However, `coeffLocSR` is only the subring generated by `grRes` images in the formal presentation. Since `grRes` has not been shown to arise from the genuine graded localization, this is not yet the coefficient localization required by S4. No source localization or induced injective localization map exists.

There is also no explicit S4 exponent-group assertion in `CoeffLocLaw`; its cyclicity is inferred from the Laurent codomain. Transferring that conclusion back to the genuine exponent group requires precisely the localization embedding deferred to the future campaign. The integral tie laws alone do not construct that localization embedding.

Thus the degree-zero equality is correct inside the formal model, but the claim that this is the unflattened coefficient localization is not established.

## 3. Laurent carry transport is genuinely repaired

This demand is satisfied.

`IsCarryChainL` implements the correct recursion:

```text
c_j + carry_j = C_j + ψ·carry_{j+1},
```

where:

- slot terms are Laurent polynomials;
- carries are Laurent polynomials;
- canonical digits are polynomials of degree `< deg ψ`;
- negative powers are not coerced away slot-by-slot.

`LandingCylinderL` uses exactly the anchored terms

```text
z^(j·m̂-a) R(B_j)
```

on the minimizing locus. Non-minimizing terms and terms past the development window are zero.

The terminal clause

```lean
∀ j ≥ N, carry j = 0
```

removes the round-4 overflow defect. Together with `cL j = 0` for `j ≥ N`, the recursion also forces `Cdig j = 0` there, so the represented development is exact.

The old polynomial `%ₘ`/`/ₘ` mechanism remains in the imported file but is no longer consumed by the round-5 landing statement. The retired polynomial-division locus is therefore fully gone from this unit.

## 5. Transition coherence

`TransitionCoreL.child_dig_frame` repairs the specific uniform-orbit defect affecting `TRANSviii_a`: `mfun` is chosen before `B`, and depends only on the parent weight. Hence one fixed index has one fixed frame exponent before the orbit scalar varies.

It does not, however, repair `TRANSvi`. Neither `child_Tvec` nor `child_dig_frame` implies:

In particular, `mfun` has no stated additive compatibility or normalization tying the aggregate transported product to `locT`. `L4_TRANSvi` continues to infer global child laws from records that do not contain or entail them.

## Numbered findings

1. **CRITICAL — the class-(TRI) H-LIST proof is false as written.**

   The failing step is:

   > “On one cell … the least admissible value … is \(\phi^\uparrow_r(h_{<j})\), affine…”

   Taking `M` to be the lcm of denominators does not make this true under increments by `M`. A class-(TRI) example is
   \[
   y\ge x/2+1
   \]
   with no upper bound and one lower bound. On the residue cell `x≡y≡0 mod 2`, the least admissible even `y` for `x=0,2,4,6,…` is `2,2,4,4,…`, not an affine function of the cell parameter. Thus the claimed triangular affine map, period vectors, and free linear-set representation do not follow. Consequently the general class-(TRI) portions of XHD-d and XHD-s are not derived.

2. **GAP — H.1.2(ii-a) overstates own-coordinate positivity.**

   The phrase

   > “the … coefficient … is a positive lattice-crossing count (≥ 1)”

   is not established by “one level per `e·STR` steps.” What is justified is positivity over admissible lattice movement. The per-period E-POS formulation is the correct convergence condition and remains properly fenced, so this does not invalidate the E-POS retype.

3. **GAP — the gate’s component and period bookkeeping is internally inconsistent.**

   The official displayed H-LIST output is two disjoint components:
   \[
   L((1,3);\{(2,4),(0,2)\})\sqcup
   L((2,5);\{(2,4),(0,2)\}),

   separated by `h₀` parity. Yet L4 tests translation by `(1,2)`, which moves `(1,3)` to `(2,5)` and therefore crosses those displayed components. The note simultaneously says “ONE component class” and that XHD-u-X is untested. Relative to the displayed two-component partition, the `(1,2)` test is cross-component, not a per-component M4b-T period test. The numerical seal remains correct, but its claimed kernel attribution/coverage is not coherent.

## B. Shallow routing and the solve/tree-series identity

The shallow repair is structurally sound.

Trace a complete tree through the new three-clause interface:

1. Start at the empty prefix. Its root/shared shallow cells belong to a unique shallow tree shape \(\widehat S\).

2. Every split encountered before a block entrance is internal to \(\widehat S\). Its shared prefix factor is included once in \(W_{\widehat S}\), and its sibling continuations are multiplied using SIB’s count/mass face. No child is inserted linearly into \(\iota\).

3. A shallow \(\tau\)-halt contributes to \(\sigma_0(\widehat S)\). Each nonhalted shallow frontier member supplies one individuated block entrance
   \[
   (e_i,\tau_i,\delta_i).
   \]

4. PCI identifies that frontier continuation with the corresponding conditional value
   \[
   \beta_{e_i,\tau_i}(\sigma_i)(q^{\delta_i}).
   \]

5. Inside that block, RS.1-DEEP solves
   \[
   \beta_e=(I-K_e)^{-1}(b_e^{\rm term}+b_e^{\rm split}),
   \]
   with every later split handled by \(b_e^{\rm split}\).

6. RS.1-SH then assembles the root object:
   \[
   W_{\widehat S}
   \prod_i\beta_{e_i,\tau_i}(\sigma_i),
   \]
   followed by the multiset convolution and the sum over \(\widehat S\).

Thus a shallow split contributes a product at RS.1-SH; a deep split contributes a product at \(b_e^{\rm split}\); neither passes through \(\iota^T\beta\). The pairing \(\iota^T\beta\) remains a marked diagnostic/COMP object only. The pass-9 counterexample
\[
\iota_{e_1}^T\beta_{e_1}+\iota_{e_2}^T\beta_{e_2}
\]
is now excluded by the stated construction.

The eventual RS.1 proof must still establish that the shallow-cut convention gives an exhaustive, nonoverlapping decomposition of every complete tree and that every \(W_{\widehat S}\) has the claimed rational construction. Those are genuine proof duties, but the required operation and routing are now present.

### SIB and induction depth

The tree-indexed SIB statement is strong enough. It quantifies directly over prescribed finite subtree events at every realized branching prefix. Structural induction on a finite tree therefore applies SIB at the first split and invokes the induction hypothesis separately on each strictly smaller child subtree. There is no need to infer arbitrary subtree independence from one-step independence, so the former hidden-induction risk is gone.

Depth-2 gating is also an honest evidentiary posture. It tests that the implementation really handles subtree events rather than only next outcomes. It does not prove the unbounded quantifier, and REV 10 explicitly records depth \(\ge3\) as derivation-only. That is the correct distinction between gate evidence and theorem proof.

Verdict: NOT ACCEPTED. The note is candid about several open obligations, but TREE-EXP also has a structural missing factor that is not merely conditional on (SIB).

## Findings

1. **CRITICAL — §T.1 does not completely discharge (c1)–(c3).**

   The (c1), (c2), and (c3-a) analysis is sound, conditional on Theorem B and the stated certificate theorems. In particular, the domain stratification correctly separates:

   - root simple factors,
   - nonempty branch sets with saturated/continuing branches,
   - empty branch sets/no-side status.

   But the claim that the residual interface is discharged is false at full strength. The note itself says:

   > “TB-CAP(ns): the literal duty FAILS”

   The derivation requires every halt decision to be finite-cap detectable. Replacing that with cap-approximability plus conjectural nullity is a new, weaker interface, not a discharge of (c3-b). Moreover, `(ns-null)` and VP-SOUND remain open. Thus §T.1 is sound as an interface analysis but incomplete as a discharge.

2. **GAP — TB-CAP(irr) is not obtained from §C C.2 exactly as cited.**

   The note identifies the verdict class with

   > `S(η,⊤) ∩ Ψ_η^{-1}(c)`.

   There are two problems:

   - The indexing is off: in §D4-R, `Σ_k` is the state after the prefix `η`; a next digit cell is a cell of the next read, not “the digit-value cell of the read at ν_k.”
   - C.2 prices one branch-history system. A full Theorem-B cell can specify the complete joint polygon and digits of several sides, while a single next-node stratum is explicitly only a union of such cells. C.2 alone therefore does not prove that the whole joint cell condition is represented by the asserted branch system.

   Theorem B(iii) does give finite-cap measurability for an ordinary case-R cell, so the desired conclusion is plausible, but the displayed derivation from “C.2 + Scale” is incomplete.

3. **GAP — the strict pro-cylinder assertion is stronger than the cited result.**

   The note says:

   > “every \(L_M\) properly contains \(L_{M+1}\) on a realized state (S2.5...)”

   The cited S2.5 construction proves that all finite truncations are nonempty and that their intersection is nonempty. It does not prove that every successive cap introduces a genuinely new free coordinate, nor that every consecutive inclusion is proper. That is essentially the same all-depth freeness issue later admitted open in `(ns-null)`.

   The pro-cylinder description and non-finite detectability are credible, but the stated strict-decrease proof is not in the citation.

4. **CRITICAL — `(ns-null)` is asserted rather than proved, and it is indispensable.**

   The note correctly admits:

   > “its ALL-DEPTH quantifier ... is written out nowhere in accepted text: an OPEN sub-obligation”

   Without that quantifier, the estimate  
   \(\mu(L_M)\le p^{-(M-M_0)}\mu(L_{M_0})\) is unavailable. Consequently:

   - no-side classes have not been proved null;
   - no-side trees cannot yet be deleted from the density sum;
   - the amended TB-CAP interface has not been established.

   If `(ns-null)` is eventually proved, then mass zero is enough for the density target: a measurable verdict class of mass zero may be omitted from the normalized density. The issue is proof, not sufficiency.

5. **CRITICAL — the no-side finite-count bound has the wrong limiting quantity.**

   Twice the note writes, in substance,

   > `#{f mod p^N : ...} ≤ p^{nN}·μ(L_{M(N)}) → 0`.

   Even if \(\mu(L_{M(N)})\to0\), the raw count  
   \(p^{nN}\mu(L_{M(N)})\) need not tend to zero. With only one new zero digit per level it can grow like \(p^{(n-1)N}\).

   What tends to zero, conditional on `(ns-null)`, is the normalized count
   \[
   p^{-nN}\#\{f\bmod p^N:\cdots\}.
   \]
   Thus the displayed consequence in §T.2 and its repetition in TREE-N are false as written.

6. **GAP — VP-SOUND is only a citation placeholder.**

   The Hensel conclusion itself is correct for every prime, including wild primes: a simple irreducible factor of \(\bar f\) is coprime to the complementary factor and lifts uniquely to an irreducible unramified factor. Hence `N=1` for `(τ-hen)` is correct.

   But the note gives no precise declaration of either:

   - the Hensel factor-lifting statement being consumed, or
   - the Ore–Montes saturation theorem with the exact hypotheses matching the accumulated \(E,F,\mu\) notation.

   Therefore VP-SOUND is honestly statused, but not discharged.

7. **CRITICAL — TREE-EXP omits the mass of the joint branching cell.**

   For a shared prefix event \(P\), a first branching cell \(c\), and two child subtree events \(S_1,S_2\), the correct decomposition is
   \[
   \mu(F_T)
   =\mu(P)\Pr(c\mid P)
      \Pr(S_1\cap S_2\mid P,c).
   \]
   Given (SIB), this becomes
   \[
   \mu(F_T)
   =\mu(P)\Pr(c\mid P)
      \Pr(S_1\mid P,c)\Pr(S_2\mid P,c).
   \]

   The shared prefix is indeed charged once, and the child continuations are correctly multiplied. But neither §C nor (SIB) supplies the missing factor \(\Pr(c\mid P)\) in the displayed node product:

   - §C prices one branch history.
   - §D4-R explicitly says a next-node stratum can be a union of full joint cells.
   - (SIB) is conditional on `Σ_c`; it does not price entry into `c`.

   The proof writes the correct unexplained factor
   \[
   \mu(S(\eta_0,\top)\cap\Psi^{-1}(c)),
   \]
   but then silently replaces it by products of single-node fresh volumes. That replacement is not established.

8. **CRITICAL — the root/Hensel base case already contradicts TREE-EXP.**

   Consider degree one and a fixed root verdict tree corresponding to  
   \(\bar f=x-a\). Its fiber has mass \(p^{-1}\).

   Under `(τ-hen)`, no window read is opened. Therefore the product

   > “over ALL nodes ... of `vol(E_fresh(ν))`”

   either has no defined root factor or is the empty product \(1\). The prose

   > “the level-0 datum is one digit layer, its cells partition”

   does not insert its mass into TREE-EXP.

   More generally, a fixed root reduction-factorization cell has a nontrivial mass that must be charged once. Multiplying separate track events is not justified merely because the root cells partition. This is the root instance of Finding 7.

9. **CRITICAL — the no-side version of TREE-EXP has an undefined/incorrect right-hand side.**

   TREE-EXP says:

   > “for (ns)-leaf trees both sides are 0.”

   But every displayed `vol(E_fresh(ν))` is a positive finite-digit factor. No zero-volume factor is defined for the infinite no-side condition. Thus the product on the right is positive—or is undefined if the no-side terminal is not a §C node—not zero.

   Nullity of the fiber does not by itself turn a finite product of positive fresh volumes into zero. Hence the stated single formula cannot include no-side trees.

10. **GAP — the equal-shape multinomial is not automatic.**

   The member-indexing convention is correct: equal-shape siblings remain distinct cell members, and no symmetry factor belongs in a member-indexed product.

   But the assertion that a shape-level factorization necessarily contributes
   \[
   \frac{m!}{m_1!\cdots m_k!}
   \]
   requires exchangeability: every assignment of the child shapes to the individuated branches must be realizable with the same count or weight. Cell membership alone does not imply that. Different residual factors can have different child states and different available continuations.

   The literal count \(C_{\widehat T}(p)\) remains meaningful, but the advertised multinomial factorization needs an additional symmetry/equivariance theorem.

11. **CRITICAL — §T.3 is strong enough as a hypothesis, but `(SIB-STEP)` is not precise enough to derive it.**

   The tree-indexed (SIB) statement itself has exactly the strength TREE-EXP would need: arbitrary prescribed finite subtree events, at every branching prefix, conditional on the full cell.

   The proposed step law says each sibling’s next read is uniform conditional on the “entire joint past.” As written, this does not imply joint uniformity. Two contemporaneous random variables can each be uniform conditional on the previous-stage past while being identical to one another.

   To imply (SIB), the statement would need either:

   - conditional uniformity of the stacked next-read vector, or
   - a specified sequential filtration in which earlier siblings’ current-stage reads enter the conditioning before later siblings are sampled.

   Neither ordering nor stacked-vector assertion appears in `(SIB-STEP)`. Therefore the claimed “mechanical lemma” is not currently derivable.

   The note is otherwise honest that §C stops at per-path marginal uniformity and that the cross-sibling unimodularity theorem is open.

12. **GAP — the mechanism discussion contains two overstatements.**

   First, the displays
   \[
   |\operatorname{Res}(g,h)|_2=2^{-2},\qquad |f'(\alpha_j)|_5=5^{-2}
   \]
   are not “Jacobian units”: these quantities have positive valuation. What the gate exhibits is a Jacobian with constant forced valuation, after conditioning/normalization—not literally a unit determinant.

   Second, a pass at the listed cells does not mathematically bound every possible failure to “depth ≥ 3 / \(n\ge5\).” It excludes the sealed families only. An untested cell of the same degree/depth could still fail unless a classification theorem reduces all such cells to the sealed representatives.

13. **GAP — ONE-F is valid, but it is not derived from the recursion.**

   The identity
   \[
   \sum_{\widehat T}
   1_{\{\operatorname{shape}(T_{\rm can}^\tau(f))=\widehat T\}}=1
   \]
   follows directly from choice-freeness of \(T_{\rm can}^\tau\). It does not use TREE-EXP and remains true even if TREE-EXP is false.

   The marked-prefix dictionary is also correct when written with the unpruned \(T_{\rm can}\). But this creates a scope issue: D4R marked histories may include syntactic below-halt continuations, while the density tree is the pruned \(T_{\rm can}^\tau\). The claim that every marked/FAM quantity converts through finite pruned tree fibers needs an explicit restriction to pre-halt marks, or an accounting of below-halt marks.

14. **CRITICAL — T-n3’s attribution scheme cannot isolate TREE-EXP from (SIB).**

   Layer A’s recursion-side identity
   \[
   N(F_T)N(\Sigma_c)=N(S_1\cap\Sigma_c)N(S_2\cap\Sigma_c)
   \]
   is itself the relevant (SIB) product identity plus the set-theoretic identification \(F_T=S_1\cap S_2\) after pruning.

   Layer B introduces a new depth-2 subtree event outside the passed SIB gate’s tested scope. Consequently, if B fails while the old word laws remain intact, the failure may still be a failure of tree-indexed (SIB) for the new event. It cannot automatically be attributed to TREE-EXP or VP.

   The gate also leaves Layer B’s continuing cell, next outcome, and sufficient box unspecified, and Layer C gives no sealed numerical prediction. Those predictions are not yet referee-derivable from §T-ASSEMBLY alone. The statement that T-n3 has not run is honest, but the gate is not yet a coherent sealed discriminator.

## Section verdicts

- **§T.1:** (c1), (c2), and (c3-a) are sound; the complete-discharge claim is false because (c3-b), `(ns-null)`, and VP-SOUND remain unresolved.
- **§T.2:** `(τ-hen)` with \(N=1\) is correct at all primes. `(τ-irr)` is plausible but its cited proof is incomplete. `(τ-ns)` is correctly recognized as non-cap-detectable, but strict pro-cylinder behavior and nullity are not proved; the raw-count limit is false.
- **§T.3:** tree-indexed (SIB) has the needed strength and the derivation stop is mostly honest. `(SIB-STEP)` is insufficiently specified, and the gate evidence is overstated in scope.
- **§T.4:** the shared prefix is conceptually charged once and node-count induction is formally well-founded for a correctly stated recursion. But the joint-cell/root mass is missing, the no-side product is invalid, and the multinomial is unsupported. ONE-F itself is valid independently.
- **§T.5/§T.6:** the perimeter is unusually candid, but the acceptance package is still open. T-n3’s attribution and prediction scheme are not yet adequate.

**Final verdict: NOT ACCEPTED.**

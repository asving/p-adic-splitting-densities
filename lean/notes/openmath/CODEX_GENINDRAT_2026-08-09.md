**Classification: JUSTIFICATION GAP.**

**Exact failure.** “Triangular” does not by itself imply surjectivity or constant fibers over truncated DVRs; the pivot maps must be shown to be units, or their invariant factors must be fixed independently of the target point. For example, modulo \(\pi^2\), the triangular map
\[
(x,y)\longmapsto (x,(x+\pi)y)
\]
is bijective in \(y\) when \(x\) is a unit, but for \(x=0\) its image is only \(\pi O/\pi^2\), with different fiber behavior. The GENIND-6 proof supplies neither a general inverse nor a fixed-pivot/Smith-normal-form calculation for the simultaneous child and ragged-cofactor coordinates. It only gives the M6 ledger. In particular, no displayed argument proves that arbitrary choices in all capped child windows and all per-coefficient stage windows can be lifted simultaneously, or that the number of lifts is independent of those choices.

**Repair need.** A repair must give a per-genre precision matrix or explicit inverse proving simultaneous surjectivity and base-point-independent invariant factors at the claimed capped and ragged windows.

> “\(r(N)/q^{nN}\to0\)”

**Classification: JUSTIFICATION GAP.**

**Exact failure.** An \(o(1)\) remainder does not imply the asserted exponential rate \(O(\operatorname{poly}(M)Q^{-M})\); for example \(1/\log(M+1)\to0\) but has no such bound. Calling the complement “exact” supplies its value, not a rate or an admissible finite geometric representation. Moreover, capped child windows are \(M_l=N-D_l\): they can remain bounded while \(D_l\) grows with \(N\), so one cannot simply apply the child’s \(M_l\to\infty\) limit. Such terms require a separate estimate from the opening prefactor, with a split between bounded and growing \(M_l\). No such split or summation is displayed. Consequently the asserted telescoping to Step 4’s \(r(N)/q^{nN}\to0\) has not been derived from the hypotheses of GENIND.B.

**Repair need.** A repair must either strengthen \(P(k)\) with a proved quantitative remainder class stable under capped shifts, or explicitly estimate the bounded-\(M_l\) and growing-\(M_l\) beta regimes, including their route prefactors.

4. PE8’s stated reason that exact child counts are necessary is mathematically false

> “replace \(u_i\) by a bound and the alternating sum is neither a bound nor a value: the \(r=2\) case \(u_1T_2 + T_1u_2 - u_1u_2\) is already not monotone in \(u_1\) once \(u_2 > T_2-u_2\)”

**Classification: JUSTIFICATION GAP.**

**Exact failure.** For
\[
F(u_1,u_2)=u_1T_2+T_1u_2-u_1u_2,
\]
\[
\frac{\partial F}{\partial u_1}=T_2-u_2\ge0
\]
throughout the counting range \(0\le u_2\le T_2\). Similarly \(\partial F/\partial u_2=T_1-u_1\ge0\). Thus the claimed non-monotonicity condition is wrong. More generally,
\[
1-\prod_i(1-u_i/T_i)
\]
is coordinatewise increasing. Upper bounds can therefore prove drainage directly, and the elementary union bound
\[
\#\{\text{some child bad}\}\le
\sum_i u_i\prod_{j\ne i}T_j
\]
requires no exact inclusion–exclusion. Exact \(u_i\) values are needed only if the stronger package demands an exact complement formula; they are not logically necessary for W-12’s (A2), which asks only for vanishing.

The numerical identity itself is correct:
\[
16^3-12^3=2368
\]
and
\[
3\cdot4\cdot256-3\cdot16\cdot16+64=2368.
\]

**Repair need.** A repair must separate the valid exact inclusion–exclusion identity from the distinct claim that exactness is necessary, and state precisely which strengthened output beyond (A2) requires exact values.

5. The CS4-F locus law is stated without its necessary visibility floor

> “**CS4-F(k)** … integer slope \(k\), heights \((4k,3k,2k,k)\), \(L=4\):
> \[
> (q(q-1)/2)\cdot q^{4N-10k-4}.
> \]”

The same unqualified law also appears in GENIND.A(IV).

**Classification: JUSTIFICATION GAP.**

**Exact failure.** DRAIN fires first unless the constant coefficient’s pinned height satisfies
\[
4k\le N-1.
\]
Without that restriction the locus is empty, while the displayed expression can even be nonintegral. At \(q=2,N=2,k=1\), the true locus is \(0\), but the formula returns
\[
\frac{2(1)}2\,2^{8-10-4}=2^{-6}.
\]
The neighboring CS4-E law explicitly carries its visibility condition, so it cannot be inferred that all displays silently include one.

**Repair need.** A repair must attach \(4k\le N-1\) to every asserted CS4-F locus law and exclude the family outside that range.

6. The proof of the claimed “\(m\ge4\)” converse establishes only the degree-\(n\) existence statement

> “a CS event at a stage with \(m\) keys requires … \(m\ge4\); conversely \(m\ge4\) realizes CS with positive locus at every \(q\)”

but the sufficiency proof says

> “At \(d=1,m=4\): the QRT-G2 … stratum exists …; at \(n>4\) pad with ONE monic irreducible of degree \(n-4\) at level 0”

**Classification: JUSTIFICATION GAP.**

**Exact failure.** Padding at level 0 produces a degree-\(n\) polynomial having a four-key CS branch plus a separate decided branch. It proves “degree \(n\ge4\) admits some CS stage,” but it does not prove the preceding converse that a stage carrying an arbitrary prescribed number \(m>4\) of keys itself realizes CS. In the padded construction the CS stage still carries four keys.

The degree criterion “CS occurs in some degree-\(n\) read iff \(n\ge4\)” is nevertheless supported by this construction.

**Repair need.** A repair must either weaken the converse to the degree-level existential statement actually proved, or construct a positive CS locus inside every \(m\)-key cluster system for \(m\ge4\).

## Checked clean

- **Five-way event disjointness and completeness.** After DRAIN is removed, unique factorization of every side residual gives the correct priority:

  - no repeated factor: DEC;
  - some repeated factor with \(e\deg\psi\ge2\): CS;
  - otherwise every repeated factor is linear on an \(e=1\) side, giving \(\alpha\) exactly for the trivial one-block partition and \(\beta\) otherwise.

  Thus a repeated linear residual on an \(e\ge2\) side is CS, not \(\alpha\); an \(e=1\) repeated nonlinear factor is CS; mixed polygons are CS by the existential quantifier; polygons with only separable residuals are DEC. No orphan or double membership was found.

- **DRAIN-first polygon exactness.** If \(v(a_0)\le N-1\), the chord from \((0,v(a_0))\) to \((m,0)\) has height \(<N\) at every \(j<m\). Hence coefficients hidden at valuation \(\ge N\) lie strictly above every possible lower-hull segment. This remains valid at \(v(a_0)=N-1\).

- **Alpha locus count in small characteristic.** For \(q=2,m=2,N=3,k=1\), the formula gives
  \[
  (2-1)2^{2(2)-3}=2.
  \]
  Indeed the \(a_1\) residual pin is zero in characteristic \(2\), so \(v(a_1)\ge2\), giving two choices modulo \(2^3\); \(a_0\) has its unique nonzero digit at height \(2\). For \(q=2,m=3,N=4,k=1\), the formula gives \(2^{9-6}=8\), matching \(4\cdot2\cdot1\) choices in \(a_2,a_1,a_0\).

- **Alpha bracket arithmetic.** At \(m=2,Q=2,\mu=2\), the histories \((2)\) and \((1,1)\) contribute \(4\) and \(4\), respectively; total \(8=(Q-1)Q^{2\mu-1}\).

- **Alpha/DEC boundary at small windows.** The \(\alpha\) visibility condition \(mk\le N-1\) ensures its constant coefficient is strictly in-window, so it cannot also be DRAIN. Zero binomial coefficients in small characteristic change a pin to a forced zero digit but do not merge different \(z\)-slices.

- **Degree descent at beta fractures.** Every continuing child has \(\mu_l\ge2\) and \(\mu_l<m\), hence
  \[
  \deg(\text{child})=\mu_l d<md.
  \]
  Repeated partitions such as \(m=4\mapsto(2,2)\) therefore cause no failure of strong induction.

- **Capped child windows cannot be negative or exceed the parent window.** At a non-DRAIN frame,
  \[
  D_l=\min_j(v(a_j)+jk_l)\le v(a_0)\le N-1,
  \]
  while \(D_l\ge\mu_lk_l\ge1\). Thus
  \[
  1\le M_l=N-D_l\le N-1.
  \]
  The only scope problem is the unhandled endpoint \(M_l=1\), recorded above.

- **Composite-stage minimum length.** A repeated factor has horizontal consumption
  \[
  e\mu\deg\psi\ge4
  \]
  in both cases \(e\ge2,\deg\psi=1\) and \(e=1,\deg\psi\ge2\). Hence no CS event can occur with fewer than four keys.

- **Displayed flagship exponents.** The CS4-F exponent is arithmetically correct on its proper domain:
  \[
  \sum_{j=0}^{3}\bigl(N-1-(4-j)k\bigr)=4N-10k-4.
  \]
  The defect is only the omitted domain. The CS5-V1E2 exponent also recomputes to \(4N-5h-3\), with the separate \(a_0\)-headroom factor as displayed.

- **GENIND-4 triangular expansion.**
  Expanding
  \[
  (x^2-s)^2+(\alpha_1x+\alpha_0)(x^2-s)+(\beta_1x+\beta_0)
  \]
  gives exactly
  \[
  b_3=\alpha_1,\quad b_2=\alpha_0-2s,\quad
  b_1=\beta_1-s\alpha_1,\quad
  b_0=\beta_0-s\alpha_0+s^2.
  \]
  Its free-slot total is \(4N-5h-3\), and the parity computation gives the node \((4h+1,2h+1)\).

- **Set-theoretic inclusion–exclusion.** Assuming the advertised product fibration and conjunction rule, the formula
  \[
  \prod_iT_i-\prod_i(T_i-u_i)
  \]
  is correct. The finding concerns the false claim about monotonicity and necessity of exactness, not this identity.

## Verdict

GENIND does **not** survive this ratification pass as CLEAN. I found no counterexample to the five-event partition, the alpha laws, beta degree descent, or the central degree-\(\ge4\) CS criterion. However, the general mixed-opening fibration—the bridge needed for mixed CS/child genres—is still justified only by the word “triangular” plus one worked genre, and the A2 proof invokes a quantitative child-drain rate not supplied by the stated induction hypothesis. Those are central proof surfaces, not cosmetic omissions. The window-\(1\) scope leak and the unqualified CS4-F law are concrete additional defects, while PE8’s claimed non-monotonicity is simply false. These findings materially challenge the prior acceptance, though they do not presently furnish a counterexample to the intended conditional theorem.

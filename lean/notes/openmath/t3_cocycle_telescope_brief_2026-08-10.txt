== BACKGROUND (2 paragraphs) ==

The project proves density statements for splitting types of degree-n polynomials
over p-adic fields via an Okutsu–Montes/MacLane-style tower calculus. A "tower"
is a chain of types with stage data (e_i, f_i, u_i) at each level: e_i =
ramification jump, f_i = residue jump, u_i = the dv_i-height of the next key
polynomial; level-1 data (e_1, f_1, h) with slope h/e_1 in lowest terms, base

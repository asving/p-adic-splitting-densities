1. **CRITICAL — the matching definition presupposes part of the sought coincidence.**

   The note first says the reconstructed classifier chooses its own keys:

   > “keys chosen in \(O_\delta[x]\), residue fields RECONSTRUCTED over \(F_q\)”

   but then defines a matched position by:

   > “same prefix keys (via the inclusion \(\mathbb Z_p[x]\subseteq O_\delta[x]\))”

   The reconstructed classifier’s independently chosen \(O_\delta\)-keys have not been shown to equal the transported \(\mathbb Z_p\)-keys. Making “same prefix keys” part of the definition therefore builds a key-level coincidence into “matched position.” This blurs the independently reconstructed tower with the comparison device and makes the subsequent coincidence argument circular.

2. **CRITICAL — DICT-1’s main implication does not follow from its two named inputs.**

   The crucial assertion is:

   > “The reconstructed tower … is an OM chain realizing the same inductive valuation (same input, same position, and the classifier's read is determined by the valuation data).”

   Neither [IN-R1] nor [IN-U] establishes that the reconstructed chain and comparison chain realize the same valuation. As [IN-U] is itself stated, it applies only to:

   > “two chains realizing the same inductive valuation”

   Thus the proof assumes the premise needed to invoke uniqueness. “Same input” and “same position” do not imply equality of inductive valuations.

   There is a second mismatch: [IN-U] promises “isomorphic residue towers,” while DICT-1(3) demands literal embedded-field equalities

   > “\(F_j^{(\delta)}=F_j\cdot F_q\)”

   No compatibility argument upgrades an abstract isomorphism to equality inside \(\overline{\mathbb F}_p\).

   Finally, the reverse implication invokes a third input:

   > “where it factors into gcd > 1 conjugate factors … ((F1), §3)”

   so DICT-1 is not proved from exactly the two advertised named inputs even at the prose level.

3. **CRITICAL — DICT-1 is not a proved Lean lemma; its supposed Lean content is assumed in structure rows.**

   The note claims:

   > “the coincidence-at-stable-prefixes display proved as DICT-1”

   There is no Lean definition of the three DICT-1 clauses and no Lean theorem proving their equivalence. Instead, `GcdSlice` assumes:

   > `coincide : ∀ ... M.splitFree δ x → M.pdata δ x = M.pdata 1 x`

   and:

   > `l4_persist : ∀ ... M.splitFree δ x ↔ persistsFrom ...`

   These are hypotheses supplied by every `GcdSlice`, not consequences proved by this note. Consequently, neither direction of the claimed “exactly at prefix-stable positions” biconditional is established in Lean.

4. **CRITICAL — the Lean “coincidence” row is drastically weaker and can be vacuous.**

   DICT-1(3) claims equality of stage tuples, valuations on \(O_\delta[x]\), and embedded residue fields. Its alleged Lean shadow is merely:

   > `M.pdata δ x = M.pdata 1 x`

   But `DictModel.Data` is an unconstrained `Type`, and `pdata` is an unconstrained function. One may take `Data` to be a singleton and `pdata` constant; then `coincide` is automatic without any tower, valuation, residue field, or stage data existing.

   Comments saying that `Data` encodes those objects do not impose that property in Lean. This is precisely a trivializable carrier and does not state what the prose claims.

5. **CRITICAL — `stable_iff_R4` uses a third substantive hypothesis, contrary to “exactly two.”**

   `DictModel` contains:

   > `splitFree_one : ∀ x : Pos, splitFree 1 x`

   and the proof first invokes:

   > `rw [M.posLetterD_one]`

   where `posLetterD_one` is proved using `M.splitFree_one x`.

   Therefore `stable_iff_R4` depends on `splitFree_one` in addition to `henc` and `hoff`. This dependency is logically essential: if both the \(\delta\)-prefix and the base prefix are declared non-split-free, both letters equal `offLetter`, so `Stable` holds while `R4` fails.

   Moreover, `henc` and `hoff` remain assumptions in `DictModel`; the generic helper lemmas do not derive them for any actual dictionary model. The corpus wiring is syntactically genuine—`AlphabetData.Stable` is unfolded—but it additionally assumes `Encodes`, and it connects corpus stability only to the note’s newly defined, semantically unconstrained `DictModel.R4`.

6. **GAP — Lemma 4’s arithmetic skeleton is proved, but the finite-field theorem is assumed wholesale.**

   The unconditional theorem `lemma4_arith` correctly proves an equivalence for the recursively defined predicate `persistsFrom`. It does not prove that actual residual polynomials persist exactly when `persistsFrom` holds.

   That hard field-theoretic identification is the row:

   > “`l4_persist` is (F1)+(F2) read stagewise at the slice”

   and is assumed as a field of `GcdSlice`. Likewise, transport coincidence is assumed through `coincide`. The later theorems are therefore conditional on hypotheses containing the missing mathematical content.

   This displacement is displayed in the note, so it is a GAP rather than a silently consumed premise. It nevertheless contradicts the stronger summary claim that Lemma 4 has been “PROVED as a lemma of the dictionary.”

7. **GAP — the cited literature cannot support the load-bearing uses from the supplied material alone.**

   The note explicitly says of [IN-U]:

   > “exact printed theorem number is a LITUNIT-roster duty, not asserted here”

   The quoted uniqueness formulation is not enough to justify equality of valuations or literal equality of embedded residue fields. [IN-R1] is also explicitly open. The actual text of Lidl–Niederreiter Theorem 3.46 and the compositum-degree source is not supplied, so their attribution and printed generality cannot be checked, although the statements quoted as (F1)/(F2) are sufficient for the standalone arithmetic argument if assumed.

8. **GAP — the \(F_9\) arithmetic is correct, but the claimed dictionary/out-of-domain realization is not formalized.**

   The numerical re-derivation is correct:

   - \(z^2+1\) has no root over \(\mathbb F_3\), hence is irreducible.
   - \(m=1\cdot2=2\) and \(\gcd(2,2)=2\).
   - Over \(\mathbb F_9=\mathbb F_3[i]\), \(z^2+1=(z-i)(z+i)\), giving two degree-one factors.

   But the note admits:

   > “that \(z^2+1\) is irreducible over \(F_3\) — so that F9 IS the field \(F_9\) — is classical and stays prose”

   More importantly, no `DictModel`, `GcdSlice`, reconstructed classifier tower, or `AlphabetData` instance is constructed for this example. `f9_out_of_domain` proves only `¬ persistsFrom 2 1 [1,2]`; it does not prove `¬ splitFree`, failure of corpus `Stable`, or that an actual `posLetterD` returns the designated off-domain letter. The assertions about realized branch letters and alphabet dimensions also remain unsupported prose.

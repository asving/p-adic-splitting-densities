## 4. Lemma W-CAP (the ledger weight cap — ADM-U7 §2's statement, proof kept)

    W_max := max_𝐣 wt(𝐣) = Σ_{i=1}^{r} (e_if_i − 1)·w(φ_i)

any c — is ≤ V̂ − w(φ₁) < V̂.

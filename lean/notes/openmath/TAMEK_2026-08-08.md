## S1. Set-up: the statistic X_K and the embedding-count lemma TK-0

**Setting.** F a local field with ring O, uniformizer π, residue field F_q, p = char of
the residue field. Fix once and for all a Teichmüller generator ζ of μ_{q^f−1} inside
the unramified closure (so μ_{q^{f'}−1} = ⟨ζ^{(q^f−1)/(q^{f'}−1)}⟩ for f' | f).

**Tame fields and their coordinates (classical, re-derived here).** A tame extension
K/F of ramification index e (p ∤ e) and residue degree f, d := ef, has a UNIQUE
unramified subfield K₀ = F_f (residue field F_Q, Q := q^f), and K = K₀(β) with

      β^e = ζ_K^c · π ,     ζ_K := the Teichmüller generator of μ_{Q−1} ⊆ K₀,

for some c. (Existence: pick any val-1/e element, normalize its e-th power's unit part
to Teichmüller — possible since 1 + m_{K₀} is uniquely e-divisible when p ∤ e, Hensel.)
Two data (e, f, c) and (e, f, c′) give F-isomorphic fields iff c ≡ c′ mod
g_K := gcd(e, Q−1): the **twist class** c mod g_K is the LETTER of K — precisely the
GRTJC letter genre (the residual unit datum that the carry algebra 𝒜(T) = K[v]/(v^E − ζ_T)
carries); J-D0 proved stratum COUNTS blind to it. X_K below is the first statistic in
the corpus that reads it.

**The statistic.** For monic f ∈ O[x] of degree n with Haar-uniform coefficients,

      X_K(f) := #Hom_{F-alg}( F[x]/(f) , K ) = #{ roots of f in K } = #{ roots in O_K }

(roots of a monic integral polynomial are integral). Almost surely f is separable, so
X_K = Σ_{g | f irreducible} #Hom_F(L_g, K), L_g := F[x]/(g). K = F recovers the corpus's
X_n (BCFGTIE/MOMENTS). Projective model as in MOMENTS S1 (uniform degree-n effective
divisor on P¹; X_K^p counts K-points of the divisor, the point at ∞ contributing its
own root count).

### LEMMA TK-0 (the tame embedding count — derived from scratch)

Let K = (e, f, c) as above and L = (e', f', a) tame (so L = F_{f'}(α), α^{e'} = ζ_L^a π,
ζ_L = ζ^{(q^f−1)(q^{f'}−1)⁻¹-power} the Teichmüller generator of μ_{q^{f'}−1}). Set
g := gcd(e', Q−1). Then Hom_F(L, K) = ∅ unless e' | e and f' | f, and in that case

      #Hom_F(L, K) = g · #{ j ∈ {0, …, f'−1} :  ζ_K^{ a r q^j − c }  ∈ (μ_{Q−1})^{e'} } ,

where r := (Q−1)/(q^{f'}−1) (the index of μ_{q^{f'}−1} in μ_{Q−1}). Membership in
(μ_{Q−1})^{e'} — the index-g subgroup — is the congruence a r q^j ≡ c (mod g).
(Note the K-side letter enters only as c mod g — through gcd(e', Q−1), the E-torsion
the target can see at level e'.)

*Proof.* An F-algebra map ι: L → K is a field embedding. (Necessity) ι(F_{f'}) lies in
the maximal unramified subfield K₀, so f' | f; and v(ι(α)) = 1/e' must lie in the value
group (1/e)ℤ of K, so e' | e. (Count) ι is determined by (i) its restriction to F_{f'} —
an F-embedding F_{f'} ↪ F_f, of which there are exactly f' (the unramified extension is
Galois; the restriction is Frob^j∘(fixed inclusion), j = 0..f'−1) — and (ii) a root in K
of the minimal polynomial x^{e'} − σ_j(ζ_L^a)π = x^{e'} − ζ_L^{a q^j} π of α over
σ_j(F_{f'}) ⊆ K₀. Every element of K of valuation 1/e' is x = β^{e̅} w with w ∈ O_K^×,
and O_K^× = μ_{Q−1} × (1 + m_K) with 1 + m_K uniquely e'-divisible (p ∤ e'). So

      x^{e'} = ζ_L^{a q^j} π   with   x^{e'} = (β^{e̅} w)^{e'} = β^e w^{e'} = ζ_K^{c} π w^{e'}
      ⟺   w^{e'} = ζ_K^{ a r q^j − c }        (identifying ζ_L = ζ_K^r).

In the cyclic group μ_{Q−1} (order Q−1) the equation w₀^{e'} = t has solutions iff
t ∈ (μ_{Q−1})^{e'} — the index-g subgroup — and then exactly g of them; the (1+m_K)-part
contributes exactly one solution (unique e'-divisibility). Summing over j gives the
display. ∎

**Remarks.**
1. **K = L:** #Aut(L) = #Hom(L, L) = gcd(e', q^{f'}−1) · #{j : a(q^j − 1) ≡ 0 mod
   gcd(e', q^{f'}−1)} (set K = L in TK-0: r = 1, e̅ = 1, g = gcd(e', q^{f'}−1)); for
   f' = 1 this is just gcd(e', q−1).
2. **Subfield count.** #{subfields of K isomorphic to L} = #Hom(L, K)/#Aut(L)
   (Aut(L) acts freely transitively on embeddings with fixed image). This is the
   Cohen–Lenstra-flavored quantity that the mean law (S4) consumes.
3. **Letters gone at the total.** Σ_{a mod g₀} #Hom(L_a, K) over the g₀ := gcd(e', q^{f'}−1)
   twist classes of (e', f') equals f' e' · [e'|e][f'|f] · (g/g₀)·(g₀/g)-bookkeeping —
   concretely: for each j, the solvability condition a r q^j ≡ c e̅ (mod g) is solvable
   in a for exactly (q^{f'}−1)·gcd-many a's; the machine leg (T-battery, check family
   TK0-SUM) verifies the clean instance Σ_a #Hom(L_a, K) counted with multiplicity
   = #{x ∈ K : v(x) = 1/e', x^{e'} ∈ Teich(F_{f'})·π}-census. The mean-level consequence
   we actually use is displayed (and machine-checked) per-case in S3/S4.

## S2. The structural point: X_K is letter-sensitive; what the letters must do

**Why X_K reads letters.** A root of f in K is an irreducible factor g together with an
embedding L_g ↪ K. Wild factors never embed (K tame: every subfield of K is tame — a
sub-extension of a tame extension has p ∤ e' and its inertia is a subgroup of tame
inertia). So X_K reads exactly the TAME sub-menu of the factorization type, refined by
TK-0's congruence: e' | e AND f' | f AND a r q^j ≡ c (mod g). The last clause is a
condition on the LETTERS (the twist classes a of the factors against the twist class c
of K). J-D0 proved the classifier's stratum counts are gauge/value-blind — one
q-expression per SHAPE; X_K is the first statistic of the program that cannot be
computed from shapes alone. What saves the possibility of clean formulas is not
letter-blindness but letter-EQUIDISTRIBUTION: if, at each type (e', f'), the g₀ twist
classes of factors are equally likely (and suitably independent across factors), then
X_K's law is again a clean function of (q, e, f) — plus one genuinely new invariant of
K, discovered next.

**The mean itself is letter-sensitive: the (2,2) separation.** Take q odd, K of type
(e, f) = (2, 2) — the two twist classes are c = 0, 1 (g_K = gcd(2, q²−1) = 2):
K_c = F_{q²}(√(ζ_K^c π)). Count ramified quadratic subfields L = F(√(ζ^a π)) ⊆ K_c
(type (2,1), a ∈ {0,1}): TK-0 with e' = 2, f' = 1, r = q+1, g = 2, j = 0:
condition a(q+1) ≡ c (mod 2), i.e. (q odd) 0 ≡ c (mod 2). So:

      c = 0:  BOTH ramified quadratics embed (a = 0 and a = 1: #Hom = 2 each,
              #Aut = 2, one subfield each — 2 ramified quadratic subfields);
      c = 1:  NO ramified quadratic embeds.

(Sanity at q = 3: K₀ = Q₃(ζ₈, √3) contains √−1 = ζ₈², hence both √3 and √−3: both
classes ✓; K₁ = Q₃(ζ₈, √(3ζ₈)) contains neither.) Both K_c share the shape (2, 2) and
the same subfields F, F_{q²}, K itself; they differ ONLY in the letter — and S4's mean
law then predicts different E[X_K]: the projective means differ by exactly 2/q. This is
the corpus's first quantity where two towers with equal shape data and different
letters have provably different densities-side statistics — the exact complement of
J-D0(ii), on the value layer where J-D0 never claimed anything.

**What the letters must do for clean laws.** Two separate inputs:
(i) **per-factor letter uniformity** — at each ramified face of a Newton polygon, the
    twist class of the resulting factor is uniform over the g₀ classes (proved at the
    single-face level in S6, LEMMA TK-2, from Haar-uniformity of the leading residual
    unit — this is a measure statement, NOT derivable from the count-layer corpus);
(ii) **joint independence across factors/levels** — the letter of one factor does not
    bias another's (level-0 across-points instance rides (J-D0-PROD); within a cluster
    across faces it is a fresh claim — boxed in S7).
Given (i)+(ii), every X_K quantity reduces to shape-menu data (the corpus's committed
menus) weighted by TK-0 congruence counts — that is the mechanism behind every closed
form in S3–S5.

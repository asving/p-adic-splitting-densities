1. CRITICAL — M1(a′) still makes a false ambient-measure claim.

The offending passage is:

> “q^{−b} … each occurrence IS the AMBIENT Haar mass of a displayed valuation box”

but the same bullet includes:

> “the conditional form used inside (a) is the displayed ratio  
> P(R_e | C_e) = q₀^{−(E−e)}”

The factor \(q_0^{-(E-e)}\), which occurs in the divisor-pattern formula, is a \(C_e\)-conditional probability, not the ambient Haar mass of any displayed box. The displayed ambient boxes have masses \(q_0^{-e}\), \(q_0^{-E}\), and \(q_0^{-N(P)}\); none supplies \(q_0^{-(E-e)}\).

Thus the announced “each occurrence is ambient Haar mass” restatement is false. The repair would be to say that each occurrence has a displayed measure-specific reading—ambient for \(C_e,R_e,\Box(P)\), conditional for \(R_e\mid C_e\)—rather than claiming ambient Haar uniformly.

The BOX-VOL lemma itself is sound, assuming the cited L6a thresholds are nonnegative integral valuation thresholds:

\[
\operatorname{vol}\Box(P)=\prod_i q_0^{-\lceil h_i\rceil}=q_0^{-N(P)}
\]

and distinct vertex coordinates contribute independent factors \(1-q_0^{-1}\). The family-(iv) exclusion in (a″) is also explicit and honest.

4. GAP — (O-4) gives the wrong probability for a fixed loop word.

The passage says:

> “conditioned on {x executes w} (mass \((\kappa_0+\kappa_1)^{|w|}\)-factored per letter, positive)”

For a fixed word \(w=(s_1,\ldots,s_k)\), its mass is

\[
\prod_{j=1}^k P(s_j\mid C_\mu).
\]

Here every individual loop letter has mass \(q^{-E_\mu}\), so the fixed-word mass is \(q^{-E_\mu k}\). The quantity \((\kappa_0+\kappa_1)^k=q^{(1-E_\mu)k}\) is the mass of “some loop letter at each of the first \(k\) steps,” summed over all \(q^k\) loop words.

Only positivity is used downstream, so replacing the parenthetical repairs the proof without changing the transport argument.

1. “Then the full (KP_i) block holds for Φ_{i+1} … the degree block: ψ̂_hom ∤ in(B) for every … deg B < e·g·deg Φ_i [= deg Φ_{i+1} whenever Φ_{i+1} is a degree-conformant lift].”  
   **CRITICAL error.** The hypotheses (K1)+(K2) do not require `deg Φ_{i+1}=eg·deg Φ_i`. Start with the degree-conformant representative supplied by Corollary 4.12, then add an arbitrarily high-degree term having valuation strictly above γ̂. The resulting polynomial has exactly the same initial form, weight-read, and side-read, hence still satisfies (K1)+(K2), but the original representative is now a polynomial of smaller degree whose initial form is divisible by ψ̂_hom. Thus the asserted full key-polynomial minimality block is false for the theorem’s quantified class. The bracketed qualification acknowledges the missing hypothesis but does not impose it. The FGMN pins do not rescue this: Corollary 4.12 really supplies “g ∈ K[x] monic” of degree \(e_rfm_r\); Theorem 4.13 gives an “isomorphism of graded \(F_r\)-algebras”; Corollary 4.14 gives the stated prime/irreducible equivalence; and Proposition 5.7 concerns two members of \(KP(\mu)\), exactly as the note says when rejecting it. See the [journal-version text](https://citeseerx.ist.psu.edu/document?doi=0915764dde26c2da6df40ef854806e27275c4ba0&repid=rep1&type=pdf) and [author-hosted/arXiv PDF](https://upcommons.upc.edu/bitstream/handle/2117/28203/ResidualIdealsArxiv.pdf).

2. “in particular every (SL-dev)-conformant standard lift … satisfies the FULL (KP_i) block.”  
   **CRITICAL error.** `(SL-dev)` also does not impose the missing degree or monicity condition. Since ψ is monic, its top stride has \(\psi_g=1\), but `(SL-dev)` only fixes the initial form of \(D_g\); it does not force \(D_g=1\) or even \(\deg D_g=0\). One may replace \(D_g\) by \(U_0\) plus a positive-degree, sufficiently high-valuation polynomial of degree below \(\deg\Phi_i\). All `(SL-dev)` initial-form identities remain true, while the resulting lift has degree greater than \(eg\deg\Phi_i\). Therefore S3.2 proves only (K1)+(K2), not the full degree block subsequently claimed for every `(SL-dev)` lift.

3. “At a refinement read (e = g = 1 …): the block quantifies over deg B < deg Φ_i … LOW-CONST alone answers.”  
   **CRITICAL error.** This correctly proves the restricted inequality for the fixed window \(\deg B<\deg\Phi_i\), but it does not check the actual corner case of the theorem as quantified. A residual-conformant polynomial can again be altered by a higher-degree, higher-valuation term without changing its initial form. Its degree then exceeds \(\deg\Phi_i\), and the original degree-\(\deg\Phi_i\) representative is a smaller polynomial divisible in initial form by ψ̂_hom. Consequently the claimed refinement-corner key-polynomial minimality fails unless degree conformance is added.

4. “P2 (DEVID canary): the development identity … on random P — 0 violations”; “336 instances”; “GATE PASS.”  
   **JUSTIFICATION GAP.** The gate establishes only the reported finite computations: order exactly two, \(p\in\{2,3,5\}\), \(d_0=g_0=1\), selected \(e_0,e_1,g_1\), and random test polynomials for P2. It does not establish DEVID universally, OP-B1, arbitrary residue fields, higher orders, or the theorem’s missing degree condition. The naked-lift example does validly refute the universal claim that the tested naked construction always preserves the intended residual factor; a reducible computed residual also refutes primeness for those particular naked examples. It does not show that every naked lift fails, that the engine uses the naked construction, or that the twist-aware formula is generally correct—especially because P3’s builder and checker share the twist formula. The note mostly acknowledges these limitations, but “GATE PASS” cannot supply evidence for the claimed full theorem.

VERDICT: CRITICAL

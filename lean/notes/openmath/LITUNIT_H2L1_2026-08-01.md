## 1. The Krasner–Serre–Monge–Pauli–Sinclair mass-formula family — VERDICT: **NO-MATCH** (strong shape analogue)

This is the ONLY family in the located literature that proves finite-level
product-box + pinned-digit counting statements. Exact quotes:

**(i) Polygon strata are floors + exact-valuation slots ([Sin15] Prop 2.9).**

> **Proposition 2.9.** An Eisenstein polynomial f has ramification polygon
> R = {(1, J₀), (p^{s₁}, J₁), ..., (p^{s_{u−1}}, J_{u−1}), (p^{s_u}, 0), ..., (n, 0)},
> where Jᵢ = aᵢn + bᵢ with 0 ≤ bᵢ ≤ n − 1, if and only if
> (a) v_p(fᵢ) ≥ L_R(i)
> (b) For 0 ≤ t ≤ u, v_p(f_{b_t}) = L_R(b_t) if b_t ≠ 0.

**(ii) The stratum is an exact product box ([Sin15] Prop 2.10; refined by
residual-polynomial digit pins in Prop 2.21 via Lemma 2.20).**

> **Proposition 2.10.** ... let Ψ_{n,J₀,R}(c) be the set of all polynomials
> ψ(x) = xⁿ + Σψᵢxⁱ ∈ O_K[x] with
>   ψᵢ ∈ R×_{1,c} if i = 0; R×_{L_R(i),c} if i ∈ B_R; R_{L_R(i),c} if
>   1 ≤ i ≤ n−1 and i ∉ B_R.
> The polynomials in Ψ_{n,J₀,R}(c) generate all totally ramified extensions of
> K of degree n, discriminant p^{n+J₀−1}, and ramification polygon R.

> **Lemma 2.20.** [digit pin] If (p^{sᵢ}, aᵢn + bᵢ) is a point on S with
> bᵢ ≠ 0 then f_{bᵢ,j} = A_{(p^{sᵢ}−p^{s_k})/e} p^{...} (−f_{0,1})^{aᵢ+1} ...
> — i.e. the residual-polynomial choice SETS specific p-adic digits of specific
> coefficients ("all ψ_{i,L_R(i)} for i ∈ B_R are set by the same choice of
> (A₁,...,A_ℓ) ∈ A", Prop 2.21).

**(iii) Counting = product of per-slot box sizes ([Sin15] Prop 6.1, Thm 6.2).**

> **Proposition 6.1.** #Ψ_{n,J₀,R}(c) = (q−1)^{#B_R+1} q^{c−2+(n−1)c−ΣL(i)−#B_R}
> **Theorem 6.2.** The number of distinct totally ramified extensions of K of
> degree n, discriminant p^{n+J₀−1}, and ramification polygon R is
> n(q−1)^{#B_R} q^{n+J₀−1−ΣL(i)−#B_R}.

**(iv) The consolidated modern form ([GRJKPRR25] §3.3).** Families I/K keyed
by Herbrand invariants; Eis(I/K) = an explicit independent-digit box ("when
these coefficients run independently over κ̃, except for the inequalities
d₀, a_σ ≠ 0, one gets the set Eis(I/K)"):

> Let δ ∈ {0,1} be the number of critical D-points, and let α, β, and γ be the
> number of A-points, B-points, and critical C-points respectively. Clearly
> |Eis(I/K)| = (q−1)^{δ+α} q^{β+γ}. Monge reduction (see §3.5) says that
> |Eis(L/K)| = (q−1)^δ q^γ / |Aut(L/K)|. Accordingly, one gets a mass formula
> (3.5)  M(I) := Σ_{L/K∈I/K} 1/|Aut(L/K)| = (q−1)^α q^β.

and Serre's theorem as a digit-space measure statement:

> "view the space Eisen(e/K) of degree e Eisenstein polynomials over K as an
> infinite product F×_q × F_q × F_q × ···, with the successive factors
> corresponding to the variables f₀, f₁, f₂, .... Serre proved in [Ser78,
> Thm. 2] that the chance that a random polynomial in Eisen(e/K) is in
> Eisen(I/K) is the Serre mass SM_P(I) = M(I)/q^{em_w}."

**(v) The fibered per-extension count ([Mon14] Thm 1).**

> **Theorem 1.** Each extension L/K is generated by a reduced polynomial, and
> the number of reduced polynomials generating a fixed extension L/K is
> B_{L/K}/#Aut(L/K).

**Why NO-MATCH (each clause checked against the (TRANS-DEEP) spec, §5 of the
blueprint):**
1. **Window:** all conditions are on the π-adic digits of the coefficients of
   an EISENSTEIN f — the φ₁ = x development, i.e. the order-≤ 1 layer the
   corpus already proves outright (M08 §2.2 + the HC2 order-≤ 1 transport
   kernels). No φ_{r+1}-development over an order-r ≥ 1 type appears anywhere.
2. **Fibering:** strata are keyed by INVARIANTS of L/K (discriminant, polygon,
   residual-poly orbit A, Herbrand invariant) — closed under the K×-action,
   quantified over the whole isomorphism class. Nothing conditions on a
   REALIZED parent stratum ρ (realized digits at level N₀) and asks for the
   induced conditions on deeper window slots; the (TRANS-DEEP) quantifier "for
   every parent realization ρ ... the ρ-fiber's membership conditions are
   EXACTLY ..." has no printed counterpart.
3. **Scope:** totally ramified generators only (Eisenstein); the (FRESH)
   consumer needs general f with mixed inertia/types at working level N.
4. What the family DOES corroborate: the SHAPE of (F)+(J) — exact axis-aligned
   valuation floors + exact-valuation unit slots + finitely many residually
   PINNED digits, with counting = the product of per-slot box sizes — is
   precisely the shape these papers prove at order ≤ 1. Corroboration-grade,
   like GMN Prop 2.10's demotion in §3.6; not a citable home for r ≥ 1.

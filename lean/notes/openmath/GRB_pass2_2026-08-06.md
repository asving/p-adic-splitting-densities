### C1 — TR-1 rescaling: GENUINE

FGMN gives
\[
e(\mu_{r-1})\lambda_r=\frac{h_r}{e_r},
\qquad
\lambda_r=\frac{h_r}{e_0\cdots e_r}.
\]
Under the leaf’s dictionary,
\[
e(\mu_{r-1})=E_i,\quad (e_r,h_r)=(e_i,h_i),
\]
hence
\[
\lambda_r^{FGMN}=\frac{h_i}{e_iE_i}
=\frac{h_i}{E_{i+1}}.
\]

GMN records the polygon slope as
\[
\lambda^{GMN}=-\frac{h_i}{e_i},
\]
so
\[
\boxed{\lambda^{GMN}=-E_i\lambda_r^{FGMN}}.
\]

This also follows directly from ordinate dilation: FGMN ordinates are \(\mu_{r-1}(a_s\phi_r^s)\), whereas project/GMN ordinates are \(E_i\) times these values. Abscissas are unchanged, so slopes acquire exactly the factor \(E_i\). FGMN’s touching line has slope \(-\lambda_r\), while GMN names that negative slope directly. These conventions are confirmed in the primary papers: [FGMN, augmentation and slope conventions](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1305.0775), [GMN, \(\lambda_r=-h_r/e_r\)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0807.2620).

Propagation is consistent:

- TR-5: \(\lambda_r=h_i/E_{i+1}\).
- S2: \(E_{i+1}\lambda_r=h_i\).
- Equivalently, \(E_i\lambda_r=h_i/e_i\).
- S6.2 therefore selects the correct component after the \(E_i\)-ordinate dilation.

Classification: PASS.

### GAP — overclaim about rational graded classes

S2.2 says:

> “conversely every rational class is such a localization fraction.”

That is too strong. FGMN’s polynomial graded algebra is
\[
\operatorname{Gr}(\mu)\simeq
F_r[y,p,p^{-1}][x]/(x^{e_r}-p^{h_r}y),
\]
not the graded ring of all rational functions with every nonzero homogeneous element inverted. For example, inverses of arbitrary prime factors \(\psi(y)\) do not belong to this ring.

The required conclusion is narrower and true: the particular distinguished classes \(x_r,p_r^{\pm1},y_r\) are available. Delete the converse rather than using it to characterize every rational class.

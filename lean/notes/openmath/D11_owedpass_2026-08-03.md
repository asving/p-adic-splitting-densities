## Defects

1. **CRITICAL — M1(a′) contradicts M1’s conditioning scope.**

> “Then, with all masses CONDITIONAL on the state law \(C_e\)”

but later:

> “\(\operatorname{vol}(C_e)=q_0^{-e}\) … \(\operatorname{vol}(R_e)=q_0^{-E}\)”

Those are ambient Haar masses, not masses conditional on \(C_e\). Under the announced conditioning,
\[
P(C_e\mid C_e)=1,\qquad P(R_e\mid C_e)=q_0^{-(E-e)},
\]
not \(q_0^{-e}\) and \(q_0^{-E}\). The singleton-residual examples are likewise conditional on a cell, rather than on \(C_e\). Thus M1 does not have one coherent meaning of “mass.”

2. **CRITICAL — the repaired family-(v) claim repeats the “factor is a mass” error.**

> “each occurrence IS the Haar mass of the displayed valuation box it names there”

and specifically:

> “the L6a cell factors \(q_0^{-N(P)}\)”

Yet the note itself states that the cell mass is
\[
(1-q_0^{-1})^kq_0^{-N(P)}.
\]
Unless a separate valuation-box event is explicitly defined, \(q_0^{-N(P)}\) is merely a factor in the cell-mass formula. No such event is defined in §1 or in M1(a′). This is the same inference the revision says it withdrew for general factors.

3. **CRITICAL — the billing still declares non-masses to satisfy the mass-identification charge.**

M1(a′) correctly admits:

> “\(q^{E_m}/(q^{E_m}-1)\) … NOT masses (they exceed 1)”

but §5 nevertheless says clause 1 is:

> “PROVED … family (v) per entry per (a′)”

and §7 says:

> “The unit charge’s ‘kernel/entry VALUES are masses’ is covered exactly at (a)/(a′)/M1′”

It is not covered at those scaling entries: only their product with an exit mass is identified as an event mass. This is a direct scope/billing contradiction, not merely an external seam.

4. **CRITICAL — M1(b)’s multi-step disjointness statement is false without a prefix-free restriction.**

> “Multi-step events built from disjoint strata by the step maps are disjoint at the first differing step.”

Counterexample: the event “the first step is loop-0” and the event “the first step is loop-0 and the second step is an exit” are both built from the displayed strata, but the second is a subset of the first. The argument works only for stopping words or another explicitly prefix-free collection. The particular \(E^{(L)}\) and loop-then-exit families appear prefix-free, but the theorem claims more.

5. **GAP — Lemma M2.0’s address induction still omits the within-block loop history.**

The address is defined using only hand-offs:

> “a finite word … of (hand-off stratum, child slot) pairs”

but the induction step asserts:

> “\(\{\text{node }a\text{ reached}\}=\{\text{node }a'\text{ reached}\}\cap\{x_{a'}\in F\}\)”

A child may be created only after \(L\ge 1\) loop steps followed by exit through \(F\). If \(x_{a'}\) denotes the state at the node’s birth, reaching that child is not the event \(x_{a'}\in F\); it is a countable union of events involving loop-map iterates. Consequently, (S1-m) on the canonical one-step restriction to \(F\) is not applied at the point actually encountered.

One can plausibly prove the desired absolute-continuity result by extending addresses to include loop words/times and repeatedly using the loop pushforwards, but that proof is absent. Assume M2.0’s conclusion for downstream verification.

6. **CRITICAL — Theorem M2 quantifies over arbitrary allocation data that the note itself says can destroy the mass interpretation.**

M2 states:

> “Assume (S1) and (S2) for the member. Then … the abstract solved values ARE the real conditional densities”

But M1(a″) and M1′ expressly say:

> “family-(iv) verdict rows under ARBITRARY allocation data \(\alpha_e\) … need not be masses at all (they can leave \([0,1]\))”

and:

> “O-12’s theorem quantifies over all of them”

Neither (S1) nor (S2) constrains arbitrary allocation coefficients to equal semantic verdict-event allocations. Thus choose an allocation allowed by the abstract family whose source/verdict row leaves \([0,1]\). Hand-off faithfulness and composition correctness may still hold, while the abstract solution cannot be a probability vector.

The proof exposes the missing premise when it says:

> “With M1(a) (each \(P(F)\) is the abstract mass entry at \(q_0\))”

M1(a) does not establish that for arbitrary family-(iv) source/verdict rows. Therefore M2 is false at its displayed universal scope; it needs an additional semantic-allocation/correspondence hypothesis, which is not billed.

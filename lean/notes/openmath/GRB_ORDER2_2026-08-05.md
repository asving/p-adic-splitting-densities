### S2.1 Lemma RESCALE

**Statement.** Let µ be a valuation on K[x] with value group Γ(µ) ⊆ (1/E)ℤ for a
positive integer E, and let w := E·µ (so w is ℤ-valued). Then for every γ ∈ ℤ the
identity map on K[x] induces isomorphisms P_γ(w) = P_{γ/E}(µ) and
P_γ⁺(w) = P_{γ/E}⁺(µ), hence a graded-ring isomorphism
gr(w) = ⊕_{γ∈ℤ} gr_γ(w) ≅ ⊕_{α∈Γ(µ)} gr_α(µ) = gr(µ) under the index dilation
γ = E·α, matching initial forms (in_w(A) = in_µ(A) as classes) and therefore
matching Δ, units, anchors, and every operator defined from the graded pieces.

**Proof.** w(A) = E·µ(A), and E > 0, so w(A) ≥ γ ⟺ µ(A) ≥ γ/E and
w(A) > γ ⟺ µ(A) > γ/E: the filtration sets coincide literally, piece by piece

α ↦ Eα, a group isomorphism Γ(µ) ∩ (attained values) → ℤ-attained; products go to
products because multiplication in both graded rings is induced by multiplication in
K[x] on the SAME cosets. in_w(A) is the class of A in gr_{w(A)}(w) = gr_{µ(A)}(µ),

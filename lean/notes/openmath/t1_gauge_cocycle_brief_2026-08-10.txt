== BACKGROUND (2 paragraphs) ==

The project proves density statements for splitting types of degree-n polynomials
over p-adic fields via an Okutsu–Montes/MacLane-style tower calculus. A "tower"
is a chain of types with stage data (e_i, f_i, u_i) at each level: e_i =
ramification jump, f_i = residue jump, u_i = the dv_i-height of the next key
polynomial; level-1 data (e_1, f_1, h) with slope h/e_1 in lowest terms, base
residue field F_Q, K = F_Q(eta) the level-1 residue field where eta is the "stage
letter" = the residue of x^{e_1}/pi^h at the stage's points (pi = the base
uniformizer, x = the base key). D' := e_1 f_1. The "ladder normalizer" nhat(k) =
x^{i(k)} pi^{a(k)} is the unique monomial of dv-height exactly k with 0 <= i(k)
< e_1 (so i(k) is determined by k: i(k)·h ≡ k mod e_1, and a(k) = (k − i(k)h)/e_1
— heights add, x-exponents add MODULO e_1 with a wrap). At level 2 the composed
key is Phi_2 = Phi'^{e_2 f_2} − Sum_{t<f_2} chat_t · nhat(u_2(f_2−t)) ·
Phi'^{e_2 t}, where psi_2 = Z^{f_2} − Sum_{t<f_2} c_t Z^t in K[Z] is the stage
residual (minimal polynomial of the new letter eta_2) and chat_t is a LIFT of
c_t to a polynomial in x, pi. Levels iterate: at level i the normalizers are
nhat_i(m) = pi^a x^i Phi'^b-type ladder monomials, the letters are z_1, z_2, ...
(FGMN's canonical letters) or eta, eta_2, ... (the repo's letters), and the same
lift-the-residual-coefficients recipe builds Phi_{i+1}.

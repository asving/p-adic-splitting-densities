## 3. Flag ML-scope (GD23 §5) — VERDICT: **MATCH** (printed scope is WIDER than claimed)

**GD23's displayed sentence.** "MacLane's standing hypotheses are a field with

**The printed standing hypotheses.** Introduction (maclane1936.txt:16–21),
verbatim:

> "The succession of values V₁, V₂, … is defined in Part I for polynomials
> with coefficients in **any field K**. This requires a general method (§§4
> and 5) of constructing a value V_k from a previously obtained value V_{k−1}.
> The value given by the limit of such a sequence needs a special study (§7).
> Here, as in §§8 and 16, we assume that every value of the field K is
> 'discrete'; that is, that the real numbers used as values form an isolated
> point set, as in the case of p-adic values."

So discreteness is assumed ONLY in §§7, 8, 16 (limit values / completeness /
normal forms); §§2–6 and Part II carry no completeness and no residue-field
hypothesis. "Value" throughout = non-archimedean exponential valuation with
REAL values (§2: "V(ab) = V(a) + V(b), V(a+b) ≥ min(V(a), V(b))"; a ring with
a value must be an integral domain).

**The two pinned statements, verbatim (maclane1936.txt:261–270, 213–216).**

> **Definition 4.1.** A key polynomial φ(x) ≠ 0 over a value W of K[x] is one
> which satisfies the following conditions:
> (i) *Irreducibility.* If a product is equivalence-divisible in W by φ(x),
>     then one of the factors is equivalence-divisible by φ(x).
> (ii) *Minimal degree.* Any non-zero polynomial equivalence-divisible in W by
>     φ(x) has a degree in x not less than the degree of φ(x).
> (iii) The leading coefficient of φ(x) is 1.
> This key polynomial is to be assigned a new value (1) V(φ(x)) = µ > W(φ(x)).

> **Theorem 4.2.** If W is a value of K[x], φ(x) is a key polynomial over W
> and µ is a real number satisfying (1), then the function V defined in (3) is
> also a value of K[x]. V is called an augmented value, and is denoted by
> V = [W, V(φ) = µ].

with (3) (maclane1936.txt:310): V(f_m φ^m + ⋯ + f_0) = min_i (W(f_i) + iµ)
over the φ-expansion (coefficients f_i of degree < deg φ).

**Comparison.** GD23's claim is confirmed and the printed scope is in fact

arbitrary-DVR instantiation (mixed or equal characteristic, F arbitrary) is

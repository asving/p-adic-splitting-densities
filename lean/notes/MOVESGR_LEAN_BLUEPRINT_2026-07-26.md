## 1. The construction (D.1(a)-(e) taken literally; all in `Defs.lean`, all compiling)

The design decision, stated once: **build `gr_w(A)` from the side FILTRATION of `A = ℤ_[p][X]`,
induce its multiplication from `A`'s multiplication, and recover `σ.R` as `Θ ∘ [·]` — never the
reverse.** The concrete objects (each an anchor; `S : SideVal p`, `σ : Stage p F`):

* **(0) `SideVal p`** — the D.1 hypothesis package: `w : A → WithTop ℤ` (value `⊤` at `0`) with
  `w_zero`, `w_ne_top`, `w_neg`, ultrametric `w_add`, scalar law `w_smul` (`w f ≤ w (C c · f)`),
  submultiplicative `w_mul` (`w f + w g ≤ w (f·g)`), `w_one`. A genuine stage's `σ.w` extended by
  `0 ↦ ⊤` is an inhabitant (built by the L1 units).

* **(1a) The filtration** `ge γ := {f | γ ≤ w f}`, `gt γ := {f | γ < w f}` as honest
  `Submodule ℤ_[p] A` (membership discharged from the `SideVal` laws — COMPILES). `gtIn γ` is
  `gt γ` viewed inside `ge γ`. **`grPiece γ := (ge γ) ⧸ (gtIn γ) = A_{≥γ}/A_{>γ}`** (D.1) and
  **`Gr := DirectSum ℤ grPiece = gr_w(A)`**.

* **(1b) The genuine piece multiplication** — the anchor of the whole discharge:
  ```
  (†)  pmul γ δ : grPiece γ →ₗ[ℤ_[p]] grPiece δ →ₗ[ℤ_[p]] grPiece (γ+δ),   [a]·[b] = [a·b]
  ```

  submultiplicative strictness `WithTop.add_lt_add_of_{le_of_lt,lt_of_le}`: raising either factor's

* **(1c) `initialForm f := [f]`** `:= DirectSum.of _ (deg f) (mkPiece f)`, the genuine
  initial-form map (`deg f := (w f).untopD 0`, so `↑(deg f) = w f` for `f ≠ 0`, `coe_deg_le`).

## 2. Mathlib-reuse report (investigated)

* **Associated graded of a filtration/valuation — NOT in mathlib.** `Mathlib/RingTheory/
  Filtration.lean` has `Ideal.Filtration` (I-ADIC only) whose `submodule` is `⊕ Nᵢ` over the Rees
  algebra — the Rees module, NOT the graded quotient ring `⊕ Nᵢ/Nᵢ₊₁`. A grep for
  `associatedGraded`/`associated_graded` over all of mathlib returns nothing. **Conclusion:
  `gr_w(A)` must be built by hand** (this file's core), which is also why R5.3 flagged it "a large,
  self-contained formalization unit … not off-the-shelf."
* **Genuinely lighter reuse we DID take** (validated in `Defs.lean`): `Submodule` for `ge`/`gt`
  (ℤ_[p]-submodules — the `w_smul` law makes them submodules, giving the richer quotient API);
  `Submodule.Quotient` for the pieces; **`LinearMap.liftQ₂`** (`Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/Quotient/
  Bilinear.lean`, D. Loeffler 2026) + `LinearMap.compr₂` for the bilinear descent of `pmul` — this
  is the single biggest labor-saver, the whole well-definedness of the graded product;
  `DirectSum ℤ` for `⊕_γ`; `WithTop.add_lt_add_of_{le_of_lt,lt_of_le}` for submult strictness.
* **Localization — reuse `Localization` / `IsLocalization`.** For the FULL graded localization `L`
  (all degrees) we localize the CommRing `Gr` at the submonoid `homMon`: mathlib
  `Localization homMon` + `Localization.isLocalization` is the canonical witness (`L2_loc_exists`
  is a one-line reuse). For the DEGREE-0 part, `Mathlib/RingTheory/GradedAlgebra/
  HomogeneousLocalization.lean` (`HomogeneousLocalization`, `NumDenSameDeg`, `val`, `Away`) is
  directly applicable to `(L^coeff)₀` and `L₀` — recommended for `L2_degZero_subring`/`L2_coeffLoc`.
* **Graded-ring assembly — reuse `DirectSum.GCommRing`/`GradedRing`.** `L1_gradedRingStr_exists`
  should route the `CommRing Gr` through mathlib's `DirectSum.GSemiring`/`GCommRing` graded-monoid
  machinery, feeding `pmul` as the `GMul` and `[1]` as the `GOne` — this discharges assoc/distrib
  from the graded-algebra API rather than by hand.

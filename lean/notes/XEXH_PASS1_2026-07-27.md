1. **CRITICAL — “ZERO” rows do not classify the actual GMN per-order gain.**  
   The arithmetic formula itself is correct:
   \[
   \operatorname{ind}(S)=\frac{EH-E-H+\gcd(E,H)}2
   =\frac{\ell(eh\ell-e-h+1)}2.
   \]
   Checks:

   - T1, e.g. \((e,h,\ell)=(1,1,2)\): \(\operatorname{ind}(S)=1\). The displayed on-side point is counted under the pinned on-or-below convention.
   - T2, e.g. \((2,3,1)\): \(\operatorname{ind}(S)=(2-1)(3-1)/2=1\), with strict-interior point \((1,1)\) in the local \(2\times3\) triangle.
   - T3, \((e,1,1)\): \(\operatorname{ind}(S)=0\).
   - T4, \((1,h,1)\): \(\operatorname{ind}(S)=0\).

   The problem is the passage from a side lower bound to the asserted GMN verdict. The note itself says:

   > “A non-last \(\ell=1\) side’s RIGHT endpoint … COUNTS, but its index unit is charged to the POLYGON (cross terms…)”

   GMN’s \(\operatorname{ind}_r\) is attached to the whole normalized polygon, not independently to the selected side. Thus a T3/T4 selected side can have side contribution zero while its order polygon has positive index from placement, other sides, or cross terms. “ZERO” is therefore only “no positive lower bound certified from this side,” not an actual zero GMN gain. This conflicts directly with X.0’s definition of \(d_{\rm gen}\) as nodes having actual \(\operatorname{ind}_r\ge1\).

1. **CRITICAL — XHD is not stated as a closed, refutable hypothesis package.**  
   XHD-d promises an “`EXPLICITLY-LISTED union of congruence-class translates of cone lattices`,” but no lists are present, and “cone lattice,” the ambient coordinates, congruence moduli, and dependence on templates/cells are not formally defined. Likewise, `H(γ)` is called the “EXACT realizable height domain” without specifying whether realizability is per representative, per fresh assignment, existential, or uniform across all assignments counted by \(T\). This is a future deliverable, not a precise theorem statement that [2b] can prove verbatim.

2. **CRITICAL — XHD-w overstates what §C supplies at fixed height.**  
   The faithful §C statement is: for one fixed transition-admissible appended read/history, the conditional cylinder ratio is  
   \[
   \operatorname{vol}_{\rm nom}(E_{\rm fresh}).
   \]
   But V.0 claims §C already identifies a cell-indexed quantity
   “`given the source cylinder at one representative of α, of one fresh assignment realizing (m,o,β)`.”  
   Section C does not prove that this ratio depends only on \((\alpha,\beta,m,o,h)\), is equal for all assignments counted by \(T\), or is representative-independent. Those are precisely table/lumpability assertions. Thus the numerical fixed-history ratio is faithful, but the claimed cell-uniform “XHD-w face” is not wholly §C-derivable.

3. **CRITICAL — the \(T\cdot G\) factorization lacks a height-uniform count hypothesis.**  
   V.2 defines
   “`T := #{fresh assignments producing (m,o,β)}`”
   with no height argument, while V.1 defines \(g(h)\) and sums over heights. Nothing states that the number of assignments producing \((m,o,\beta)\) is the same \(T(q)\) for every \(h\), or that every assignment counted by \(T\) realizes every \(h\in H(\gamma^1)\). Without that rectangularity,
   \[
   \sum_h T(h)\,g(h)\neq T\sum_h g(h)
   \]
   in general. XHD-w controls weights, XHD-d controls a domain, and neither states the missing count/domain uniformity.

4. **CRITICAL — INIT-RAT does not follow from the displayed XHD package.**  
   The proof writes
   “`(ENT-COUNT polynomial) · p^{-A(ε,h_ent)}`”
   and invokes XHD-s. But ENT-COUNT gives only: for each fixed \(h_{\rm ent}\), \(I^{\rm ent}(h_{\rm ent})\) is some polynomial in \(q\). It gives no finite uniform family or semilinear/quasipolynomial dependence of its coefficients on \(h_{\rm ent}\). XHD-s sums products of the \(g_i(h_i)\); it does not cover an arbitrarily height-varying multiplier \(I^{\rm ent}(h_{\rm ent})\). Hence the asserted rational entrance vector is not derivable from XHD + ENT-COUNT as stated.

5. **CRITICAL — (iv)-PART contradicts the accepted D4-R branch-tree structure.**  
   V.1 says the sets over all “`(m,o,β), terminal verdicts ... PARTITION the full fresh-assignment set`” and hence sum to \(1\). But D4-R L5 explicitly says one digit cell can carry several eligible \((\text{side},\psi)\) continuations and that distinct next-node strata may overlap or even be identical. V.2 adopts exactly that branch-cell semantics: each continuation is a separate marked branch entry. Therefore one fresh assignment may contribute to several continuing \((m,o,\beta)\) entries. The row sum is a marked offspring mass and can exceed \(1\); it is not a partition or a substochastic probability row. Consequently calling the A-block a “chain” is also unjustified without a separate branching-kernel interpretation.

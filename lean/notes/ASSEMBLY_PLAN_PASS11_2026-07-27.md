## C. The §T-ASSEMBLY demolition versus the plan

### Missing joint-cell/root mass

This is a note-drafting defect, not a defect in the plan’s displayed first-split recursion.

The plan’s deep recursion is

\[
b_e^{\rm split}(\tau)(\sigma')
 =\sum_{o\ \mathrm{branching}} T\!\cdot G
   \sum_{\sigma'=\biguplus_j\sigma_j}
   \prod_j\beta_{e_j,\tau_j(o)}(\sigma_j).
\]

The shared branching-cell mass is the single \(T\!\cdot G\) factor outside the sibling product. At the root/shallow layer, the corresponding shared mass is in \(W_{\widehat S}\). Consequently the plan charges:

- the shared cell/root factor once;
- each child continuation conditionally afterward;
- no child product before the shared factor.

If the draft omitted that factor, the draft failed to instantiate the plan.

### Invalid no-side product

This exposes a genuine plan-side defect. The plan noticed that ns is a pro-cylinder and altered TREE-N, but left the old finite-terminal product architecture in several other displayed statements. In particular, TREE-EXP and \(b_e^{\rm term}\) still treat every ns halt like an ordinary finite-cell terminal event.

The defect is not the first-split product for ordinary branching cells. It is the unresolved routing of the exceptional null pro-cylinder through an architecture designed for finite terminal cells.

### Unsupported multinomial

The member-indexed first-split recursion itself is sound: children are individuated by cell data, and the convolution sums member-indexed tuples directly, with no symmetry factor.

The blanket shape-level statement

> “any shape-level factorization … **DISPLAYS the multinomial factor**
> \(m!/(m_1!\cdots m_k!)\)”

is too strong. That multinomial is valid only if the individuated sibling slots are exchangeable for the proposed child-shape assignment. Sibling slots can carry different residue degrees, child states, or other cell data, so arbitrary permutations need not be admissible or equal-mass.

Because the plan elsewhere defines \(C_{\widehat T}\) by direct counting, this does not presently destroy TREE-EXP or \(b_e^{\rm split}\). It is nevertheless a false general counting prescription and must not be used in the \(C_{\widehat T}\) or \(W_{\widehat S}\) derivations.

### Other demolition items

The missing root mass, invalid “no-side product,” and draft multinomial do not collectively refute the plan’s ordinary first-split display. Their dispositions are:

- joint-cell/root mass: note-only;
- no-side/null leaf: plan-side;
- multinomial: plan-side overstatement, but not intrinsic to the member-level recursion;
- `(ns-null)` proof gap: honestly ledgered, but its consequences are incompletely routed;
- T-n3 attribution: the plan’s “computed both ways” description is still too vague to guarantee that either side is independent of the assembly formulas under test.

1. **CRITICAL — `b_e^split` uses the wrong measure object, invalidating the claimed (K-SUB) reconciliation.**  
   The note defines
   > “`b_e^split := Σ_{o branching} T·G · …`”
   
   and later asserts
   > “`b_e^split consumes each branching CELL once — T·G per cell`.”
   
   But §V explicitly types `T·G` as a marked, per-continuing-branch transition: a branching digit cell contributes once for every eligible continuation, producing PART-2’s surplus. Nothing in the pasted interface turns one such `T·G` into the mass of the underlying digit cell counted once. That requires a joint-cell/cell-level object such as `jvol(c|Σ)` and a sum indexed by digit cells, not the marked CTS transition table. DEG-CONS proves that the same-size part of `K_e` has at most one mark per cell, but it does not repair the branching exit vector. Consequently, the displayed recursion can multiply-count a branching cell, and RS.2’s rationality inventory also lacks the rationality hypothesis for the required joint-cell coefficients.

2. **CRITICAL — (β-2b) does not establish joint-cell height invariance from its stated hypotheses.**  
   The decisive sentence is:
   > “joint-cell factors `jvol(c_s │ Σ)` are per-read digit-cell conditional masses determined by the read’s nominal digit inventory, which `(u-R)` routes through retained data.”
   
   XHD-u/u-R is stated for single-branch template counts and per-assignment ratios. It does not imply representative/history invariance of the whole joint digit-cell law. `(SIB)` starts only after conditioning on the branching cell; it cannot prove that the probability of selecting that cell is independent of `h_ent`. `(JC-multi)` is named, but no quoted clause says that its joint-cell mass is invariant across representatives and admissible histories of the same retained state. Thus the passage from single-branch marginal invariance to full tree-fiber invariance contains an unlicensed joint-lumpability step. This is exactly where equal marginals can coexist with different correlations or different branching-cell probabilities.

   The narrower no-PCI claim is otherwise correct: the proof expands prescribed finite trees directly and makes no substitution of a previously defined β-value. The defect is missing joint representative-uniformity, not a hidden β-substitution.

3. **CRITICAL — RESUM-n3 is called sealed without containing a seal.**  
   S.7 says
   > “SEAL BEFORE ANY CENSUS”
   
   but supplies none of the five rational functions, none of their four-prime values, no active-state lists, no partial-sum integers, and no junk-block determinants. The status
   > “`RESUM-n3 SEALED-NEW (not run)`”
   
   therefore describes a protocol to create a seal, not an actual sealed prediction set. A referee cannot reconstruct the comparison target from the note alone without using the implementation under test. The acceptance blocker is honest, but the gate is not yet referee-derivable or sealed.

4. **CRITICAL — the claimed “exact” PARI discriminating layer lacks cylinder-level decisiveness.**  
   The gate proposes
   > “`decided_σ(N)` by EXHAUSTIVE enumeration … types from the PARI `factorpadic` oracle”
   
   and calls the resulting equality exact. Enumeration modulo \(p^N\) counts residue cylinders. Running `factorpadic` on a chosen integer representative does not by itself prove that the factorization type is constant throughout that cylinder; near discriminant-zero or insufficient-precision loci, lifts of the same residue class can have different types. The gate needs either a certified determination radius for every counted box, or an explicit unresolved-box policy and proof that only classifier-decided cylinders are compared. It also needs the precise PARI-output-to-\(\sigma\) map and handling of zero-discriminant/precision-failure cases. Without those, the layer is extractor-independent but not an exact discriminator for the finite-level density totals.

5. **GAP — (CUT-WD) leaves the entrance boundary ownership off by one.**  
   It declares
   > “a node `ν` is SHALLOW iff its root-path contains no entrance node at-or-before `ν`”
   
   while `W_Ŝ` is said to include the shallow cascade “to every block entrance,” and β is conditioned after entry. The note must pin whether the entering read/edge belongs to `W_Ŝ`/the entrance mass or to the block subtree. As written, the node-based “at-or-before” convention can disagree with the edge-based entrance-shape definition, risking omission or double charging of the entering read. The abstract first-entrance trichotomy is otherwise sound once this boundary convention is fixed.

6. **GAP — RS.4’s standing tag is incomplete.**  
   The proof uses
   > “a rational function equal to 1 at infinitely many prime powers”
   
   but its standing tag does not explicitly inherit RS.2 or CTS-M(iv)-POLY/CL-6. RS.1 and the nine probabilistic inputs establish the primewise mass equality; they do not alone establish that \(\sum_\sigma R_\sigma\) is one fixed rational function. INIT-RAT and CL-17(ii)’s rational \(W_{\widehat S}\) burden must likewise remain attached through RS.2. The checksum argument is valid after those hypotheses are added, but the advertised tag completeness fails.

7. **GAP — ESCAPE-probe layer 1 states an insufficient closed-class test.**  
   It declares a class closed when there are
   > “no outgoing edges leaving it AND zero one-step terminal mass.”
   
   For the same-size kernel, branching/split mass is also an exit and appears as a row deficit rather than an \(A\)-edge or terminal column. A class with positive split mass is not stochastic and does not imply \(\rho=1\), even if the stated two conditions hold. The conclusive test must require total row mass inside the class to equal one—equivalently zero terminal and zero branching exit mass. This affects the probe specification, not the conditional determinant argument itself.

The remaining audited pieces are sound under their displayed assumptions: (K-TRI) follows from DEG-CONS; β-1 and β-2a need XHD-w/u/d/s but no HMC; HMC is needed only to replace whole-template height sums by products of one-step \(G\)’s; the adjugate induction is valid once the correctly typed exit vector and determinant are available; and RS.3’s symbolic nonvanishing follows from E0 at infinitely many all-active primes. The n=3 degeneracy caveat is honest and correctly admits that the gate does not exercise the first surplus or genuinely multi-continuing deep split.

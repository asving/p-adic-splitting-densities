### 3.A The E5 (U)∧(R) pair and its hoist

The displayed pair (E5:343-348), over a fixed reduction datum g:

    (U)  ∀ y ∈ rootCell χ g, ∀ ν ν', T.child none ν y → T.child none ν' y →
           trackOf ν = trackOf ν' → ν = ν'
    (R)  ∀ y ∈ rootCell χ g, ∀ ν, T.child none ν y →
           2 ≤ Multiset.count (trackOf ν)
             (UniqueFactorizationMonoid.normalizedFactors (redPoly χ y))

Content (MOVES 7112–7119, the (c2) covering case analysis): realized root
children are EXACTLY the first window reads on the reduction's REPEATED tracks —
(U) says "THE first window read" (per point, per track, at most one child), (R)
says "repeated tracks only" (m_i = 1 gives τ-hen, which carries NO child).
Everything downstream is already machine-checked in E5 (hcard6/hheads_eq/hτmem/
hτsurj/hnostray/hconv/hfeq + the SIB counting): the pair is the ONLY gap.

Why it is not provable from the displayed laws: `RedCellPartition` puts a
realized child's track among the normalizedFactors and keys it to the .red-cell
branch set, but (a) nothing bounds a .red-cell branch set's cardinality (two
distinct children can share a track), and (b) nothing forces multiplicity ≥ 2
(`ChildCover` is the ∃-direction only; `KBTotTower.root_total` likewise). Why no
countermodel compiled: the pair sits under the FULL `CellAssign` premise row and

### 3.B TREE-N stability (treeN_stable)

Statement to prove (Defs.lean:1624): for N ≥ Tr.thr n and any working level
N' ≥ N, two boxes agreeing on the level-<N block (the first n·N coordinates
under the standard layout) have equal fiberAt truth values.

Anatomy of `fiberAt` (six clauses) by x-dependence:
- clauses (i)/(ii)/(iv): read x only through `T.mem (some H) x` and
  `NsHalts T (some H) x` (inside PrunedMem duties), quantified over ALL
  histories H (clause (i) is an ↔, so the BACKWARD direction needs mem/NsHalts
  determinacy for arbitrary H, not just Tr.chains members);
- clause (iii): reads x only through `henPayload χ x` = a function of
  `redPoly χ x` = a function of the chart coordinates x(χ b);
- clause (v): x-free (tree data only);
- clause (vi): reads x only through `normalizedFactors (redPoly χ x)`.

So TreeNStable follows from exactly three level-determinacy inputs:

    (S-chart)  ∀ b, ((χat N') b : ℕ) < n·N  — for every N ≥ 1 it suffices that
               the chart lands in the level-1 block: ∀ b, (χat N' b : ℕ) < n.
    (S-mem)    ∀ H, ∀ x x' agreeing below n·N:
               (Tat N').mem (some H) x ↔ (Tat N').mem (some H) x'.
    (S-ns)     the same transfer for NsHalts (Tat N') (some H) ·.

(IrrHalts is x-free.) Given these, the proof is clause-by-clause transfer using
E5's `hconv` as the template; the redPoly clauses use the E5 `hredeq`
computation generalized from "both in one root cell" to "agree on the chart
coordinates" (identical Finset.sum_congr argument).

Reduction of (S-mem) to a child-level law: TreeModel's mem is GENERATED by
child through `mem_single` (one-node histories) and `mem_snoc` (extensions), so

    (S-child)  ∀ o ν, ∀ x x' agreeing below n·N:

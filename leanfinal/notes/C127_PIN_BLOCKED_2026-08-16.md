## The gap: the note's §3 Step 4 and Step 5 — six named missing pieces

`dv2Hgt (T.levelDatum hπ) A` unfolds (C.11 → C.06 → C.02) to

```
dvSupp F A u₂ e₂ = inf_{b′ ≤ deg A} ( e₂ • (inf_{i} ( e₁ • gaussVal (C ((dev Φ′ A b′).coeff i))
                                                     + h·i )) + u₂·b′ )
```

which at `e₂ = h = 1` is the note's slot pin `min over slots (D′·v(λ) + a′ + b′u₂)`.  To
evaluate it on the two polynomials above, the corpus is missing:

1. **`red` of a monomial as a Lean identity.**  `(C λ * X^N) %ₘ (X^{D′} − C(πω))
   = C (λ·(πω)^{N / D′}) * X^{N % D′}`.  Nothing in the landed corpus proves this;
   C.16's `slotIdx`/`twistExp` are the STAGE-FIELD analogue (`x^k ↦ η^{Q(k)}x^{slotIdx k}`),
   not the `O[x]/Φ′` statement, and cannot be reused verbatim.  Needs an induction on
   `N / D′`.  This is the single largest missing piece; every slot value below depends on it.
2. **The `Φ′`-digits of a reassembly.**  `dev Φ′ (reass F S) b′ = modByMonicHom F.hmonic
   (S.coeff b′)` when the reps are in-grid — provable from the landed
   `dev_eval_eq_coeff` (the digits of an in-grid two-variable evaluation are its
   coefficients), but the bridge lemma is not written.
3. **`stageHeight` of a monomial.**  `F.stageHeight (C λ * X^{a′}) = e₁·v_π(λ) + h·a′`
   (with `= ⊤` at `λ = 0`).  The landed height layer has `stageHeight_eq_inf` (C.02) and the
   `gaussVal` API (B.08/B.09/B.32a) but no evaluation of the inner `inf` at a monomial.
4. **`gaussVal` of a unit times a `π`-power.**  `gaussVal (C (u·π^n)) = n` for `IsUnit u`
   — the reason the three `IsUnit` hypotheses (`hc`, `hc₂`, `hc₀`, `hω`) are in the frozen
   signature and are NOT consumed by the two landed clauses.
5. **An `inf`-evaluation lemma over `ℕ∞`.**  "all terms `≥ M`, one term `= M`, absent slots
   `= ⊤` ⟹ the `Finset.inf` is `M`", plus the arithmetic
   `e₁·(α + (k+1)v₂ + w_k) + ((s+k i₂) − D′w_k) + (b−k)u₂ = H_C + δ` at
   `w_k = (s + k i₂) / D′` — Nat arithmetic that `omega` closes once `div`/`mod` are named,
   but only after 1–4 supply the slot data.
6. **The `b = m−1` collision (Step 4's *Non-collision* paragraph).**  When `b = m−1` the
   `P`-chain deposit lands on the `k = 0` slot of the `c₀`-family (because `D′ ∣ m i₂`, from
   (F3) `D′v₀ = m u₂`); the merged value `g·π^{v₀}((−1)^m ĉ₂^m ω^{m i₂/D′} − ĉ₀)` may have
   HIGHER valuation or vanish, and the pin then rests on the slots `k = 1, …, b`, which is
   why `m ≥ 2` and `b ≥ 1` are needed there.  Formalizing this needs the divisibility
   `D′ ∣ m i₂` (immediate from `hv₀`, `hu₂`) plus a case split that the other five pieces
   must already support.

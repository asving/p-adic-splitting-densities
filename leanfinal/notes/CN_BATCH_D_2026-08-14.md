## CN-21 — the informal derivation (written before the Lean)

**Claim.** `O` a DVR, `π` a uniformizer, `k = O/𝔪`; `f ∈ O[X]` monic, `n = deg f > 0`, and
`f̄ = f mod 𝔪` irreducible in `k[X]`. Then `typeOf f = ⟨{(1, n)}⟩`.

**Step 1 — `f` is irreducible over `O`.** `Monic.irreducible_of_irreducible_map`. Hence `{f}` is
*the* monic factorization and `typeOf f = ⟨{efPair f}⟩`; everything is now about one pair.

**Step 2 — `𝔪A` is a maximal ideal of `A = O[X]/(f)`.** `AdjoinRoot.quotEquivQuotMap` gives
`A ⧸ 𝔪A ≅ k[X] ⧸ (f̄)`, and `k[X]` is a PID in which `f̄` is irreducible, so the right side is a
field (`PrincipalIdealRing.isMaximal_of_irreducible`, `Ideal.Quotient.maximal_ideal_iff_isField_quotient`);
transport the `IsField` back along the equiv (`MulEquiv.isField`) and apply
`Ideal.Quotient.maximal_of_isField`.

**Step 3 — off `𝔪A` everything is a unit.** Let `z ∉ 𝔪A`. By maximality there are `w` and
`i ∈ 𝔪A` with `zw − 1 = −i`, i.e. `z` is invertible *modulo* `𝔪A`. So multiplication by `z` is
surjective on `A ⧸ 𝔪·A`. Now `A` is a **finite** `O`-module (`AdjoinRoot.powerBasis'`, `f` monic)
and `𝔪 ⊆ Jac(O)` (`O` local), so Nakayama in the form
`LinearMap.surjective_of_surjective_comp_mkQ` upgrades this to surjectivity of
`x ↦ z x : A → A`; taking a preimage of `1` gives `z x = 1`. **This is the step the spec's RISK
line feared** — and note the route taken is *not* the suggested one: no DVR structure on `A` is
built, no Krull intersection, no uniformizer of `A`. Nakayama over the base does all the work,
which is why the node came in well under its 150-line estimate.

**Step 4 — every norm valuation is a multiple of `n`.** Strong induction on
`k = v(N_{A/O} z)`. If `z` is a unit, so is `N z` (the norm is a monoid hom), so `k = 0`. If not,
Step 3 puts `z ∈ 𝔪A = πA`, say `z = π z'`; then
`N z = N(π·1)·N z' = π^n · N z'` (`Algebra.norm_algebraMap` with `finrank_O A = n`), so
`k = n + v(N z')` with `v(N z') = m < k`, and induction gives `n ∣ m`, hence `n ∣ k`.
(The argument never needs `z ≠ 0`: if `z = 0` the hypothesis `v(N z) = k ∈ ℕ` is false.)

**Step 5 — read off `(e, f)`.** `n` is itself a norm valuation (landed
`natDegree_mem_normValues`, via `N(π) = π^n`), and by Step 4 it divides all of them, so the
recognition principle `inertiaDegOf_eq_of` gives `inertiaDegOf f = n`, whence
`ramIndexOf f = n / n = 1` and `efPair f = (1, n)`. With Step 1, `typeOf f = ⟨{(1, n)}⟩`. ∎

**Hypothesis audit.** The proof never uses `[IsAdicComplete (maximalIdeal O) O]`. It is carried
because the signature is a frozen contract, and it is *not* redundant in spirit: completeness is
what makes `inertiaDegOf` mean the classical residue degree (the trust-boundary discussion in
`TypeOf.lean`). The identity proved here is true over any DVR.

## The other five routes, in one line each

* **CN-04** `efPair_pos_of_mem`. A member of `(typeOf f).data` is `efPair g` for a monic
  *irreducible* factor `g`; such a `g` has positive degree, so `normValues g ≠ ∅` and
  `inertiaDegOf g > 0`, and `ramIndexOf g = deg g / inertiaDegOf g > 0` by `Nat.div_pos` with
  `inertiaDegOf_dvd_natDegree`. The spec's optional companion `efPair_mul_le_natDegree` is
  included (each `eᵢfᵢ` is one summand of `∑ eᵢfᵢ = deg f`), because CN-22 needs it.
* **CN-15** `exists_anisotropic`. `rootPairMap : Sym2 K → K × K`, `s(r,s) ↦ (rs, r+s)`, is
  injective with image exactly the *non*-anisotropic pairs; if no pair were anisotropic it would
  be bijective, forcing `q(q+1) = 2q²` and so `q = 1`, impossible. Lift the resulting
  `(b̄₀, b̄₁)` through `residue_surjective` and read anisotropy of `![b₀, b₁]` off `aniForm_iff`
  after reducing the form mod `𝔪`. Sign convention checked against `quadForm`:
  `a 0 ↦ b₀`, `a 1 ↦ b₁`, matching `u² − a₁uv + a₀v²`. Characteristic two is not excluded.
* **CN-16** `card_certSet_gen`. Verbatim re-parametrization of leancheck's `card_certSet`:
  `readEquiv γ : Coeff O 2 N ≃ Res O N × Res O N` becomes `read γ : Coeff O n N ≃ D`. The
  bijection `(γ̄, d) ↦ (read (towerSection γ̄)).symm d` is injective by `huniq` and surjective by
  `hshift`, and `Nat.card_prod` + `card_res` give `q^m · #S`.
* **CN-17** `exists_monic_factorization_finset`. `Finset.induction_on` on `s`, with `f` (and the
  hypotheses naming it) universally quantified inside the motive by a private auxiliary — the
  `insert` step must apply the inductive hypothesis to the *cofactor*, not to `f`. The peeled
  block's coprimality to the product of the rest is `IsCoprime.prod_right` (mathlib form checked:
  `(∀ i ∈ t, IsCoprime x (s i)) → IsCoprime x (∏ i ∈ t, s i)`). The lifted family is
  `fun j => if j = i then g else gs j` — junk `1` off `s`, with **no clause pinning it**, per the
  spec's RISK (b). Base case: `f̄ = 1` and `f` monic force `f = 1`.
* **CN-22** `exists_coveringMenu`. CN-04 plus `typeOf_degree` give `1 ≤ e, f` and `e·f ≤ n`, hence
  `e, f ≤ n`, and `card ≤ n` because every summand of `∑ eᵢfᵢ = n` is `≥ 1`
  (`Multiset.card_nsmul_le_sum`). The menu is the image of the finite type
  `(k : Fin (n+1)) × Sym (Fin (n+1) × Fin (n+1)) k` — route (b) of the spec's RISK line, which
  worked as predicted. The witness is built by *clamping* (`min · n`), which is the identity on
  the pairs that occur, so no dependent-type bookkeeping is needed.

## Proof routes taken

* **CN-13** `dvd_sub_coeff_of_scaleRoots`. `coeff_scaleRoots` turns each hypothesis instance into
  `π^N ∣ (G'.coeff i − G.coeff i) · π^(m(n−i))` (the two exponents agree after `hdeg`), and the
  private kernel `dvd_of_mul_pow_dvd` cancels: with `k := m(n−i) ≤ P := m·n`, writing
  `N = (N−P) + ((P−k) + k)` and cancelling `π^k` in the domain gives `π^(N−P) ∣ …`. The `N ≤ P`
  branch is discharged by `π^0 = 1`. The docstring states explicitly that this is where the
  conclusion becomes vacuous (level exhausted), per the node's RISK line.
* **CN-12** `exists_monic_scaleRoots`. The side condition is first extended from `i < n` to all
  `i` (above the degree the exponent `m(n−i)` is `0`, so divisibility is free), then `choose`
  extracts quotients `c i` with `f.coeff i = π^(m(n−i)) · c i`. Two observations make the
  witness clean: `c` vanishes above `n` (there `c i = f.coeff i = 0`), and `c n = f.coeff n = 1`
  by monicity. So `G := ∑_{i ≤ n} monomial i (c i)` has `G.coeff = c` *everywhere*
  (`finsetSum_coeff` + `Finset.sum_ite_eq'`), is monic of degree exactly `n`
  (`monic_of_natDegree_le_of_coeff_eq_one`, `le_natDegree_of_ne_zero`), and
  `(G.scaleRoots (π^m)).coeff i = c i · (π^m)^(n−i) = f.coeff i`. `Classical.choice` enters
  exactly where the spec's RISK line predicted (the divisibility witnesses).
* **CN-12 corollary** `exists_monic_scaleRoots_typeOf` composes CN-12 with the landed
  `typeOf_scaleRoots` to give the depth-reduction statement the deep-tail recursion consumes:
  same degree, `typeOf f = typeOf G`.

## CN-19 — the informal derivation (written before the Lean)

**Claim.** `O` a commutative domain, `f ∈ O[X]` monic of degree `n`, `α = AdjoinRoot.root f` the
image of `X` in `A = O[X]/(f)`. Then `N_{A/O}(α) = (−1)ⁿ · f.coeff 0`, where the norm is the
determinant of multiplication-by-`α` on the free `O`-module `A`.

**Step 1 — `A` is free with power basis.** `f` monic gives `AdjoinRoot.powerBasis' hf :
PowerBasis O A` with `dim = n` and `gen = α`; the basis is `1, α, …, α^(n−1)`.

**Step 2 — the minimal polynomial of `α` over `O` is `f` itself.** `f` is monic and
`aeval α f = mk f f = 0`. Conversely, if `q ≠ 0` had `deg q < deg f` and `aeval α q = 0`, then
`PowerBasis.dim_le_degree_of_root` would give `n ≤ deg q`, contradicting `deg q < deg f = n`
(that lemma is exactly linear independence of `1, α, …, α^(n−1)`). By `minpoly.unique'`,
`f = minpoly O α`. **This is the step that is not in mathlib:** `AdjoinRoot.minpoly_root` is
stated over a *field* (`minpoly K (root f) = f · C f.leadingCoeff⁻¹`), and over a non-field base
irreducibility is unavailable — but monicity plus freeness is all one actually needs, and no
completeness or irreducibility hypothesis enters.

**Step 3 — the norm of a power-basis generator.** For any `pb : PowerBasis R S` over a
commutative base, `Algebra.PowerBasis.norm_gen_eq_coeff_zero_minpoly` gives
`N(pb.gen) = (−1)^pb.dim · (minpoly R pb.gen).coeff 0`. Its own proof is
`norm = det (leftMulMatrix)`, `det M = (−1)^n · M.charpoly.coeff 0`
(`Matrix.det_eq_sign_charpoly_coeff`) and `charpoly_leftMulMatrix` — i.e. mathlib already
performs, once and for all, the `n × n` determinant expansion that the node's RISK line feared
having to redo by hand.

**Step 4 — combine.** With `pb = powerBasis' hf` (`dim = n`, `gen = α`) and Step 2,
`N(α) = (−1)ⁿ · f.coeff 0`. ∎

**Route note.** This is the spec's route (ii), tried first as instructed; it succeeded, so route
(i) (companion-matrix determinant by first-row expansion, and the missing companion-matrix API)
was never needed. At `n = 2` the result specializes to the landed `norm_root_quad`
(`QuadCert.lean:175`), where `(−1)² = 1`.

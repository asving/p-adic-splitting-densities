**Date:** 2026-08-18.  **Node:** H.116b4.  **Artifact class:** OM-unit phase 1 (mathematics

## 0. Verdict and fence

The signed statement is the uniform cardinality of the fibres of the **raw pair-presentation
map**

\[
 ((b_p)_{p\in L},Q)\longmapsto
 \left[\prod_{p\in L}P_p(b_p)\,Q\right]_{\mathfrak m^N}.
\]

Here `P_p` is the landed `alphaParent` at the pinned centre and the map itself is the landed
`plantedPoly`.  The target is restricted by hypotheses to one beta genre `(L,D)`.  The desired
integer `G` may depend on all data preceding it in the signature, in particular on `L`, `r`,
`N`, and `D`, but not on the target class `c`.

There is a viable Smith-normal-form/Sylvester-lattice programme, but it is **not currently a
proof**.  Two genuinely open statements remain:

1. the invariant factors of the **restricted** multi-Sylvester lattice must be shown to be
   determined by the genre (a resultant computes the determinant, not the invariant factors);
2. the nonlinear multiplication fibre must be linearised, uniformly and exactly at every
   level through `N` (constant Jacobian/SNF data alone does not imply constant nonlinear fibre
   cardinality).

Both are stated explicitly below as open lemmas GR-7 and GR-9/GR-10.  No step below uses a
finite list of examples to justify a universal assertion.

This is the obstruction recorded in `CLOSING_CHAIN_AH6_2026-08-16.md`: after b4 and the
H.116b assembly land, `betaExtract_fiber_card` supplies the missing decay transfer in H.121
clause (iii); the existing beta census does not replace this count.

**Standing fence A-H.7/F1.**  This route never constructs a map from the child fibre over `t`
to the child fibre over `t'`.  In particular it does not use peel-shift-replant, a round trip,
or presentation stability.  D2/D3/E3 in the amendment rule out that route.

## 3. Structural reductions valid for every nonempty genre

Fix `c : Genre(L,D)`.  The following deductions are general and use only landed facts.

1. `IsBetaState π c` gives `¬ IsDrainState c` by definition.
2. For every `p ∈ L`, the genre equivalence supplies
   `HasChildAt π c p.1 p.2.1 p.2.2`.  Unfolding `HasChildAt` gives
   `2 ≤ p.1`, `1 ≤ p.2.1`, and `p.2.2 ≠ 0`.
3. If `p,p' ∈ L` have `p.2 = p'.2`, then the two child events are at the same `(k,z)`;
   `hasChildAt_mult_unique` gives `p.1 = p'.1`, hence `p=p'`.  Thus the `(k,z)` frames in `L`
   are pairwise distinct, including distinct residues for children of the same slope.
4. `betaChild_mult_lt` gives `p.1 < m` for every `p ∈ L`.
5. For every `p ∈ L`,

   \[
   p.2.1\,p.1\le \operatorname{betaContent}(c,p.2.1)=D(p)\le N-1.
   \]

   The first inequality is `mul_le_betaContent`; the equality is the genre hypothesis; the
   last inequality is `betaContent_le` applied to the non-drain state.  In particular all
   graded windows used in the ghost count are positive.
6. The degree identity needed by `plantedPoly_natDegree` is exactly the signed hypothesis
   `hdeg`; no degree-sum claim is reproved in b4.

If `Genre(L,D)` is empty, choose `G=1`; the universal conclusion is vacuous and `0<G` is
immediate.  Thus all remaining work may assume a chosen `c₀ : Genre(L,D)`.  This case split is
important: the signed theorem requires a positive `G` even for inconsistent `(L,D)`.

## 4. The two presentation levels

For `p=(μ,k,z)`, let

\[
 \iota_p : \operatorname{ClusterState}(O,\mu,N)\longrightarrow A_p(N)
\]

send a child class `d` to the level-`N` coefficient class of
`alphaParent π (classSect O μ N d.1) k (resSect O z)`.  Define `A_p(N)` to be the range of
this map.  A **raw presentation** uses `d`; an **image presentation** uses only `ι_p(d)`.

For a child event in a nonempty genre, Step 3.5 gives `μ*k ≤ N-1`.  From
`monicPoly_alphaParentCoeff` and `proj_alphaParentCoeff_eq_iff`, two raw child classes have the
same factor image exactly when coordinate `j : Fin μ` agrees modulo

\[
 \pi^{N-\mu k+k j}.
\]

Since both source coordinates live in `𝔪/𝔪^N`, the fibre cardinal is

\[
 \operatorname{ghost}(\mu,k)
   =\prod_{0\le j<\mu}q^{\mu k-kj}
   =q^{k\mu(\mu+1)/2}.
\]

This is a theorem for all `μ,k,N` satisfying `1≤N` and `μk≤N-1`: each coordinate is a fibre
of the quotient `Res O N → Res O (N-μk+kj)`, whose size follows from `card_res` and
surjectivity of `resFactor`; the finite product is `Nat.card_pi`.  No sampled instance is used.

The argument is not yet packaged under a landed name:

### OPEN GR-1 — per-child ghost count

```lean
theorem card_plantedFactor_fiber
    {μ N k : ℕ} (hN : 1 ≤ N) (hμ : 1 ≤ μ) (hw : μ * k ≤ N - 1)
    (z : ResidueField O) :
    ∃ ghost : ℕ, 0 < ghost ∧
      ∀ y ∈ Set.range (plantedFactorClass (O := O) π μ N k z),
        Nat.card {d : ClusterState O μ N //
          plantedFactorClass π μ N k z d = y} = ghost
```

The proof reduces completely to the landed lemmas named above.  A second clause may expose the
closed value `ghost = residueCard O ^ (k * (μ * (μ + 1) / 2))`; the final b4 theorem needs only
constancy and positivity.

Taking the dependent product over `L` gives a constant

\[
 \operatorname{Ghost}(L)=\prod_{p\in L}\operatorname{ghost}(p.1,p.2.1).
\]

The cofactor presentation has no ghost: `Qc.1` is recovered by applying `proj` to the low
coefficients of its canonical monic lift (`proj_classSect`).  Therefore, for every target `c`,

\[
 \#\operatorname{Pres}(c)=\operatorname{Ghost}(L)\cdot
 \#\operatorname{ImgPres}(c). \tag{4.1}
\]

Here `ImgPres(c)` is the fibre after replacing each raw child class by its factor image.

### OPEN GR-2 — product-of-constant-fibres bookkeeping

This is a finite-set lemma, not new number theory: a dependent product of maps with fibre sizes
`g_p` has fibre size `∏g_p`, and adjoining an injective coordinate changes no fibre size.  It
should be proved once with `Nat.card_eq_of_bijective`, `Nat.card_pi`, and a choice of one lift in
each range.  GR-2 plus GR-1 proves (4.1).

## 5. Eliminating the cofactor coordinate

Fix level-`N` monic child-factor classes `(P_p)`.  Their product `A=∏P_p` is monic.  For a fixed
target monic class `F`, there is at most one monic degree-`r` class `Q` with `A Q=F`: monic
polynomial division over `O/𝔪^N` has a unique quotient.  Thus `ImgPres(c)` is in bijection with
the tuples `(P_p)` for which that quotient exists and its low coefficients lie in the image of
`𝔪` (so it represents a `ClusterState O r N`).  Call this set `Fit(c)`.

### OPEN GR-3 — cofactor elimination modulo `𝔪^N`

```lean
theorem imagePresentation_equiv_fit (c : ClusterState O m N) :
  ImgPres π L r c ≃ Fit π L r c
```

This is a mechanical polynomial-division node.  Its proof must be over the quotient ring, not by
cancelling in `O[X]` (the quotient has zero divisors).  The divisor is monic, so uniqueness does
not require a domain.  `plantedPoly_monic`, `plantedPoly_natDegree`,
`monicPoly_coeff_self`, and `proj_classSect` discharge the conversions to the landed carrier.

At this point the exact question is: why does `#Fit(c)` depend only on `(L,D,N,r)`?

There is also a necessary directionality check.  Landed `plantedPoly_genre` proves that a
cofactor satisfying the child-free and root-free hypotheses produces the prescribed child
profile.  It does **not**, as stated, prove the converse.  Since `Pres(c)` counts every pair
mapping to `c`, b4 must show that the cofactor in every such pair is admissible.

### OPEN GR-3b — admissibility of every cofactor in a genre presentation

```lean
theorem cofactor_admissible_of_plantedPresentation
    (c : ClusterState O m N) (h0 : ¬ IsDrainState c)
    (hchildren : ∀ p, HasChildAt π c p.1 p.2.1 p.2.2 ↔ p ∈ L)
    (hcontent : ∀ p ∈ L, betaContent c p.2.1 = D p)
    (bb : ∀ p : {y // y ∈ L}, ClusterState O p.1.1 N)
    (Qc : ClusterState O r N)
    (hc : proj O m N (fun i => (plantedPoly π L bb Qc).coeff i) = c.1) :
  (∀ ν k z, ¬ HasChildAt π Qc ν k z) ∧
  (∀ p ∈ L, ∀ aQ, proj O r N aQ = Qc.1 →
    ¬ π^(betaContent Qc p.2.1 + 1) ∣
      ((monicPoly aQ).comp
        (C (π^p.2.1) * (X + C (resSect O p.2.2)))).coeff 0) ∧
  (∀ p ∈ L,
    D p = (∑ p' ∈ L, p'.1 * min p'.2.1 p.2.1) + betaContent Qc p.2.1)
```

The proof should run the landed exact frame profile `plantedPoly_frame_profile` backwards.
At a member of `L`, a positive cofactor abscissa would raise the target multiplicity above the
unique multiplicity supplied by `hchildren` (`hasChildAt_mult_unique`), proving root-freeness.
At a foreign frame, a cofactor abscissa at least two would give an extra target child through
`hasChildAt_of_exists`, contradicting the `↔`; at an occupied frame it would again raise the
unique multiplicity.  The same profile and `betaContent_eq_of_recentre_exact` give the displayed
content identity.  Each implication is general over all frames and all lifts.  Until this
reverse lemma is proved, restricting `Fit(c)` to an admissible cofactor cell would omit
potential presentations and would be a gap.

## 6. The planted pairwise resultant

For actual lifts `b_p` with all coordinates in `𝔪`, put

```text
P_p = alphaParent π b_p k_p (resSect O z_p).
```

For distinct members `p,p' ∈ L`, the expected exact formula is

\[
 v_\pi(\operatorname{Res}(P_p,P_{p'}))
   =\mu_p\mu_{p'}\min(k_p,k_{p'}). \tag{6.1}
\]

This is independent of all deep child digits.

A root-free proof is preferable in Lean.  Undo `alphaParent` using `alphaParent_coeff` and the
translation/scaling laws for the resultant.  If `k_p=k_{p'}`, factor the common scale; after
division, the reductions are `(X-z_p)^μ_p` and `(X-z_p')^μ_p'`, whose resultant is a unit because
`z_p≠z_p'`.  If, say, `k_p<k_p'`, scale at `k_p`; the first normalized factor has roots in the
nonzero residue class `z_p`, while the second reduces at that scale to a power of `X`, so the
normalized resultant is again a unit.  The extracted scale is exactly the right side of (6.1).
This treats all lifts uniformly and does not assume the polynomials split over `O`.

\[
 v_\pi\!\left(\prod_{p<p'}\operatorname{Res}(P_p,P_{p'})\right)
 =\sum_{p<p'}\mu_p\mu_{p'}\min(k_p,k_{p'}). \tag{6.2}
\]

Equation (6.2) is a determinant valuation.  It is not yet a fibre count.

## 7. The restricted multi-Sylvester lattice

Let `s=∑p∈L p.1`.  For a tuple `P=(P_p)`, the ordinary multi-Sylvester differential is

\[
 T_P((\delta_p)_p)=
   \sum_p\delta_p\prod_{p'\ne p}P_{p'}
   \quad:\quad
   \bigoplus_p O[X]_{<\mu_p}\longrightarrow O[X]_{<s}. \tag{7.1}
\]

The relevant source is not the whole direct sum.  After quotienting out GR-1's ghosts, a
factor can vary only in the image lattice `Λ_p` of the landed shear `alphaParentCoeff`.
Consequently the b4 map uses

\[
 T_P^{\mathrm{pl}}:\bigoplus_p\Lambda_p\longrightarrow \Lambda_{\mathrm{prod}}, \tag{7.2}
\]

where the target lattice is the coefficient lattice left after monic division eliminates the
cofactor.  GR-3b first shows that every cofactor occurring in the counted fibre is admissible.
Both lattices need explicit bases.  In those bases, (7.2) is the
"Sylvester matrix restricted to the planted perturbation lattice" named by A-H.7.

### OPEN GR-5 — multi-Sylvester determinant identity

Define `multiSylvester` by (7.1).  Induction on `L`, using the two-factor
`Polynomial.sylvesterMap`, `Polynomial.toMatrix_sylvesterMap'`, and
`Polynomial.resultant_mul_left/right`, should prove

```lean
theorem det_multiSylvester :
  LinearMap.det (multiSylvester P) =
    unitSign P * ∏ e : {e // e ∈ L.offDiag},
      (P e.1).resultant (P e.2) e.1.1 e.2.1
```

with `unitSign P` a unit.  The precise unordered-pair index should be chosen before coding.
This is general commutative algebra; it does not contain the b4 research step.

### OPEN GR-6 — bases for the planted source and quotient target lattices

The source basis is obtained by writing `alphaParent_coeff` as an invertible triangular shear
followed by the diagonal grades `π^(k(μ-j))`; `proj_alphaParentCoeff_eq_iff` is the already
landed congruence-level statement of exactly this fact.  Monic long division gives the target
basis and shows that an admissible cofactor acts by an automorphism on the normalized local
quotients at the child frames.  The last assertion is where GR-3b's root-free conclusion is
used.  Its content levels are read from

\[
 D(p)=\sum_{p'\in L}\mu_{p'}\min(k_{p'},k_p)
       +\operatorname{betaContent}(Q,k_p),
\]

the landed content clause of `plantedPoly_genre`; hence every lattice shift involving `Q` is
genre-determined.

GR-6 must include both directions: every normalized unit action preserves the lattice, and its
inverse does too.  Merely knowing that a determinant is a unit is insufficient for a claimed
lattice equality.

### OPEN GR-7 — restricted Smith invariants are genre-determined (research core I)

```lean
theorem plantedSylvester_smith_invariants
    (c : GenreData O π m N L D) (x : ImgPres π L r c.1) :
  ∃ e : Fin (∑ p ∈ L, p.1) → ℕ,
    RestrictedSylvesterCoker π L D N x ≃ₗ[O]
      (⨁ i, O ⧸ Ideal.span {π ^ e i}) ∧
    e = gaugeExponents L D N
```

The right side must be a function of the genre data and not of `c` or `x`.  A proof must give
explicit DVR row/column operations after GR-6, or compute the determinantal ideals of every
minor.  GR-4 and GR-5 prove only

```text
sum e_i = valuation(det) = sum of pairwise resultant valuations
```

when no truncation intervenes.  That equality alone does **not** determine the multiset `e_i`
and is not an acceptable proof of GR-7.  One may weaken the conclusion to
`∀ i, min (e i) N = fixedExponent i` because only the truncated kernel is counted, but that
truncated multiset still has to be proved constant.

The Smith construction itself is available from mathlib.  The new mathematics is the equality
with `gaugeExponents`.  `ChapB/B50.exists_smith_of_norm` is the implementation template, not a
substitute for this calculation.

### OPEN GR-8 — cardinal of the truncated linear gauge

Given GR-7, Smith diagonalization and `card_res` give, for all `N`,

\[
 \#\ker(T_P^{\mathrm{pl}}\bmod\pi^N)
   =q^{\sum_i\min(e_i,N)}. \tag{7.3}
\]

This is a mechanical general lemma about a full-rank map of finite free DVR modules:

```lean
theorem card_kernel_mod_pow_of_smith
    (T : (Fin s → O) →ₗ[O] (Fin s → O))
    (hT : Function.Injective T) (e : Fin s → ℕ) (hsmith : HasSmithExponents π T e) :
  Nat.card {x : Fin s → Res O N // mapModPow T x = 0} =
    residueCard O ^ (∑ i, min (e i) N)
```

The proof uses the landed Smith basis API, `Nat.card_pi`, and `card_res`.  It proves a universal
formula, not just the cases used by b4.

## 8. From the linear lattice to the nonlinear multiplication fibre

For a base presentation `P`, write a second tuple as `P_p+δ_p`.  Then

\[
 \prod_p(P_p+\delta_p)-\prod_pP_p
 =T_P(\delta)+\sum_{|I|\ge2}
   \left(\prod_{i\in I}\delta_i\right)
   \left(\prod_{j\notin I}P_j\right). \tag{8.1}
\]

The second sum is nonlinear.  It cannot be discarded merely because every `δ_p` lies in a
positive-depth lattice: some Smith directions are themselves nonunits, and the required depth
comparison must be proved coordinate by coordinate.

### OPEN GR-9 — one-level filtered linearisation (research core II-a)

Choose the filtration supplied by the planted bases of GR-6.  Prove that for every admissible
presentation and every level `n<N`:

1. the derivative of the multiplication map on the `n`-th graded piece is the reduction of
   `T_P^pl`;
2. replacing `P` by another lift with the same level-`n` data changes that derivative only by
   filtration-preserving invertible row/column operations;
3. all terms of degree at least two in (8.1) land one filtration step above the obstruction
   being solved;
4. multiplication by the normalized cofactor is an automorphism on the obstruction module
   (GR-6's root-free use).

A useful signature packages the actual lifting tree rather than asserting a vague inverse
function theorem:

```lean
theorem card_factorisation_lifts_succ
    (y : FitAtLevel π L r n) (Fnext : TargetLift y (n+1)) :
  Nat.card {y' : FitAtLevel π L r (n+1) // trunc y' = y ∧ product y' = Fnext} =
    if ObstructionVanishes y Fnext then
      card (kernel (restrictedSylvesterOnGrade π L D n)) else 0
```

The formula must hold for every `y` and `Fnext`; existence at a final presentation is not enough
to show that all intermediate branches lift equally.

### OPEN GR-10 — iteration and exact gauge equivalence (research core II-b)

Iterate GR-9 from the forced residue-level factor tuple (each planted factor reduces to a power
of `X`, with the frame labels carried by the weighted lattice) through level `N`.  GR-7 makes
the nonzero branching numbers genre-determined.  The conclusion should be either an explicit
equivalence or at least an exact cardinality:

```lean
theorem card_fit_eq_gauge
    (c : GenreData O π m N L D) :
  Nat.card (Fit π L r c.1) = gaugeCard O L D N
```

with


## 9. Positivity and final assembly of H.116b4

Assume `c₀ : Genre(L,D)`.

### OPEN GR-11 — package a landed peel as a `plantedPoly` presentation

Apply `exists_peel_finset` to `c₀` and the canonical lift `classSect O m N c₀.1`.  It gives
actual child lifts `b_p`, a monic `Q`, an exact factorization, and the degree equation.
Reduce the factorization modulo `𝔪^N` and define the child classes by `proj O p.1 N b_p`.
To define the cofactor state, show the low coefficients of `Q` lie in `𝔪`: reduce the exact
factorization modulo `𝔪`; `monicPoly_map_residue` gives `X^m`, every
`alphaParent_map_residue` gives `X^μp`, and cancellation in the residue-field polynomial ring
gives `Q mod 𝔪 = X^r`.  Then replace all lifts by their canonical `classSect`s; congruence
modulo `𝔪^N`, `alphaParent_coeff`, and `proj_eq_iff_pow_dvd` show that the planted product has
class `c₀.1`.

Package this as:

```lean
theorem exists_plantedPresentation_of_genre
    (c : ClusterState O m N) (hβ : IsBetaState π c)
    (hchildren : ∀ p, HasChildAt π c p.1 p.2.1 p.2.2 ↔ p ∈ L) :
  Nonempty {x :
      (∀ p : {y // y ∈ L}, ClusterState O p.1.1 N) × ClusterState O r N //
    proj O m N (fun i => (plantedPoly π L x.1 x.2).coeff i) = c.1}
```

This is a mechanical corollary of the landed b1 theorem, but no declaration of this shape is
currently landed.

Now set

\[
 G=\operatorname{Ghost}(L)\cdot\operatorname{gaugeCard}(O,L,D,N).
\]

GR-1 gives `Ghost(L)>0`; GR-11 also directly shows `Pres(c₀)` is inhabited; GR-10 gives the
positive gauge count.  For an arbitrary `c : Genre(L,D)`, the chain

```text
#Pres(c)
  = Ghost(L) * #ImgPres(c)                         (GR-1, GR-2)
  = Ghost(L) * #Fit(c)                            (GR-3)
  = Ghost(L) * gaugeCard(O,L,D,N)                 (GR-10)
  = G
```

proves the universal clause.  Together with the empty-genre `G=1` case, this proves the exact
signed theorem.

Notice what is **not** used in this final line: `plantedPoly_genre` and
`not_isCSState_plantedPoly_swap`.  They are needed by H.116b's soundness assembly, not by the
cardinality of the already-specified fibres in H.116b4.

## The defect in one paragraph

The committed signature lets the development coefficients `A j m` have ANY degree, while the
carrier's coefficient link `hcoeff` speaks only about polynomials of degree `< C.D`. So the floors
`hgt (A j m) ≥ (μ₂ − m)·λ_j` can be satisfied by a polynomial whose visible (constant-coefficient)
part is tiny and whose remaining part carries the entire discrepancy — the partial sums then need
not converge to anything, and `F` need not be a `μ₂`-th power. Separately, the committed signature
says nothing about `deg Ψ`, so `Ψ − W` may be a unit in the fraction field, whose `μ₂`-th power IS
squarefree. Both are frame data present in the SOURCE (`EFF.HE7.53`: the `A_m` are `Φ′`-development
coefficients of degree `< D″`, and `Ψ` is the current key of degree `D″`) and dropped by the
signature.

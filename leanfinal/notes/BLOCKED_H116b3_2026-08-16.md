## 2. THE DEFECT — A-H.7 §6, PROOF step 1, is false as written

Verbatim from the amendment:

> 1. **The difference is one digit above the polygon, coefficientwise.** … So the summand's
>    Newton polygon lies at least `1` above `P(b_p)`'s at every abscissa, hence
>    `v(δ_j) ≥ 1 + npHgt(monicPoly a)(j)` for all `j`.

At `φ = X` the Lean `npHgt` is the **coefficient valuation**, not the hull height —
B.15's landed `npHgt_X : npHgt X f j = addVal O (f.coeff j)`. With that reading the displayed
inequality is FALSE, and the smallest counterexample is inside the genre:

* `O = ℤ₂`, one child `p = (μ, k, z) = (2, 1, 1)`, `Q = 1`. Then
  `P(b) = alphaParent π b 1 ŵ` has `alphaFrame b ŵ ≡ (Y − 1)² = Y² − 2Y + 1 (mod 𝔪)`, so
  `alphaFrame_1 ≡ −2 ≡ 0`, i.e. `v(P(b)_1) ≥ 2`.
* The landed `pow_min_succ_dvd_coeff_recentre_alphaParent_sub` (H116bR §3) gives only
  `v(δ_1) ≥ 1 + k(μ − 1) = 2`.
* So `v(δ_1) ≥ 2 = 1 + 1` while `npHgt X f 1 = v(f_1) ≥ 2`, i.e.
  `v(δ_1) ≥ 1 + npHgt X f 1` is **not** available: the difference is one digit above the
  **HULL** (height `k(μ − j) = 1` at `j = 1`), not one digit above the coefficient.

The consequence is not that the node is false — abscissa `1` is strictly ABOVE the hull on both
sides, so it never sits on a side and the polygon is unmoved. The consequence is that the proof
must be run against the **hull profile**, and the hull of `plantedPoly` is the Minkowski sum of
the hulls of its factors. **That Minkowski identity is the whole missing content**, and A-H.7
§6's DEPENDS field (H.109, H.110, H.115b, the ChapB polygon API `sideSet`/`sideMin`/`npHgt`/
`resPoly`/`OnSide`/`suppVal`, and H116bR's `pow_min_succ_dvd_coeff_recentre_alphaParent_sub`)
does not contain it. Its **SIZE estimate of 60 lines is wrong by roughly a factor of six.**

Two further reasons the naive reading cannot be repaired in place:

* **B.77's congruence package does not apply.** `suppVal_congr` / `sideSet_congr` (B77a) and
  `resPoly_congr` (B77b) transport the polygon along `π ^ N ∣ (f − f').coeff i`. Here
  `bb` and `bb'` are DIFFERENT level-`N` classes, so `δ` is only `π ¹`-deep relative to the
  planted floor; the hypothesis of B.77 fails at every cell of the genre.
* **`IsCSState` is `∀`-over-lifts.** `hcs` with `h0` yields only `∃ a₀` lift of `c` with no CS
  witness, and `a₀` need not be the planted lift. Turning that into "no witness at the planted
  lift" is a separate (easy, but real) lift-independence step — B.77 DOES apply there, since
  two lifts of one class differ by `π ^ N` and non-drain is exactly `Visible π X · N` (B.75).

## 3. THE REPAIRED PROOF (complete, informal; nothing below is formalized)

Write `P := ∏_{p ∈ L} alphaParent π (classSect (bb p)) k_p ẑ_p`, `P'` the same with `bb'`,
`Q := monicPoly (classSect Qc)`, so `f := plantedPoly π L bb Qc = P·Q`, `f' = P'·Q`, and
`δ := f' − f = (P' − P)·Q`. Fix `(u, ℓ)` with `0 < ℓ` and `Nat.Coprime u ℓ` (`IsCSState`'s
own side data), and write `S := suppVal X f u ℓ`.

**(3a) The planted hull, exactly.** For one planted factor `Pₚ := alphaParent π b k ẑ`
(`b` `𝔪`-valued, `1 ≤ k`, `z ≠ 0`):

* `v(Pₚ_j) ≥ k(μ − j)` for `j ≤ μ` (`alphaParent_coeff`), with EQUALITY at `j = 0`
  (`alphaFrame_coeff_zero_not_dvd`, valuation exactly `kμ`) and at `j = μ` (monic, valuation 0);
* hence `suppVal X Pₚ u ℓ = μ · min (ℓk, u)`, attained at abscissa `0` when `ℓk ≤ u` and at
  abscissa `μ` otherwise (the function `j ↦ ℓk(μ−j) + uj` is affine, so its min over `[0, μ]`
  is at an endpoint);
* `v((P'ₚ − Pₚ)_j) ≥ 1 + k(μ − j)` — this is exactly the landed
  `pow_min_succ_dvd_coeff_recentre_alphaParent_sub` read at the trivial frame, or directly
  `alphaParent_coeff` plus `alphaFrame b' ẑ ≡ alphaFrame b ẑ (mod 𝔪)`.

**(3b) The Minkowski identity — THE MISSING BRICK, and it IS in the corpus.**
`Uniformity/ChapB/B39a.lean` lands the **general graded product law**, purity-free:

```
suppVal_mul_gen, npHgt_mul_gen, sideMin_mul_gen, sideDeg_mul_gen, resPoly_mul_gen
  (hπ) (hφ : IsKey φ) (hu : 0 < u) (hℓ : 0 < ℓ) (hcop : Nat.Coprime u ℓ)
  (htf htz : suppVal ≠ ⊤) (hnf hnz : sideSet nonempty) (hHf hHz : heights at sideMin)
```

Iterating `suppVal_mul_gen` over `L.attach` and once more against `Q` gives
`S = Σ_{p} μ_p·min(ℓk_p, u) + suppVal X Q u ℓ`, i.e. exactly
`S = min_j (ℓ·Λ(j) + u·j)` for the hull profile
`Λ(j) := min { Σ_p k_p(μ_p − j_p) + v(Q_{j₀}) : j₀ + Σ_p j_p = j }`.
**This is the step B.33's superadditivity alone cannot supply** (it gives `S ≥ …`, the wrong
direction) **and B.35d's `suppVal_mul_of_pure` cannot supply either** (a planted factor is
`(u,ℓ)`-pure only at `u = ℓk_p`, so mixed-slope `L` is out of its reach).

**(3c) The perturbation estimate.** From (3a) and the convolution bound,
`v(δ_j) ≥ 1 + Λ(j)` for every `j`, hence for every `j`
`ℓ·v(δ_j) + u·j ≥ ℓ + (ℓΛ(j) + uj) ≥ ℓ + S > S`.

**(3d) The transport (a new lemma, the analogue of B77a's `npHgt_dichotomy`).**
If `ℓ·v(δ_j) + u·j > S` for all `j` and `f'.natDegree = f.natDegree`, then:
at an abscissa with `ℓ·v(f_j) + uj = S` one has `v(δ_j) > v(f_j)` so `v(f'_j) = v(f_j)`;
at an abscissa with `ℓ·v(f_j) + uj > S` one has `ℓ·v(f'_j) + uj > S`. Therefore
`suppVal X f' u ℓ = S`, `sideSet X f' u ℓ = sideSet X f u ℓ`, hence `sideMin`, `sideMax`,
`sideDeg` agree; and `resCoeff` agrees because it reads `digAt π (H₀ − uk)` at the lattice
abscissae, where `H₀ − uk` is the LINE height `H` there and `v(δ) ≥ Λ ≥ H` gives
`π^(H+1) ∣ δ`, so B77b's `resMk_congr` applies digit by digit. Hence
`resPoly π X f' u ℓ h' H₀ = resPoly π X f u ℓ h H₀`.

**(3e) The `u = 0` branch, separately.** `B39a`'s product law carries `hu : 0 < u`, so `u = 0`
(forced by `hcop` to `ℓ = 1`) is not covered. It is however the easy case: `P ≡ P' ≡ X^{Σμ_p}
(mod 𝔪)` by `alphaParent_map_residue`, so `f ≡ f' (mod π)` COEFFICIENTWISE, `suppVal X f 0 1 =
0` (both monic), and the whole side datum at `(0,1)` is read at height `0`, i.e. off
`f mod π = f' mod π`.

**(3f) The lift plumbing.** `hcs` + `h0` give a lift `a₀` of `c` with no CS witness; B.77 (with
`Visible` supplied by `h0` through `isDrainState_iff_resOrd_eq`/`visible_iff_npHgt_lt`) moves
that to the planted lift of `c`; (3d) moves it across the swap; B.77 again spreads it over all
lifts of `c'`; `h0'` closes `¬ IsCSState π c'`.

## How piece 6 dissolved (the `b = m − 1` collision)

The BLOCKED record expected a case split on the merged digit
`g·π^{v₀}((−1)^m ĉ₂^m ω^{m i₂/D′} − ĉ₀)`, whose valuation may rise or be infinite. It is not
needed, for two independent reasons:

1. **Lower bound.** The two colliding deposits sit at the *same* stage height (both give
   `e₁α + a + δ + b·u₂ + E₂` after the `+ u₂b′` term), so
   `KeyFrame.min_stageHeight_le_add` already yields `≥ M₀` for the merged digit. Whether the
   merged unit is a unit, deeper, or zero is irrelevant to a lower bound.
2. **Attainment.** The `inf` is attained at coordinate `b′ = 0`, which is in range for ANY
   polynomial (`Finset.range (natDegree + 1)` always contains `0`) and which the `P`-chain
   deposit never reaches, since it sits at `b′ = m − 1` and `m ≥ 2` from `T.hcomp`.

So the pin is exact even at the degraded collision, and no `hcomp`-derived divisibility
`D′ ∣ m i₂` is consumed anywhere in the Lean proof.

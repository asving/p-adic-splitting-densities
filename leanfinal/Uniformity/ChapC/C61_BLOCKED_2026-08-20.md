## Two determinations made here (both machine-checked, both correcting written text)

1. **C.61 does NOT need `D′ ∣ deg g`.** The blueprint's STATEMENT reads the degree pin
   `deg g = L.keyDeg₂ = (e₁ℓ)(f₁d_r)` off "C.26's degree law + C.33's degree clause". That is not
   a consequence of C.61's own hypotheses: `hasLabel_natDegree_dvd` was REFUTED the same day
   (`C35B_D13_REFUTED_2026-08-20.md`, `g₀ = x³−2x+4` at `s2Frame` with `e₁f₁ = 2 ∤ 3`), so
   `HasLabel` supplies only the FLOOR identity `deg g / D′ = ℓ·d_r`
   (`natDegree_div_eq_of_isDvPure`, C.35). The repair, executed in `tier1_typeOf_of_ramLeg`: the
   two legs supply the divisibility themselves — `E ∣ e'` and `D ∣ f'` on every member make
   `E·D ∣ Σ e'f' = deg g` via `typeOf_degree`, and the floor identity then pins the cofactor to
   `1`, giving `deg g = E·D` exactly. **The degree pin is a theorem of the node, not a rider it
   must carry** — and `tier1_natDegree_eq` proves it at every `L.ℓ` without `hram` at all, using
   only the `F.e₁` half of the leg.
2. **`hx` is load-bearing and SHARP.** Deleting it (equivalently: deleting its downstream C.59
   content) makes the node FALSE — `tier1_typeOf_without_hx_false`. `g₀`'s x-polygon at
   `(h, e₁) = (1,2)` has cleared support `3`, attained only at `j ∈ {1,3}`, so
   `0 ∉ sideSet X g₀ 1 2`; correspondingly `ramIndexOf (x+2) = 1` is not divisible by
   `F.e₁ * L.ℓ = 2`.

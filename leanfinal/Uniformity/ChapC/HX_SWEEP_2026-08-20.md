## 3. The ready-made probe — why this is cheap to settle

`g₀ = x³ − 2x + 4 = x·Φ′ + 2²` at `(s2Frame, L₀)` over ℤ_[2] is **exactly a witness that
"labelled but NOT x-pure" is inhabited**:

* `hx_g₀_false` — LANDED (`ChapC/C61.lean`): the x-purity guard FAILS at `g₀`.

So `g₀` sits precisely in the gap between the two columns of §2. **For any signed statement
whose conclusion genuinely requires x-purity but whose signature omits it, `g₀` is an
immediate counterexample candidate** — no new frame construction needed, and every surrounding
lemma is already landed and exercised (three separate results today rest on this frame: the
D13 refutation, C.61's sharpness certificate, and C.27's refutation).

**The falsification program**, one unit, cheap because the frame is reusable: instantiate each
of the three UNEXAMINED statements at `(s2Frame, L₀, g₀)` and check whether the conclusion
fails. Each outcome is a genuine result:

* **conclusion fails** ⟹ the signed statement is FALSE and the fault is the missing guard;
  record it, propose adding `hx`, and stop (a re-sign is OWNER-GATED).
* **conclusion holds** ⟹ that statement does not need x-purity; the guard's absence is
  correct there, and the sweep's correlation is *not* a systematic defect. Equally valuable —
  it bounds the problem.

## 4. ⚠ CAUTION — what this sweep does NOT establish

**Being label-layer without an x-purity guard does not make a statement false.** Whether the
guard is needed depends on whether the conclusion actually requires purity, and that is a
per-statement question. This sweep produces a CANDIDATE LIST with a cheap probe; it is not
evidence of six defects, and it must not be cited as such.

Two of the six are already BLOCKED for causes that have nothing to do with `hx`
(`exists_dv_residual_dissection` at the dv-Hensel frontier; `blockFactor_spec` at D13, now
known inseparable from C.34), so the honest unexamined count is **three**.

Also not established: that the three statements which DO carry the guard carry it *correctly
scoped*. C.61's is proved undroppable; the other two are unexamined in that direction.

## 5. Verdict, stated at the strength the evidence supports

**Suggestive, not settled — and now cheaply testable.** 3 of 159 signed chapter-C obligations
carry an x-purity guard; one statement that lacks it is refuted today precisely for lacking
it; one that has it is proved to need it. That is a real pattern worth chasing, and the probe
costs one unit because `g₀` and its two lemmas are already landed. It is NOT yet a finding of
systematic spec defect, and this record does not claim to be one.

**Nothing here touches a signed statement.** A re-sign is owner-gated; this is a reading of
the spec, not a repair of it.

# §6 THE THREE CANDIDATES, ANALYSED (orchestrator, same day)

The falsification program of §3 was run as an ANALYSIS pass over the three unexamined
statements. Result: they do **not** behave alike, and only one is a live refutation
candidate. This narrows §2's correlation considerably and is the reason §4's caution was

## 6.1 `fullSide_block` (L1965) — NOT A CANDIDATE: protected by its own hypothesis

It carries `hdeg : f.natDegree = μ₂ * T.D₂` as a **hypothesis**. At the probe frame
`T.D₂ = 2` and `deg g₀ = 3`, so no `μ₂` satisfies `3 = 2μ₂`: **`g₀` cannot instantiate this
statement at all.** The exact-degree precondition is exactly the guard whose absence breaks
the others, and here it is present and explicit.

Independently, it quantifies over a `TowerDatum`, and **no `TowerDatum` value is landed
anywhere in the corpus**, so its locus is unwitnessed regardless (the C.73 disclosure).

## 6.2 `side_degree_conservation` (L2349) — EXPECTED TRUE, not a candidate

Conclusion: `dvSideLen F f u ℓ hne = ℓ * (dvResPoly …).natDegree`. At `(s2Frame, g₀, u = 3,
ℓ = 1)` this reads `dvSideLen = 1 * deg ρ = 1`, and the side runs from abscissa `0` to
abscissa `⌊deg g₀ / D′⌋ = 1` (`dvside_g₀ : dvSideSet … = {0, 1}`, landed in C35b). So the
claim is consistent at the probe.

More importantly it is **structurally immune**: the conclusion is about the side's LENGTH and
the residual's DEGREE — both floor-level quantities — and never asserts an exact degree of
`f`. The D13 refutation kills exact-degree reads, not floor-level ones. Its hypotheses are
`hf : f.Monic` and `hpure : IsDvPure`, with no `HasLabel`, so it does not even reach the
provenance gap that `HasLabel` hides.

## 6.3 ★ `multiplicity_tie` (L2285) — LIVE REFUTATION CANDIDATE

Its first conjunct is
```
(blockFactor L f).natDegree = (F.e₁ * F.f₁) * L.ℓ * (L.r.natDegree * m)
```
i.e. `deg (blockFactor L f) = L.keyDeg₂ * m` — **precisely the exact-degree form that D13's
refutation kills**, and the same shape as C.40's blocked clause (iii)
(`fS'.natDegree = L.keyDeg₂ * (mult₂ L f − 1)`).

Predicted refutation at the probe frame, with `f := g₀` and `m := 1`:
* `hmult` at `m = 1` HOLDS: `L₀.r = ρ = dvResPoly … g₀ …`, so `ρ¹ ∣ ρ` ✓, and `¬ ρ² ∣ ρ` ✓
  since `deg ρ = 1 < 2 = deg ρ²`.
* `blockFactor L₀ g₀ = g₀`: `g₀` is a `HasLabel` divisor of itself (`hasLabel_g₀`, landed),
  and EVERY labelled divisor of `g₀` divides `g₀` trivially, so `g₀` satisfies
  `blockFactor`'s maximality existential; two maximal such divisors divide each other and are
  monic, hence equal.
* Then conjunct 1 demands `3 = 2 * 1 * (1 * 1) = 2`. **FALSE.**
* Note conjunct 2 (`mult₂ L f = m`) is **TRUE** here: `mult₂ = ⌊3/2⌋ = 1 = m`. So the
  statement fails on the EXACT-degree conjunct while its FLOOR conjunct survives — the
  cleanest possible illustration of the D13 mechanism, and further evidence that the
  floor/exact distinction is the real fault line, not x-purity as such.

**FORMALIZATION GAP, stated honestly: this is ANALYSIS, not a theorem.** Landing it needs
three pieces, of which one is genuinely missing:
1. `BlockContext L₀ g₀` — needs `g₀.Monic` (landed), `¬ key ∣ g₀` (landed, `g₀_not_dvd`),
   the side data (landed: `hne_g₀`, `dvhgt_g₀_*`, `dvside_g₀`, `0 < dvSideDeg`), the residual

   probe frame `disc(x³ − 2x + 4) = −4(−2)³ − 27·4² = 32 − 432 = −400 ≠ 0`, so `g₀` is
   squarefree mathematically; the Lean proof for a concrete cubic over a DVR is the one real
   piece of work here.
2. `blockFactor L₀ g₀ = g₀` — the maximality/uniqueness argument above, through
   `blockFactor`'s `Classical.choice` (mutual divisibility + monic ⟹ equal).
3. The arithmetic `3 ≠ 2`.

## 6.4 Revised verdict on the sweep

The §2 correlation is **real but narrower than it looked**, and its true content is not

* 1 of 3 unexamined candidates is a live refutation candidate (`multiplicity_tie`);
* 1 is protected by an explicit exact-degree hypothesis (`fullSide_block`) — showing the

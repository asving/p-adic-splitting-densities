## 4. ⚠ CAUTION — what this sweep does NOT establish

**Being label-layer without an x-purity guard does not make a statement false.** Whether the
guard is needed depends on whether the conclusion actually requires purity, and that is a
per-statement question. This sweep produces a CANDIDATE LIST with a cheap probe; it is not
evidence of six defects, and it must not be cited as such.

Two of the six are already BLOCKED for causes that have nothing to do with `hx`
(`exists_dv_residual_dissection` at the dv-Hensel frontier; `blockFactor_spec` at D13, now
known inseparable from C.34), so the honest unexamined count is **three**.

Also not established: that the three statements which DO carry the guard carry it *correctly
scoped*. C.61's is proved undroppable; the other two are unexamined in that direction.

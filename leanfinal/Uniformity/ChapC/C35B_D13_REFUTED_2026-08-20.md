### Why the purity clause cannot see `deg g mod D′`

`IsDvPure` names the top abscissa by FLOOR division (`⌊deg g / D′⌋`), and the level polygon of
an odd-degree `g` at a `D′ = 2` frame simply has no lattice point at the fractional abscissa
`deg g / 2`. The purity here is not an endpoint artifact — `g₀`'s level polygon IS one-sided —
the lost information is provenance: `g₀ = (x + ⋯)·(quadratic θ-like factor)` mixes a valuation-1
root into an odd degree, and the factor-level surrogate `HasLabel` (DECISION C-D1) cannot
detect it. This makes C.29's standing FAITHFULNESS warning ("the root-side reading is NOT
stated and must not be assumed") *executable*.

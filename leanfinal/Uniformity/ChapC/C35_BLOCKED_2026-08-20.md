## Obstruction inventory

1. **The labelled slope factor is not obtainable, even with the A-C.8 cites.** The natural
   route is: `hctx` gives `f` monic with `¬ F.key ∣ f`, so the LANDED gate-(b) cite
   `exists_dvDissection` (allowlisted) yields `D : DvDissection F f`; `D.hsides` fires at
   `(L.u, L.ℓ)` (positivity `L.hℓ`, coprimality `L.hcop`, floor from `L.hκ` up to an
   associativity bridge, and the genuine side from `hctx`'s `0 < dvSideDeg` clause), so
   `(L.u, L.ℓ) ∈ D.slopes` and `G := D.factor (L.u, L.ℓ)` is monic, `IsDvPure`, and divides
   `f`. **The route stops there.** `HasLabel L G` additionally requires
   `dvResPoly … G … = L.r ^ m`, and `hctx` only gives `L.r ∣ dvResPoly … f …`. Relating the
   residual of `f` to the residual of its slope factor is `dvResPoly` multiplicativity, and
   the only landed cite (`fgmn_residual_mul`, C.66) demands BOTH factors already `IsDvPure`
   with `D′ ∣ natDegree` — it cannot produce the below-floor/other-slope factors' purity.
   This is `C33_BLOCKED_2026-08-17.md`'s missing mechanism and `C34_BLOCKED_2026-08-18.md`
   FRONTIER 2 obstruction 2, unchanged.
2. **Even granted `G`, the residual-primary split inside one slope is C.34 itself.**
   `L.r ∣ R` must be refined to `G = f_S · g'` with `dvResPoly f_S = L.r ^ m` — the
   `dv`-graded one-slope Hensel lift of `C34_BLOCKED_2026-08-18.md` FRONTIER 1. Not landed;
   its absence is the same missing engine (residual Bézout over `F.stageField` + Newton step
   + `IsAdicComplete` limit).
3. **Maximality (`∀ fS'`) needs both separations.** A labelled divisor `d ∣ f` must be shown
   to divide `f_S`: cross-slope (`d` is pure of slope `(L.u, L.ℓ)`, so `d ∣ G`) plus
   within-slope (`d ∣ f_S`). The first is the shape of the A-C.8 cite
   `fgmn_dvDissection_factor_eq`, but that cite quantifies over `DvDissection` structures of
   the SAME `f` and says nothing about an arbitrary divisor's factor placement; the second is
   C.34's uniqueness leg. A-C.8.2's scope fence states explicitly that the signed pair
   "imports no residual-primary dissection (C.34)".
4. **The divisibility rider `F.e₁ * F.f₁ ∣ fS.natDegree` is inseparable.** It is free once the
   block comes from the dissection (`DvDissection.hdeg` gives
   `deg factor = D′ · (ℓ · dvSideDeg)`), and it is NOT derivable from `HasLabel` — see
   `C35_CLAUSE3_DEFECT_2026-08-20.md`. So it cannot be split off as a separate cheap lemma;
   it travels with obstructions 1–2.

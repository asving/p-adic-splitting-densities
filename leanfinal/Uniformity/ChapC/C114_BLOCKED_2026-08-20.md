### 2.2 Mechanism — a missing lemma of theorem strength, not a repair

The corpus route is: (step 3) fiber the realizing stratum over the tree, node by node; (step 4)
telescope the resulting product.  **Step 4 is landed; step 3 is not, and nothing in the corpus
can state it.**

* `ht_rec` (C.112) supplies only the **division-free arithmetic telescope of the FORMULA**:
  `htBranchCount Q census t N * Q ^ (Σ_{u≠root} D_u(N)) = ∏_v (κ_v · ∏_S census · Q ^ B_v(N))`.
  It says nothing about the stratum.
* `ht_fiber_exponent_nonneg` (C.111) supplies only that telescope's **exponent nonnegativity**.
* `ht_node_cell_card` (C.109) supplies only the **single-node** exact cell count — which is
  exactly why the one-node stratum closes and nothing deeper does.

The missing statement, precisely (this is the honest open lemma):

> For a node index `i` of `t`, a bijection between the level-`N` classes of `htCell`-members at
> `i` realizing the subtree rooted at `i`, and the pairs (class at `i`, tuple of classes at the
> children of `i` realizing their subtrees), deflating each child by `Q ^ D_u(N)`.

Two structural obstructions, both in the carriers rather than in the proof:

1. **`HTTree` has no subtree operation and no `children`-indexed recursion principle.**  The
   tree is index-encoded (`nodes : List HTNode`, `parent : ℕ → ℕ`); there is no `subtreeAt`,
   no reindexing lemma, and no well-founded recursion on `t.children`.  So the induction the
   proof needs **cannot even be stated** over the landed carriers, let alone proved.
2. **`htRealizes` is a flat existential over `G K : ℕ → Polynomial O` with global clauses.**
   Restricting it to a subtree requires the reindexing of (1), plus a lemma that the restricted
   key/divisor tower is again a `htRealizes` witness.  Neither exists.

The blueprint's own PROOF line concedes the first half of this ("*under `hnode` the fleet must
still supply it*"), and A-C.3's finding **D6** proves the corpus's own derivation of the step-3
exponent is **FALSE at multi-child nodes** (`B_v − Σ_u D_u = −s(m_v² − Σ_u m_u²)/2 < 0` when the
children exhaust the node; measured truth vs formula on three such nodes: 243 vs 9, 486 vs 108,
128 vs 32).  So step 3 is not merely unformalized — its published derivation is wrong outside
`hnode`, and inside `hnode` the multi-child regime is **CERTIFIED-OPEN** (smallest instance at
`q = 3` needs `N ≥ 10` and a root cell of size `3^24`, out of brute-force range).

**Consequence for the DAG.** C.114 is not a transcription unit.  Before the signed names can
land, a new node is needed supplying the `HTTree` subtree/reindexing apparatus plus the step-3
fiber bijection under `hnode`.  Assigning further transcription fleets to C.114 as it stands
would be assigning agents to a blocked route.

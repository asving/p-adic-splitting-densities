## Obstruction inventory — `dvDissection_unique` (the separation frontier)

1. **The blueprint's tool does not fire.** The route names
   `Hensel.monic_factorization_unique` + "purity separation … C.20's class separation".
   `monic_factorization_unique` (`Uniformity/HenselFactorization.lean`) requires the two
   factorizations to reduce to the SAME **coprime** pair over `ResidueField O`. At
   `F.h ≥ 1` every `dv`-pure above-floor factor has its `j = 0` development block in
   `𝔪`-shifted position (`dvHgt > 0` forces only `v(a₀) > 0` per block), so distinct
   factors generically share the divisor `x` mod `𝔪` — the coprimality hypothesis is
   structurally unavailable at the residue field. The phrase "in the graded ring" would
   need a `dv`-graded analogue of the whole uniqueness argument; no such carrier exists
   (C.20 provides slot-CLASS arithmetic on stage heights — `stageHeight_class`,
   `stageHeight_unattained` — not polynomial-level coprime separation).
2. **Separation needs a cross-slope product law, landed nowhere.** Any argument that a
   common irreducible divisor cannot serve two distinct-slope pure factors must relate the
   polygon of a product to the polygons of its factors. At order 1 this is
   `suppVal_mul_gen` (B39a, ~700 lines, `IsKey`-fenced). At the `dv`-carrier NOTHING is
   landed: no `dvSupp` sub/super-additivity, no additivity ([FGMN] Thm 2.8), no
   "irreducible ⟹ one-sided `dv`-polygon" (the completeness-consuming classical step).
3. **Even the chapter's own cite does not cover it.** The signed clause of C.66
   (`fgmn_residual_mul`) is residual multiplicativity for two factors pure at the SAME
   slope `(u, ℓ)`. Cross-slope polygon additivity ([FGMN] Thm 2.8 proper) is cited in
   C.66's faithfulness header but is NOT part of the signed axiom. So the separation leg
   is out of reach even under the full four-cite allowlist — this block is not an artifact

## A-C.7 vacuity audit — PASS

The audit was run before proof work, statement by statement.

* `budgetFloor_le_iff`: `hj : j < μ₂` excludes the zero-`μ₂` stratum and genuinely controls the
  subtraction `μ₂ - j`; `F.he₁` and `T.he₂` make the divisor positive. There is no side/nonempty
  implication guard. The underflow branch remains meaningful and gives floor `0`.
* `dvAbove_boxMonomial`: there is no `Nonempty`, `≠ ⊤`, or one-sided implication guard. At `c = 0`
  both the monomial and its required lower bound are at `⊤`, so this is not a vacuous escape.
  `hh`, `hπ`, and the finite-residue-field instance remain scope hypotheses.
* `dev_pow_self`: `hd : 0 < φ.natDegree` forces a nonzero positive-degree monic key; neither
  `μ = 0` nor `j = 0` makes the claim vacuous (both branches were compile-checked).
* `composedKey_pow_mem_towerLocus`: `hμ₂ : 0 < μ₂` excludes the zero block. Membership itself
  contains `IsDvPure`; its `0 ∈ dvSideSet` conjunct supplies side nonemptiness, and `DvOnSide`'s
  second conjunct supplies a finite height pin.
* `mem_towerLocus_of_budget`: `hμ₂`, `F.he₁`, `F.hf₁`, `T.he₂`, and `T.hf₂` make all three budget
  ranges inhabited. `hf` and `hdeg` force the top composed digit; they are not implication-only
  guards. A coefficient with valuation `⊤` satisfies a real lower bound rather than erasing the
  remaining slots.
* `budget_of_mem_towerLocus`: the same three ranges are inhabited. `hmem` is not a one-sided empty
  side guard: unfolding the locus supplies purity, hence a nonempty side and a finite pin as above.

Thus none of the C.111/C.94/C.113/C.118 degenerate-guard patterns occurs here.

## Obstruction inventory

1. **The dv-graded product law is absent.** C.52 Step 0 needs the inequality half saying that
   `dvSupp`/all `dvHgt` slots of a product lie above the sum of the factor weights. Step 1 needs
   its exact/purity half for powers. A repository-wide search finds no `dvSupp` multiplication
   theorem, no `IsDvPure` multiplication theorem, and no `dvResPoly` power theorem.
   `C33_BLOCKED_2026-08-17.md` independently audits the same missing mechanism and records that
   the order-one `suppVal` engine (B.33/B.35/B.39) cannot be applied directly at the dv carrier.
2. **C.47 supplies only the test-key hood.** `composedKey_isTestKey` gives the monicity, degree,
   lattice digits, exact single-key heights, and reads needed for the base key. It does not export
   the required passage from `IsTestKey` to `IsDvPure`, nor closure under powers, nor residual
   multiplicativity.
3. **The adjacent allowed cite does not close Step 1.** C.66's `fgmn_residual_mul` assumes both
   input factors are already `IsDvPure`, assumes the product side and all finite pins, and concludes
   residual multiplication only up to an unspecified nonzero scalar. It therefore neither creates
   the power's purity nor yields the exact equation `dvResPoly = towerLabel ^ μ₂` required by
   `towerLocus`.
4. **The triangular dictionary named by the assignment is not landed.** There is no
   `leanfinal/Uniformity/ChapC/C53.lean`. Steps 2 and 3 require precisely its conversion between
   bounds on the two-key development coefficients and bounds on the original polynomial/dv
   carrier. C.128's `composedKey_trinomial` is a specialized pinned-parameter identity, not that
   general dictionary.
5. **C.118a/b congruence is one-way at the relevant frontier.** Its capped tools transport visible
   reads from coefficientwise congruence of polynomials. C.52 starts with congruence/bounds on
   nested `dev` coefficients; without C.53 triangularity there is no landed theorem that turns
   those hypotheses into the coefficientwise capped congruence C.118 requires.

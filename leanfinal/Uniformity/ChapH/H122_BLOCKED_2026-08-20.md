## 2. THE CARRIED BINDER IS INHABITED — `betaLeg_two`, and why it matters

```lean

    (Nat.card {c : ClusterState O 2 N // IsBetaState π c ∧ ClusterUndecided O π 2 N c} : ℝ)
      ≤ (1 / 3 : ℝ) * (N : ℝ) * (residueCard O : ℝ) ^ (2 * (N - 1))
          * ((residueCard O : ℝ) ^ (N - 1))⁻¹
```

is the `m = 2` instance of `hbeta`, proved.  Three further audit legs (full table in the
landing file's §0 header):

* **not trivially true.**  The trivial census bound is `#(β ∧ undec) ≤ Q^(m(N−1))`; `hbeta`'s
  right-hand side is strictly smaller at every `N ≥ 1`, `Q ≥ 2` (it is that bound times
  `(1/3)·N·Q^(−(N−1))`, and `(1/3)N < Q^(N−1)` for every `N ≥ 1`).  So the binder genuinely
  restricts.
* **load-bearing.**  It is used exactly ONCE (step 5), and the pre-A-H.6 FROZEN envelope
